## What changed

- A residual of the windows plans, the sum over t <= min(j, reach) of (W_t Q_(j-t))[s, 2s), is
  one `inverse_product` of up to 8 products (`kMaxWindows <= ntt::kMaxTerms`, asserted). Before,
  past 4 terms the first ones were leaf products in memory (`multiply`, `multiply_add` into a
  sum leaf form, a term alone) and the inverse took that sum and 3 products: one more array of
  2s words, and about 1.0 ns a word per extra term on Zen 3 against 0.76 now (the cost table).
- The cost model's `leaf` row (multiply_add) is gone: `product(lg, terms)` = product + (terms - 1)
  term. `term` is measured as a quarter of inverse_product of 5 products less of 1, on 10 leaf
  forms of their own (`tune.hpp`; it was half of 3 less 1, on 6). Shared leaf forms (an earlier
  try: 5 products on 6 forms) measured Zen 5's term at 2^19 as 0.42 ns a word, where its sums of
  distinct forms cost about 1.0; divmod/22 then picked 7 windows at 2^18 and ran 1.047x.

## The cost table

`tools/tune.py division` on every target's timing machine, then only `term` kept from it
(`merge_term.py`): the other rows, measured at 4ea3a2b, moved by work other pull requests
merged since (avx2 `forward` 0.86-0.96x), and with all of them new the model moved
divmod_long/11 and divmod_short/11 to blocks of 128 (tails), 1.054x and 1.021x on Zen 3, 1.088x
and 1.053x on Emerald Rapids, plans this change does not touch. `term` is scaled by main's
`product` / the new one (inverse_product of one product: code this round did not change) where
those differ by more than 2%: Emerald Rapids 0.927 (lc-intel ran every row 5-15% slower than at
4ea3a2b), scalar 0.940. Zen 5's `term` from 2^19 on stays main's: there the measurement moved
0.4x-1.7x between runs of the same code, and the new values sent divmod/22 to s = 2^20 with one
window, 1.058x in the bench (in `sweep.cpp`'s layout both plans ran within 1%).

Plans that change (`plans.cpp`): n_max_04 on every target to lc-opt's (s = 2^15, 7 blocks, 6
windows, the last block full, the split remainder; it was s = 2^16, 3 windows); max_random_02 to
s = 2^15, 3 windows on avx2, avx512 and zen5 (from 2^16 with 2, or tails at 2^17 on zen5); divmod
and the others unchanged.
