# #102 r1: lc-opt's division moved onto ntt::Transform, every target

2026-10-12, claude (issue #102, round 1, target all). New kernel `backend/division` (design:
`notes.md`): lc-opt's `lib/poly/division.hpp` (13e78b2, its issue #81) rewritten on the fused
Transform ops of #112 (PR #142) and `series::inverse`, with a cost model tuned per target. Base:
main f5ece5f (PR #142 merged). Exploration (same-run comparison with lc-opt, plan sweeps):
`~/Documents/hpc_library-explore/division/issue102/` (`compare_division.cpp`, `run_compare.sh`,
`ship.py`; lc-opt's `lib/` copied in `lcopt/`).

## What changed against lc-opt

1. Ops: lc-opt's cyclic products, inverses of sums and forwards from a span map onto
   `cyclic_product`, `inverse_product` and `forward(in, F)`. lc-opt's `cyclic_product(in, offset,
   ...)` placed the tail product's source at x^(Lt - 2d); here the transform of G is shifted
   instead (Gt = T(x^(Lt/2 - d) G)), so the residual's head is the upper half's first d words,
   negated by the op. lc-opt's sums of up to 8 terms in the bottom: here inverse_product takes 4,
   so past 4 the first ones are leaf products in memory (multiply, multiply_add).
2. Blocks from the top of Q with a short last block (c = k - (B-1)s words) instead of F padded in
   front (lc-opt's first block carried up to s - 1 zeros). The last block's product: at 2s, at a
   shorter length (its own transform of h mod x^c), by small::multiply, or (new) its residual by c
   dot products of g with Q and no transform at all (`Last::dot`): for k = 2^j + 1 (the divmod
   bench: f of 2^lg by g of 2^(lg-1)) the last block is one word, whose residual by windows cost
   an inverse_product of 4 terms and a forward of 2s; by one dot product of d terms it is about
   0.2 ns a term. divmod 0.93-0.95 of lc-opt -> 0.81-0.83 (Zen 3).
3. Cost model from a tuned table (`select.hpp`, "division.costs"): ns per word per length of
   forward, cyclic_product, inverse_product (1 term, each further term), multiply_add and
   series::inverse, and per-word costs of a pass and of long division; small products by
   `small::model_ns`. `tools/tune.py division` measured it on every target's timing machine
   (`tune.cpp`, up to 2^25). Model against measurement (`compare_division sweep`, Zen 3): every
   plan within 0.95-1.03 except one block (1.07-1.10); the chosen plan within 0.5% of the measured
   best on n_max_04 and n_max_02 (lc-opt's problem shapes).
4. Long division by Montgomery products with c 2^32 mod P (no Shoup quotient, which costs a 64-bit
   division per step).
