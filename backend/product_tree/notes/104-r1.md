# #104 r1: kept transforms (a Keep hook) for descents

2026-10-12, claude (issue #104, round 1, for backend/evaluation).

- `LaneTree<Leaves, Keep>` and `StandardTree<Keep>` take a Keep (default `NoKeep`):
  `keep.place(lo, mid, hi, words)` returns rooms (`Rooms{left, right}`, null for the tree's own
  memory) where the node builds its children's transforms of its length, so they outlive the
  build; `keep.root(words)` (StandardTree) a room for the root's product transform, written by
  the root's leaf products as their copy output. `degree`, `split` and a new
  `base(lo, hi)` are public, for descents that walk the same structure. `Stack::ever_spilled()`
  tells whether a take went past the span (for callers' size tests).
- product() runs NoKeep, and its code is kept as before: the tree still takes a and c from its
  stack where the original did (unused when a room replaces them) and the doubling uses them as
  before (c, never kept, is the longer extensions' buffer), so with NoKeep every operation and
  its order are the original's (objdump on the zen5 build: the trees' functions within 1-2
  instructions of main's, Stack::take one more for the spill flag). A first form (rooms
  replacing the takes, the doubling through an `upper` pointer) ran 1.0052 and 1.0048 gated
  geomean on `lc-zen5` (product_mixed/14 1.022) in two runs. Final, same-run A/B against main
  0e59c89, `kernel.py bench product_tree`, 11 rounds, gated geomean: `lc-bench` 0.9946,
  `lc-intel` 0.9986, `lc-zen5` 1.0016, `lc-arm` 0.9998.
- Tests: `test_keep.cpp` (new): a Keep that records every room, on lane and standard trees of
  random degrees; each kept transform equals the forward of the node's product computed apart;
  the root's transform; products unchanged.
