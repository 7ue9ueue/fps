## Kept

1. **Lane pairs (item 3 of the issue, and more).** The standard tree over the 8 lane products
   began with 8 forwards of length T = 2L (L the lane root's length; the lane products read
   every 8th word) and 4 nodes of leaf products at T. Now `LaneTree::root_doubled` also gives the
   lane root's transform at 16 L words: its product pass writes the lower half (pointwise2), and
   forward_upper from the coefficients (ntt's `forward_upper(in, H)`, the coefficients with word
   l less top for a moment) the upper. Its leaf v holds the 8 lanes at w_v for the 2L roots of
   X^(2L) - 1; the 8 roots of the standard leaf u of length T (x^8 - w_u) are its leaves 8u ..
   8u + 7, at rho_u z^bitrev3(i) (ntt's values form order; checked: w_(8u+i) = (-1)^i r(4u)
   r(i/2), and (w_(8u+i))^8 = w_u). `lane_pairs`: per step the 8 vectors of those leaves, lanes
   2j and 2j + 1 multiplied pointwise (a deinterleave and one Montgomery product per vector),
   an 8-point inverse with fixed twiddles (r(1)^-1, z^-1, r(3)^-1: 5 products), an 8 x 8
   transpose and the untwist rho_u^-k / 8 (one row of a table per leaf, prefix compatible across
   lengths). The 4 nodes over lanes 2j, 2j + 1 take these as their product transforms (`Pairs`):
   no forwards of the lane products, no leaf products at T. Per word of input on L1-resident
   data: Zen 3 0.34-0.43 ns, Emerald Rapids 0.34-0.37, Zen 5 0.30-0.32, Neoverse V2 0.64.
   - Memory: the first form held the doubled transform (16 L) and the 4 pairs (16 L, with
     copies) next to the standard tree: peak RSS of one product of 2^24 linear factors 813 ->
     969 MB on `lc-amd`. Now the pairs go where the root's left child was (8 L words, dead after
     the product pass; the right child's room keeps the coefficients), the pairs carry no copies
     (a pair node copies its product transform into out and its upper half, one pass with two
     stores), the doubled transform is released before the standard tree is built, and so are
     the coefficients when all 4 pairs are taken (`pair_nodes` finds them from the leaves'
     degrees before the tree exists; the pairs carry the leading coefficients). Peak RSS 813 ->
     779 MB (2^24), 208 -> 200 (2^22), 54 -> 53 (2^20), with item 4.
   - Alone, against main (3 rounds, the first form): gated geomean 0.977 (Zen 3), 0.982 (Emerald
     Rapids), 0.991 (Zen 5), 0.976 (Neoverse V2); product/18 0.965 / 0.987 / 0.988 / 0.970.
2. **The driver: identity order.** When every factor is small, of one degree and none constant
   (x - r), the counting sort is the identity: no placement pass, no order array (`Slots` with a
   null order reads fs[8k + l]). Same-run A/B (sed on a copy, 5 rounds, product/ and
   product_mixed/ cases): 0.983 (Zen 3), 0.951 (Emerald Rapids), 0.968 (Zen 5), 0.963 (Neoverse
   V2); product/8 0.96 / 0.87 / 0.92 / 0.90; mixed unchanged.
3. **The driver: a first run of one size.** The first pass counts a first run of factors of one
   size by a loop of its own (no histogram increment, no extremes per factor). A/B (7 rounds,
   same cases): 0.970 (Zen 3), 0.950 (Emerald Rapids), 0.952 (Zen 5), 0.965 (Neoverse V2);
   product/18 0.971 / 0.953 / 0.943 / 0.974; mixed 0.99-1.00. More than the histogram's chain
   alone (item 2 of Not kept): the whole loop body per factor.
4. **One table per thread (item 5).** ntt's `Transform(lg, tables)` (ntt notes/91-r2.md): this
   thread's transforms all on the tables of the longest reserved (`reserve_transforms`, called
   by the driver with the longest length it will use: the standard root's, or 16 L), moved in
   place when a longer one is reserved. About 1.25 bytes per word of the longest instead of 2.5
   (2^25 coefficients: about 32 MB of tables per thread, was about 64). Speed alone (A/B, 5
   rounds): 1.001 (Zen 3), 1.003 (Emerald Rapids, noisy), 0.996 (Zen 5), 0.997 (Neoverse V2).
   The first form held three pointers in Transform; GCC then inlined differently across ntt
   (`Ntt<Avx512Vl>::forward` 753 instructions against 673), and Zen 5's `forward_values` ran
   2-6% slower at 2^12-2^23 (twice, 7 rounds), `forward/24`, `/25` 1.02-1.03. A padded
   Transform of main's code (object size alone) ran as main (0.999). The kept form keeps a line
   pointer and the tables' length, so the accessors compute as main's: the same function sizes
   in ntt's benches (objdump), Zen 5 ntt geomean 1.001.
5. **Slots of one degree d > 1** (`load_full`): each factor's words as vectors, the last one by
   a masked load (AVX2 vpmaskmovd, AVX-512 vmovdqu32 {k}) or lane loads (Neon): no word past a
   factor is read (test_slots.cpp puts each factor at the end of a page before an unreadable
   one); then transposed in registers and taken to Montgomery form, instead of words stored one
   by one and reloaded by the conversion pass. product_mixed 0.99-1.00 (Zen 5 mixed/20 0.956).
   For linear slots on Zen 3 (no `load_linear` there) it was slower (product/20 1.064), so linear
   slots keep the generic path there. Re-measured with the identity order: `load_linear` on Zen
   3 is still 0.7% slower than the generic path (0.993), so `kLinearSlots` stays off on avx2.
6. The lane root's upper half by forward_upper from the coefficients instead of a copy and an
   in-place forward_upper: 0.993 (Zen 3), 0.991 (Emerald Rapids), 0.997 (Zen 5), 0.999
   (Neoverse V2).

## Not kept

1. Leaf products with two leaves per zmm (`leaf_products_wide`, ntt's WideBottom's layout:
   windows by load_halves, b by broadcast_halves, two pairs per step): alone 0.95x the time of
   `leaf_products<Avx512Vl>` on Zen 5 (0.47-0.51 against 0.52-0.55 ns per word), 1.08x on
   Emerald Rapids. In the trees after item 1 two levels of leaf products are left for lanes
   inputs (0.4% at 2^18 by arithmetic). The zmm form gains 0.65x in ntt over ntt's own Bottom,
   which is slower than product_tree's kernel (0.60 against 0.52 ns per word); both are at about
   3 loads per word. Code in the exploration folder (`tried/`).
2. The histogram in four copies (i % 4: no chain of increments through one counter): 1.007
   (Zen 3), 0.995 (Emerald Rapids), 1.015 (Zen 5), 0.995 (Neoverse V2). Item 3 instead.
3. The driver without branches on constants (their words to a buffer, multiplied 64 at a time
   in 4 chains; constants placed after the small factors, d = 0): 1.010 (Zen 3; mixed 1.02),
   1.003 (Emerald Rapids), 1.007 (Zen 5), 1.002 (Neoverse V2). The branches are cheaper than the
   work that replaces them.
4. The standard tree in ntt's values form (pointwise products instead of leaf products; the
   split in forward_upper(h, Form::values), the join in the inverse), timed per node (products,
   inverse of the copy, forward_upper of 2L; explore_values.cpp): Zen 5 0.89-0.97 of the leaf
   form's time per node, Neoverse V2 0.96-0.99, Emerald Rapids 1.05-1.35 (slower); and the
   leaves' forwards in the values form cost 1.3-1.9x the leaf form's (Zen 5 0.40-0.68 against
   0.21-0.49 ns per word), more than the nodes save for the standard tree's own leaves. Not built.

## Leads

- backend/evaluation's point tree builds the same top tree (lane products by transposes, then
  their forwards and the pairs' leaf products) and keeps each node's children's transforms: the
  lanes' transform at twice the root's length gives both the lane leaves' transforms at T (the
  8-point inverse and untwist per lane, no products) and the pair nodes' (evidence on #136).
- `lane_pairs` with lazy reductions (values < 2P through the 8-point inverse) and Shoup products
  for the constants on Neon (3 instructions against 5): the kernel is 1-2% of product/18.
- Zen 3 is slower with `load_linear` and `load_full` for linear slots in the tree, faster alone
  (r1, this round): not understood.
