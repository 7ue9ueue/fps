# #136 r2: two hooks for backend/evaluation's top tree (the lane root's transform, lane_pairs' sums)

2026-10-18, claude (issue #136, round 2: backend/evaluation, its log
`backend/evaluation/notes/136-r2.md`). Base main ce94baa. GCC 16.2 (the pinned image).

- `LaneTree::root()` writes the root's product transform (the lanes at the roots of X^L - 1, 8 L
  words, before the inverse) to `keep.root(8 L)` when the Keep returns a room: the product pass
  becomes `pointwise2` into the coefficients' array and the room. NoKeep returns null: the same
  `pointwise` as before. evaluation's point tree takes it as the lower half of the lanes'
  transform at twice the root's length (its top tree's leaves), as `root_doubled` builds it for
  the driver's pairs; `root_doubled` itself takes no Keep, and the point tree needs its rooms.
- `lane_pairs_by<L>(lanes, T, out, untwist, pair, copy)`: lane_pairs with its pointwise step a
  functor (pair(a, b): the vector of lanes 2j, 2j + 1 combined, from word offsets of the two
  vectors deinterleave() splits); evaluation's `lane_pair_sums` passes the cross sums R_2j Q_2j+1
  + R_2j+1 Q_2j of interpolation's ascent. `lane_pairs` passes the tag `LanePairProducts`, which
  keeps the products inline as before: with them as a functor GCC scheduled the loop 5% slower on
  Zen 3 (`lane_pairs` alone, A/B against main, T = 2^6 .. 2^16: 1.047-1.060; inline 0.968-0.981).
  Inline against main: Zen 3 0.977, Emerald Rapids 0.978 (noisy), Zen 5 0.997, Neoverse V2 1.011
  (geomeans over T; scripts/lp.cpp in the exploration folder). An optional copy output (the same
  words to copy[j], a compile-time no-op when absent) lets evaluation's pairs double in their rooms
  with no copy pass.
- product_tree's own calls are unchanged in what they compute: `test_pairs.cpp` passes as before;
  `test_root_copy.cpp` (new) checks the root's transform through the hook against the forward of
  the root's lanes mod X^L - 1, the coefficients as without it, and lane_pairs' copies. Timing:
  `kernel.py bench product_tree --base ce94baa` in evaluation's log (Numbers).
- Seen: `product()` of more than 2^24 small factors throws (#253): the driver's `root_doubled` at
  16 L = 2^26 words passes ntt's 2^25, and `untwist_factors` would read `kRoots[1][21]`, one past
  the table, at T = 2^23. evaluation's tree runs those transforms twisted at that size and takes
  its roots from its own table (`kTopRoots`).
