# #136 r3: leaf products by target, and two products of one window (for backend/evaluation)

2026-10-18, claude (issue #136 round 3, for backend/evaluation; its notes/136-r3.md has the rest).
Base main d708598 (explorations), 9fd0030 (final runs). GCC 16.2, each target's flags.

`leaf_products` is now `leaf_products_k<L, K>` (K products of one a, one window per leaf for all
of them; `leaf_products` K = 1, `leaf_products_twice` K = 2, which evaluation's descent uses), with
three changes to the kernel every caller runs:

- Rows of 16 words (one 64-byte line), not 24: the x86 loads of x^i A mod (x^8 - w) at words 8 - i
  .. 15 - i and their odd words one on stay in the line but for i = 0's odd words (24-word rows put
  every other row's loads across a line). Zen 3 0.97-0.98, Emerald Rapids 0.95-0.96, Zen 5 0.99,
  Neoverse V2 1.00 of the time (kernel alone, 2^12-2^20).
- Neon: b_i an element of b's two vectors (`vmlal_laneq_u32`, `vmlal_high_laneq_u32`: no
  broadcast loads), two leaves per step (8 sums of 64 bits; four leaves 1.33 ns per word, slower).
  Neoverse V2 1.47 -> 1.25 ns per word at every size (0.84x); ntt's `Transform::multiply` is 1.33.
- Zen 4/5 (`kWideLeaves`, select.hpp): two leaves per zmm, windows by `load_halves` of two rows and
  b_i by `broadcast_halves`, as ntt's WideBottom; the odd words by loads (by a shift: 2-4% slower).
  Zen 5 0.52 -> 0.41-0.43 ns per word (0.80-0.83x) to 2^20, where ntt's multiply was 0.50. Emerald
  Rapids 0.78-0.82 against 0.66-0.69 for the rows: not there.
- Two products of one a: Zen 3 0.90, Emerald Rapids 0.82-0.85, Zen 5 0.64 (zmm), Neoverse V2 0.78
  of the time per product of one call each.

Rows are not zeroed whole any more (1.5 KB per call): the row read one word past by the x86
odd-word loads is (its lane is unused; zeroed for memory checkers).

Tests: `test_leaves.cpp`, every simd struct the machine runs (Scalar, Lanes, Sse, Avx2, Avx512Vl
with the zmm path on Zen 5, Neon) against a schoolbook product mod x^8 - w with random weights,
counts 1 .. 40 (short batches, odd counts for the pairs' tails) and powers of 4 to 4096, random,
P - 1 and zero inputs; outputs into a and into b, the copy, guards past the leaves; both products
of `leaf_products_twice`, the first into a. A mutation of a lane index in the Neon terms failed
1328 checks.

Bench: the final runs (`kernel.py bench product_tree --base 9fd0030`) are in evaluation's
notes/136-r3.md (Numbers).
