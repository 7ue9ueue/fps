# #106 r1: move lc-opt's chirp, geometric evaluation and interpolation, extrapolate

2026-10-12, claude. Base: main at 6856d40. New kernel `backend/chirp` (design: `notes.md`).

## What moved, and how

- `chirp.hpp`: lc-opt's `detail::Chirp` as `Terms<S, K>` on `backend/simd` (any struct, any K),
  `chirp`, `multiply_chirp` (out of place too). Tails through `S::store_first`, no buffer copy out.
- `geometric.hpp`: `evaluate_geometric` (one `ntt::middle_product`, no reversal: the identity
  t(i - j) = t(i) + t(-j) - i j puts the sum in middle-product form directly, unlike lc-opt's
  reversed kernel), `interpolate_geometric` (Newton's form, two `ntt::multiply` mod x^n; Q, 1/Q and
  the chirps r^t folded into one product sequence A_k = prod (r - r^-s) and its inverse B).
- `shift.hpp`: `extrapolate` (lc-opt's shift_of_sampling_points: Lagrange, one middle product per
  run of points outside the samples, samples copied).
- `scan.hpp`: prefix products and their inverses, in lanes (below).
- No change outside `backend/chirp/`. lc-opt's palindrome trick (half of the kernel's forward
  transform) needs ntt internals; not moved (below, next steps).

### Attempts

- Terms per step, K (one binary, `chirp@k<K>/<lg>`, ns): avx2 2^10: K = 1 554, 2 413, 4 415, 8 582;
  2^20: 509842, 297762, 238978, 289672. avx512 2^20: 453209, 253534, 191132, 221201. zen5 2^20:
  296958, 153816, 93976, 97548; 2^10 K = 2 best on all three (269-413), K = 4 best from 2^12 on.
  Kept: K = 2 below 2^11 terms, 4 from there (Terms' scalar setup grows with K: 2 W products).
  An earlier binary (fewer bench cases) ranked K = 8 first at 2^16 and 2^20 on all three
  (avx2 2^20: K = 4 303599, K = 8 250151): the same source moved 20% between binaries, so the
  ranking of 4 against 8 is within code-layout noise (as in issue #93). lc-opt's 6.3 cycles per
  vector on Zen 3 matches K = 4 here (0.228 ns per term, about 7 cycles per vector).
- Prefix products (`products@...`, 2^20 geometric factors, prefix and inverse): scalar chains
  (8 interleaved, u64 % P) 6.03 ms avx2, 3.86 avx512, 3.43 zen5; lanes with an in-register
  transpose every S::lanes steps: K = 1 1.55, 1.35, 0.72; K = 2 1.39, 1.05, 0.71; K = 4 1.71,
  1.25, 0.87. Kept: lanes, K = 2, from 1024 factors: 4.3x, 3.7x, 4.8x the scalar chains.
  interpolate_geometric/20 runs 2^20 factors (1.4 ms of its 22.4 on avx2; scalar chains would add
  4.6 ms), extrapolate/20 2^21 (about 2.8 of 15.3 ms).
- Interpolation's two products through `ntt::Transform` in the values form, with B's transform
  shared (5 transforms, not 6): slower everywhere, 1.03-1.14x at 2^16-2^22 (avx2 /20 23.70 against
  22.50 ms, avx512 27.12 against 23.80, zen5 11.89 against 10.30), 1.4-3x below 2^12 (each
  separate pass over the array costs more than the fused depth-first `multiply`). Not kept.
- Horner below n m = 4096 at first: evaluate_geometric/6 (64 x 64) took 14.0 us on avx2 (a
  dependent u64 % P chain, 3.4 ns per term), 0.88 us through the middle product. Cutoff now
  n m <= 256.
- GCC's ASan build for avx512 crashed (SEGV in `_mm512_loadu_si512`, inside Terms' constructor)
  while Terms kept `__m512i` members; -O2 passed. Terms now holds words and passes load them into
  locals: all six targets pass under ASan/UBSan.

## Checks

`tools/kernel.py test chirp` on every target (scalar, sse, avx2 on `lc-amd`; avx512 on
`lc-intel-test`; zen5 on `lc-zen5-test`; arm on `lc-arm` and the Mac): -O2 and ASan/UBSan pass.

## Next steps

- Evaluation: lc-opt chose the kernel's offset so that it is a palindrome over the transform
  length, and ran its forward transform for half of the nodes; that needs an ntt hook (a forward
  of a palindromic factor, or a middle product taking a stored transform).
- Extrapolate: h_t = Pre_t / Pre_(t+1) and W come from two more passes over the products; they
  could be fused into the lanes' downward pass.
- interpolate_geometric shares nothing between its two products; a Transform-level product that
  is as fast as `multiply` (issue #112's one-pass cyclic products) would let B's transform be
  reused.
