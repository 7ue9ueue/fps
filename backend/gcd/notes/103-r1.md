## Steps

1. The port on the leaf form (commit 416b543): lc-opt's ops map onto `forward(in, F)`,
   `forward_upper(in, H)`, `inverse_product` (2 terms) and `multiply`/`multiply_add`;
   `combine3` (Euclid's step) on `simd::Native` (x86: 64-bit sums and one Montgomery reduction,
   as lc-opt; Neon: `umull`/`umlal`; else Shoup products). Never `inverse(F, out)`: it takes the
   thread's NTT region, which the kernel holds (each call would map a fresh region). Same run
   against lc-opt on Zen 3: 1.06-1.08 of its time (inv/10 to inv/18). The combine was 3 passes
   per entry (multiply, multiply_add, inverse) against lc-opt's one pass of 2 products sharing
   the windows; a guess at the gap.
2. Values form (`ntt::Form::values`) for every transform: a product is one Montgomery product per
   word for a split per forward and a join per inverse. Doubling needed the upper half in the
   values form: `Transform::forward_upper(h, Form::values)` (`backend/ntt/notes/103-r1.md`).
   Matrix transforms kept times 2^32 (one reduction per product, a second for the combine's
   inverse); sums of 2 products in 64-bit sums, one reduction. Against the leaf form, same run,
   `kernel.py bench gcd --base` (3 rounds, new/base):

   (gcd / inverse_mod; geomean 0.919, 1.003, 0.918, 0.944.) Kept on every target: one code path;
   Emerald Rapids loses 4-8% at 2^8-2^10 and gains 2-4% from 2^14 (its leaf products are cheap,
   0.78 ns per word, and its split and join dear). A per-target or per-length choice of the form
   is left for later (issue below).
3. gcd: the spine without the matrix, Euclid's tail by fraction-free steps on whole remainders
   (first version: monic remainders with an inversion per step, 8.3 us at n = 64 against 1.8).
   Divisions with quotients of at most 32 words by long division (`division::divide` plans by a
   search each call; the bench's f of n words by g of n has a one-word quotient).
4. Euclid's step factors from alpha, beta, gamma with 4 products (lc-opt's), and the second
   reduction of the combine's products in one pass: equal (0.9995 geomean, Zen 3).
5. The stack bound: 56 N words first; random inputs used 31-34 N from 2^12 on (`peak.cpp`:
   binary search on the scratch size), now 48 N (lc-opt's) plus the arrays' gaps per level. The
   tests run every structure (common factors, x^a - 1 pairs: long quotients) in the caller's
   scratch of exactly `gcd_scratch` / `inverse_mod_scratch` words.

## Profile (`lc-intel`, `-march=x86-64-v3`, inverse_mod at 2^14, 3.39 ms, perf, commit 8a24d37)

Euclid 23.9%, values split 15.1% and join 11.9%, the transforms' levels 27.4% (forward 7.1,
forward_leaves 6.6, inverse 4.6, inverse_leaves 3.6, inverse_top 2.8, inverse_top4 2.7),
pointwise products 8.1%, copies, folds and scaling into the transforms 5.6%. Euclid: about 1
cycle per word of each step's 3-product combination (the remainders over 2k - 2 progress words,
the two rows over progress words each), 64 steps per leaf; split and join together cost more
than the transforms' butterflies (#98, item 2).

## Not done, for later rounds

- Euclid's base (24%): two steps per pass (5 products for the second remainder from c and d, one
  load of each), or a leaf below 2^6 without transforms.
- Emerald Rapids: the leaf form at short lengths (a per-target or per-length form; switching
  needs the split of a kept lower half), or cheaper split and join (#98).
- The combine's inverses: 4 per node, 2 when one row is needed (inverse_mod's top).

Tests (`test.cpp`): every pair of sizes up to 40 (random and sparse words, zero tops) with Euclid's
cutoff 1, 3 and 64; common factors of degree 0-33, k/2 and k - 1 for 21 sizes up to 1000, cutoffs
1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 64; sizes 2^k - 1 .. 2^k + 1 to 2^12 with gaps 0, 1, 2, 37; x^a - 1 and x^b - 1 up
to 65536 (gcd x^gcd(a,b) - 1) and sparse random pairs; large known common factors up to 2^18 (by
ntt::multiply), inverse_mod checked by f h mod g = 1 (division::divide); this thread's region and
the caller's scratch (garbage-filled, exactly the bound), guard words around out and h; argument
checks. -O2 and ASan/UBSan on `lc-amd` (scalar, sse, avx2), `lc-intel-test`, `lc-zen5-test`,
