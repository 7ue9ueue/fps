`coefficient(p, q, k)` = [x^k] p / q, for `Fraction::operator[]` (the frontend still runs its own

Exploration files: `~/Documents/hpc_library-explore/bostan_mori/issue114/` (`compare.cpp`:

Machines and flags: `lc-bench` (EPYC 7B13, avx2), `lc-intel` (Xeon 8581C, avx512), `lc-zen5`

flags, medians of 3-5 rounds. Times below are us unless marked.

## 1. Values-form steps (kept)

The issue's guesses, measured as one design: one forward each of p and q (no transform of
q(-x)), U and V from the values at w and -w (rows 2m, 2m + 1 of a values-form block) straight
into the length-N layout (no inverse of 2N: the pass is the "half-length inverse"), doubling
across steps (inverse of N into the upper half, `forward_upper(h, values)`), no allocation per
call. The layout claim (w^2 of row pair m of leaves 2u, 2u + 1 lands in row m, m + 4 of leaf u
at N) is tested: the pass against the values form of U and V computed from coefficients
(`test.cpp`, every struct, 2^7 .. 2^13). First version against the frontend, same run:
d = 10^5: Zen 3 96.7 -> 38.3 ms, Emerald Rapids 89.6 -> 40.3, Neoverse V2 139.3 -> 60.2;
d = 1024: 832 -> 501, 724 -> 540, 1213 -> 792.

A step at 2^17 on Zen 3 is 832 us: pass 76, two inverses into other memory 364, two
forward_upper 393 (values form). The four transforms are the floor of this scheme (inverse and a
twisted forward of N per polynomial; a radix-4 step needs four of N per polynomial per two bits:
the same). The values form's join and split are 23% of the step on Zen 3 and 63% at 2^11 on Zen 5
(notes.md, Measurements).

## 2. Schoolbook steps for small d

- First version: per output block of W words a `sums()` over two terms (qe with pe, -qo with x po,
  ...), the fold test in the term loop, U and V in separate blocks, then a scalar split into the
  halves and `std::fill`s. Zen 3 avx2: d = 2 1.85 us, 16 3.90, 32 7.93, 64 20.0; the scalar
  struct 4-6x slower than avx2 (per-term overhead).
- Second (kept): U and V of a block in one loop (the broadcast of qe[a] or -qo[a] feeds both),
  folds every 17 terms outside the inner loop, the halves by `unzip` of two output blocks, zeros
  by vector stores where the sizes shrink. M4 (Neon): d = 16 3.55 -> 2.83 us, 48 17.4 -> 13.0, 64
  28.4 -> 22.4; scalar struct d = 64 268 -> 50 us. Zen 3: d = 16 3.72, 64 19.8 us.
- Struct per target: on `lc-intel` and `lc-zen5` Avx2 and Avx512Vl tie (within 1%), Sse is
  1.3-1.6x slower from d = 16; Avx512Vl kept on AVX-512 (as for the pass).
- Fills by vector stores instead of `std::fill` (two memsets per step): no change on M4 at d = 2
  (976 -> 958 ns).

## 3. Tiny scalar path (kept for at most 4 words)

Sums by the definition in scalar code, one `% P` per output word: Zen 3 d = 2 1.18 us against
1.68 (schoolbook avx2), d = 4 2.05 against 1.78, d = 8 4.51 against 2.19; M4 d = 2 0.72 against
0.95 us, d = 4 1.19 against 1.14. Used for p and q of at most 4 words.

## 4. Cutoff schoolbook / values (kept, select.hpp)

values is flat per N (Zen 3: 19.3 us for d < 64, 36.5 below 128, 65.6 below 256); schoolbook
grows as d^2. Crossovers (`small.cpp`, first schoolbook version): Zen 3 between 80 (28.0 against
36.5) and 96 (37.7 against 36.5); Emerald Rapids 96 (40.7 against 40.0); Zen 5 between 80 (21.3
against 24.0) and 96 (28.9); Neoverse V2 between 64 (36.2 against 49.2) and 80 (54.0). Set:
`kSchoolbookMax` 96 (avx2, avx512), 88 (zen5), 76 (arm), 64 (sse, scalar: not timed). The values
path hands off to schoolbook once the cut brings p and q under the same size.

## 5. Smaller things

- The scale 2^-h from a constexpr table (was a modular power per call).
- The w^-1 table built with Shoup products by constants (was `% P` per entry: about 0.6 ms at
  2^17 on the first call per thread).

- Doubling order: inverse and forward_upper of p, then of q (p's upper half still in cache),
  against both inverses then both forward_upper: 0.998-1.014 at d = 1024, 16384, 10^5 on the four
  machines (4 alternating rounds; 1.012 at 10^5 on Emerald Rapids). Kept both inverses first.

## 6. Not done: a leaf-form path (estimate only)

The pass on leaf pairs instead of values: U's even (or odd) words of p q(-x) mod x^8 - c (q(-x)'s
leaf is q's with odd words negated: x^8 - c is even), V's even words of q q(-x), 32 products each
per leaf, then the CRT of leaves 2u (x^8 - s) and 2u + 1 (x^8 + s) into leaf u at N: (A + B) and
(A - B) / s. Then no conversions: leaf-form inverses and forward_upper. Counted against
`leaf_product` (64 products per leaf): about 1.0-1.3 of its time per input word, so per step at
2^11 Zen 5 5.8 -> 4.5-5.3 us, Emerald Rapids 10.2 -> 7.9-8.9, Zen 3 9.5 -> 8.9-10.1; at 2^17
1-8% faster except Zen 3. Left for a later round; faster values conversions (#98) would help this
kernel more, and gcd and `fps::Values` too.
