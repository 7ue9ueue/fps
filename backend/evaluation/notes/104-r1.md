# #104 r1: the move of lc-opt's multipoint evaluation, interpolation and Newton basis

2026-10-12, claude (issue #104, round 1). Base main 0e59c89. GCC 15.2 (pinned image), each
target's flags; same-run ratios new/base. Explorations (lc-opt comparison harness `lcopt_bench.cpp`
and `lcopt_run.sh`, phases, crossover grid, A/B script, the transform-domain attempt as a diff) in
`~/Documents/hpc_library-explore/evaluation/`.

- The kernel (Design in notes.md): point tree on product_tree's LaneTree and StandardTree with a
  Keep (product_tree notes/104-r1.md), the transposed descent with halving by ntt's new
  `Transform::inverse_upper` (ntt notes/104-r1.md), Karp and Markstein's root division,
  schoolbook bases in lanes, the interpolation's fused pass, the Newton basis' descent; Horner's
  rule and O(m^2) loops below the trees.
- First version (all of it; descent and ascent as separate passes, lanes from scalar
  transposes): `lc-bench`, n = m = 2^17, ms, against lc-opt (`~/lc-opt`, its Arena reused across
  calls) in the same run: evaluate 11.67 / 11.02, interpolate 17.21 / 15.88, newton 10.93 / 9.99.
  Phases (evaluate, `lc-bench`): tree 3.83, division 2.50, descent 5.02 (lc-opt's notes: trees
  3.81, division ~2.3, descent 5.3). perf on `lc-intel`: transforms about 55% of the cycles,
  product_tree's leaf products 10%, pointwise 7%, ntt's leaf products (division) 5%,
  multiply_lanes 4%, middle_lanes 3%.
- Kept:
  - The top tree's root product transform kept for the division (one forward fewer; the root's
    leaf products write it as their copy output), the 8 top leaves' windows to the lanes by
    vector transposes (were scalar Montgomery products per word), and the interpolation's descent
    and sums in one depth-first pass with one inversion per base: against the first version,
    `lc-bench` 7 rounds: evaluate/14 0.951, /17 0.946; interpolate/14 0.951, /17 0.947; newton/14
    0.935, /17 0.938. (On the Mac the fused pass alone was neutral: 9.36 ms against 9.13 for the
    two passes.)
  - Horner's rule with 8 chains on 32-register targets (4 elsewhere) and lazy reduction (acc <
    2P): M4 2^10 x 2^10 370 -> 169 us. The choice between Horner and the tree by a cost model
    fitted per target on a grid of n, m in 8 .. 16384 (`crossover.cpp`): Horner ns per product
    0.21 (Zen 3), 0.105 (Emerald Rapids), 0.063 (Zen 5), 0.227 (Neoverse V2); the tree about
    fixed + a m8 lg m8 + b K lg K. lc-opt's rule (n m <= 2^22) ran Horner where the tree is 4x
    faster (2^10 x 2^10: Horner 217 us, tree 51 us on Zen 3). Crossovers at n = m: 256-512.
  - The Newton basis' slots padded to the power of two above past 0.65 of it (lc-opt's rule):
    `lc-bench`, 3 rounds, ms new / old: 126000 10.31 / 10.79 (0.956), 101656 10.29 / 10.56
    (0.974), 94808 0.984, 85000 1.000, 80000 (not padded) 0.997, 65537 0.991, 131072 0.999.
- Tried, not kept:
  - The top leaves to the lanes in the transform domain (`standard_to_lanes`: a leaf's state
    halved to its standard transform of the lane length; per standard leaf p three radix-2 levels
    to the lanes' leaves 8p .. 8p + 7, twiddles r(p), r(2p + e), r(4p + f) from the leaf
    weights; tested exact against the lanes forward). Against the version without it, 7-11
    rounds: Zen 3 0.996 (geomean of the six cases), Emerald Rapids 1.005 and 1.012, Zen 5 1.007.
    It saves a transform of m8 words in all (8 inverses of 2N and a forward of 8N against 8
    inverse_upper and forwards of N), less than the passes it adds on AVX-512. Diff in the
    explore folder (`attempts/standard_to_lanes.diff`).
- Final (commit 9323462): notes.md, Measurements (ns per point on the four timing machines, and
  against lc-opt in the same run on `lc-bench`: evaluate 0.97-1.01 from 2^12, interpolate
  1.02-1.03, newton 1.00-1.03). Tests pass on every target's test machine (-O2 and ASan/UBSan),
  with the workspaces at their computed sizes (no spill to the heap).
