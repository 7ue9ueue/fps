## Kept

1. **The top tree's leaves in the lanes** (#91 r2's lead, evidence on #136). The top tree over
   the 8 lane products (pairs of length T = 2L, halves of 2T, the root of 4T = 8L; L the lane
   root's length) kept its leaves as 8 standard transforms at T (8 forwards of T from the lane
   products as rows), built the pairs by leaf products, and its descents ended at the leaves with
   a leaf product, an inverse of T and a scaled row per leaf, then rows_to_lanes and a lanes
   forward of 8L; the interpolation's sums began with 8 forwards of T. Now (`PointTree`, tree.hpp,
   a fixed three-level top in place of product_tree's StandardTree):
   - Build: the lane root's transform at T (`doubled_`, 16 L words: the root's product pass writes
     the lower half through a new `keep.root(8 L)` hook in `LaneTree::root()`, forward_upper of its
     coefficients the upper) is the leaves' kept transform. The pairs' product transforms come
     from it by product_tree's `lane_pairs` (now with a copy output, into the room's halves where
     the doubling runs); the halves and the root as before.
   - Descent (evaluate, interpolate): the 4 pairs' states (standard leaf forms at T) to the 8
     leaves' products in the lanes in one pass (`pairs_to_lanes`: per standard leaf the twist by
     rho_u^k, an 8-point transform, the products with the siblings' lanes: lane_pairs run
     forward), then one halving of the lanes at T (inverse_upper of 16 L words and a forward of
     8 L): the lane root's state, factor 8. The 8 leaf products, 8 inverses of T, scales,
     rows_to_lanes and the lanes forward are gone.
   - The Newton basis: the same with odd lanes (right leaves) taking the pair's values alone
     (`pairs_to_lanes<L, true>`: one product per pair of leaves, not two) and, when C = L, one
     halving with a sign per lane (`combine_alternating`: left leaves the upper half, right ones
     the lower); the rightmost path's known windows are scaled by 2 per level so every pair has
     factor 4. When C < L (the right leaves' windows do not halve) the lanes' inverse of 16 L and
     each lane's window (`lane_windows`) then a forward of 8 L.
   - Interpolation's sums: the lanes' at T (the lane root's sums doubled as any node's), the pairs'
     by `lane_pair_sums` (lane_pairs with R_2j Q_2j+1 + R_2j+1 Q_2j for the products, product_tree's
     `lane_pairs_by`), then the halves and the root as before. The 8 forwards of T are gone.
   - At m8 = 2^25 (interpolate and the Newton basis allow it) the lanes' transforms of 16 L words
     pass ntt's 2^25: there they run twisted (`lanes_forward_upper`, `lanes_inverse_upper`,
     `lanes_inverse`: coefficient j of each lane times zeta^j, zeta of order 2L, and a transform of
     8 L), and the twist tables take their roots from `kTopRoots` (to 2^23; ntt's `kRoots` stop at
     2^22). Tests run them at small sizes (`twisted_from`).
   - Kernel costs at m8 = 2^17, us (`top.cpp`, Zen 3 / Emerald Rapids / Zen 5 / Neoverse V2):
     pairs_to_lanes 108 / 105 / 90 / 186 against 8 leaf products of T 218 / 176 / 134 / 386; the
     lanes' inverse_upper of 16 L (with a copy) 177 / 174 / 74 / 256 against 8 inverses of T 239 /
     210 / 96 / 352; lane_pairs 89 / 86 / 79 / 162 against 8 forwards of T 230 / 200 / 90 / 336.
   - Alone, against main (`phases.cpp`, A/B on each timing machine, 5 rounds at 2^17, 2-3 at
     2^20), new/base: see Numbers.
2. **A lane node's two children's products in one pass** (`pointwise_twice`): the descents
   computed child 0's product, ran its subtree, then child 1's product, reading the state twice
   (the second time after the subtree had pushed it out of the caches). Now both at once, the
   left one in place of the state. perf on `lc-intel` at 2^17 (evaluate, after item 1):
   `pointwise<Avx512>` was 8.4% of the cycles. Alone (5 rounds at 2^17, against item 1), descent
   0.981 / 0.972 / 0.968 / 0.971 (Zen 3 / Emerald Rapids / Zen 5 / Neoverse V2), evaluate_tree
   0.989 / 0.980 / 0.983 / 0.987, interpolate_tree 0.994 / 0.991 / 0.988 / 0.987, the Newton
   basis 1.000 / 0.997 / 0.996 / 0.999 (one product per node: nothing to share).

## Tried, not kept

- **The top tree's root and halves in ntt's values form** (pointwise products instead of leaf
  products; the split in the halvings' forwards, a join before each inverse_upper). Not built: by
  the kernels' costs at 2^17 (`top.cpp`), per level of the descent the two leaf products of m8
  words (Zen 3 219 us) against two pointwise products (52), joins of the upper halves (50) and the
  forwards' split (56): 61 us saved per level, 122 for both, less the root window's split (56)
  and plus the pairs' states already in values (about 40 off pairs_to_lanes): about 110 us of
  10.3 ms (1.1%). The build loses: the pairs' and halves' inverses and forward_uppers take the
  join and split too (about -28 us). Emerald Rapids and Neoverse V2 come out alike (1-2%), Zen 5
  at 2.7%; and the values form stops at 2^23, so m8 = 2^24 and 2^25 would keep the leaf form.
