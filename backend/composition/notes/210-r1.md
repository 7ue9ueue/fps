# #210 r1: P^'s forward in place in compose's up pass

2026-10-18, claude (issue #210, round 1: ntt's avx2 tiles; this unit's part). The whole log, with
the measurements: `backend/ntt/notes/210-r1.md`.

- What: `compose_levels`' generic levels transformed P^ at 2m from p into FP, and the product hook
  of the inverse at 4m read FP. Now the forward runs in place in p and the hook reads p; the
  inverse writes R's upper half to p in its top level, after its last hook call (ntt's
  `inverse_hooked` documents it now). FP is not used in that loop any more.
- Why: from another array the forward's top level moves 1.25 times the lines (FP fetched for
  writing besides P's half read). The levels below were already as fast as lc-opt's.
- Measured (`lc-bench`, Zen 3, GCC 16.2; ntt's `issue210/phases.cpp`, compose's call sequence):
  the forwards of P^ summed over the levels at m = 2^20 33866 -> 31629 us (lc-opt 31900), at
  2^17 2959 -> 2806 (2835); compose/21 0.9916 of the time of the same tree with the old call
  (`kernel.py bench`, 11 rounds, IQR 0.9908-0.9925), compose/16 0.9939, compose/18 0.9964.
  With ntt's round, against main 018ef8a: compose/21 0.9924 on Zen 3, 0.9873 on Emerald Rapids,
  0.9881 on Zen 5, 0.9935 on Neoverse V2; below 2^16 layout moves it by up to 3-4% either way
  (Zen 5: 1.014-1.040 at the default alignment, 0.990-1.008 with `-falign-functions=64`).
- Tests: `test.cpp` and the round 2-3 tests pass unchanged (compose's outputs are the same words);
  ntt's `test_hooked_in_place.cpp` checks both in-place uses on every variant.
