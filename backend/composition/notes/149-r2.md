## Small sizes

The first bench against main had compose/6 at 1.05 (Zen 3), 1.12 (Emerald Rapids), 1.015 (Zen
5), power_projection/6 1.04, 1.12, 1.02. Main's composition and the new one in one binary
(`ab.cpp`) put Emerald Rapids at +13% for compose at n = 32, Zen 3 at +1.6%; perf on `lc-intel`:
15% of the samples in compose_levels against 3.5%, at `rep stos`: GCC made the fills of a few
words (zero_columns' rows of 2-16 words) inline `rep stos`, slow to start on Intel. Vector stores
(`zero_words`, and below a vector a product by 0 / 1 lanes in `zero_columns`) took it to +2-4%;
the four permute indices of `Stride2` are built once (they cost about 50 ns a call on Neoverse V2
at n = 32), and the coefficient loops run on ymm on AVX-512 (Narrow, as the leaf passes).
`double_level` is out of line (compose/6 on Zen 3 1.064 -> 1.028 in kernel.py's runs: layout).
