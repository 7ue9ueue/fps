## Steps (`hook.cpp` on `lc-bench`, ns per 8 pairs, tile / m = 2^20)

| step | projection | Graeffe | product |

| lc-opt | 113.3-114.4 / 120.3-122.2 | 61.6-62.2 / 62.7-63.6 | 75.0-75.9 / 79.2-80.8 |

- 1: both leaves of a pair in one body (`graeffe_odd` twice) spilled; lc-opt calls a noinline
  `leaves` per leaf. Shoup products (`PairTables::w`, `wq`: 4 multiplies on x86) for t c_j where
  Montgomery's take 6. With the leaf function alone the projection's is then 4-7% behind lc-opt's
  (101.8-105.0 against 97.8-98.2): the same instructions within 2%.
- 2: lc-opt's CRT, (A + B) + u^4 (A - B) / s, twice the true one: 4 Shoup products per output
  leaf for 8 Montgomery ones, and no scaled table per level (round 2's 2^32 8 / B constants: 0.92
  ms of compose at 2^20 to build). The factor goes into the leaf forms: c' = c^2 2^-31 (L / 8) per
  doubled level (`doubled_factor`), row 0's fix 2c, the last doubled level's hook undoing c^2
  (`scaled_crt`), compose's up pass starting from f reversed times 1 / prod c (its products by the
  stored Q_s carry the c's).
- 3: `reduce_sums<true>`: A, B below 2P, A + B reduced twice: 16 instructions fewer per 8 pairs.
- 4: x86 dispatch of both leaves' 16 vectors at once spilled too; the product's leaf inline
  (out of line 83.6 against 76.8).
- Level 1's products the same way (`level1_out_of_line`, Shoup weights): its hook 1218 -> 1079
  us of power projection at 2^17, 11404 -> 10390 at 2^20 (levels 0 and 1 now 1688 / 16118 against
  lc-opt's 1752 / 16304).
- The projection's sums interleaved (`graeffe_odd`: both even-word sums, then both odd-word
  sums; step 1 had the Graeffe's then the odd product's): power projection at 2^20 in its call
  sequence, ms, Emerald Rapids 285.7 / 287.1 -> 282.1 / 282.9, Zen 5 152.5 / 153.0 -> 151.7 /
  151.9, Neoverse V2 433.1 / 432.3 -> 429.9 / 431.4, Zen 3 279.7 -> 280.1 (the doubled Q 0.3%
  faster).
- Column levels: the T - 1 add and the column fixes as vector loops (they were scalar): at 2^20,
  us, the add 815 -> 301 (lc-opt 585), the fixes of level T - 3 2605 -> 2268, T - 2 1274 -> 1019.
