## What

- The leaf passes in the transforms (ntt's hooked ops): the Graeffe (and power projection's V,
  W) in Q's forward at 4m, on each run of leaves in cache; compose's backward step as the hook of
  R's inverse at 4m: R's leaves from P^'s leaf at 2m and Q_s's stored leaf, 32 products per leaf
  (a(z^2) c(-z), signs (-1)^k), where the spread pass and the 64-product leaf product of
  `inverse_product` were. Q_s's leaves are stored as they are (no negated copy). The CRT keeps
  the sums' 2^-32 (the hooked inverse takes it); V's inverse runs in place in G.
- Pruned transforms: the column bit of each row (Q's forward bit L, V's inverse L/2, P^'s forward
  L/2, R's inverse L), the top level included.
- One-dimensional levels (lc-opt's): 0 (Graeffe of g at 2m; compose's step one product at 2m),
  1 (m >= 128; transforms of q1, q2 at m, three products per leaf pair, five for power
  projection, inverses at m/2), T - 3 and T - 2 as columns in y (m >= 256), T - 1 as before.
- Neon: 64-bit leaf sums (umull/umull2, adds folded into umlal), as x86's; every simd struct now
  takes the one leaf-sum path (the Montgomery-per-term code is gone).
- The coefficient loops of the special levels vectorized (8 x 8 transposes for the columns,
  branch-free loops with __restrict for GCC's -O2 vectorizer); the projection pass shares Q's
  t c_j and odd words between its Graeffe and odd product.

## Steps, `lc-bench` (Zen 3), same run as lc-opt, n = 2^17, ms

| version | compose | power_projection | lc-opt (compose / projection) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| base (#148) | 42.4 | 39.7 | 29.5 / 26.0 |
| 1: hooks + pruned groups (not the top level) | 38.1 | 36.7 | 29.3 / 25.7 |
| 2: + levels T - 3, T - 2 as columns | 35.3 | 34.5 | 29.3 / 25.7 |
| 3: + level 0 one-dimensional | 34.2 | 32.4 | 29.3 / 25.6 |
| 4: + level 1 one-dimensional | 33.2 | 31.5 | 29.7 / 25.9 |
| 5: + top levels pruned (avx2) | 32.1 | 31.3 | 29.6 / 25.9 |
| 6: + coefficient loops vectorized | 31.6 | 31.0 | 29.4 / 25.9 |
| 7: + projection pass shares t c_j | | 30.3 | 25.8 |
| final (the full run of notes.md) | 31.7 | 30.3 | 29.2 / 25.8 |

Per step (`parts.cpp`, m = 2^17, us), after step 1: a generic level s costs 2.37 ms at s = 0 .. 2
(nothing above the column bit but the top level) down to 1.96 at s = 11; s = 14, 15 (L = 8, 4:
columns of one vector, nothing to prune) 2.37 each, which is why the column levels pay most.
Level 0 one-dimensional: 0.88 + 0.57 ms against 2.37; its coefficient loops were 0.33 ms of the
0.88 before step 6. Column levels down 1.23 ms, up 1.37, T - 1 0.47 (lc-opt: T - 3 1.26, T - 2
and T - 1 1.55).

Not a gain by itself: the Graeffe inside the forward costs what the pass did apart (265 us at m =
2^17 either way; 772 instructions per 8 pairs, 208 vpmuludq, compute-bound); the gain of step 1
is the passes over memory and the halved products of the backward step.

perf (`lc-intel`, compose at 2^17, avx2 build, after step 4): asm butterflies 33%, the product hook
10%, the Graeffe 8%, the top levels 16% (then pruned in step 5), the coefficient loops 2%.

## Small sizes on Zen 5

The first bench against the base had compose/8 at 1.12 and power_projection/8 at 1.04 on Zen 5
(1.03 on Emerald Rapids): at 2^7 the intrinsics' subtrees have 4 leaves, so the hooks ran one pair
at a time (ntt's log). With one run of all the leaves below 2^8, `ab.cpp` on `lc-zen5` (us, base
-> new): compose n = 32 2.88 -> 2.60, 64 2.87 -> 2.68, 128 6.38 -> 5.91, 256 14.5 -> 11.6;
power_projection 2.84 -> 2.29, 2.92 -> 2.40, 6.37 -> 5.37, 13.7 -> 10.7.
