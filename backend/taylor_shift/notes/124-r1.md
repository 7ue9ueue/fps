## Tests

`test.cpp`: `shift` against the binomial sums (O(n^2)) for n in 0 .. 200, powers of two and their
neighbours to 2^12 and random sizes (points out(x0) = f(x0 + c) past 1024; t < n against the full

minimum and around it, n / 2, n - 1, n}, out over f (whole, first half), out at word offsets 0-3
with guard words; every scan variant (`Scan<S, K, step>`, every struct, K in {1, 2, 4, 8}, both

argument checks. Mutations caught: the output without A_k (2273 failures), out always taken as

(`lc-amd`), avx512 (`lc-intel-test`), zen5 (`lc-zen5-test`), arm (`lc-arm`, Mac).
