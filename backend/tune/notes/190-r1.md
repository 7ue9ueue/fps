# 190 r1: the pinned compiler, GCC 15.2.0 -> 16.2.0

2026-10-13, branch `agent/toolchain-all-r1`, measured on main 1c59503, retuned on main 5ec304b (2026-10-13, after #193, #202, #206).
Raw times, logs and the scripts: `~/Documents/hpc_library-explore/toolchain/190/` on the Mac.

## Why

GCC 15.2.0 miscompiles a reduction loop at -O2 with AVX2 (#183, worked around in `backend/roots`).
The user's decision: do not stay pinned to a compiler with a known bug; pick 15.3.0 or 16.2.0 by
measurement. Images (all Debian 13, glibc 2.41): `gcc:15.2.0@sha256:3ae15afe...`,
`gcc:15.3.0@sha256:1fdcd041...`, `gcc:16.2.0@sha256:17043c23...` (index digests: the same pin
serves x86 and arm).

## Tools

- `kernel.py --image IMAGE` builds (and runs) in another image; `kernel.py bench` builds each side
  with its own checkout's pin (`IMAGE` of its `tools/kernel.py`; `--base-image` overrides), and
  when the two differ it runs each side in its own image (its libstdc++), one container per side
  and round. Comparing compilers on the same code: `vm.py --base HEAD`, then
  `kernel.py --image <candidate> bench <unit> --base $BASE`.
- `kernel.py test --base` skipped a test when the preprocessed text matched the base's, assuming
  the same compiler. After a pin change that is wrong (the base passed with the old compiler), so it
  now skips only if the base was tested with the image in use (its `IMAGE` or `COMPAT`).
- So the gate of this change times 16.2.0 (this tree) against 15.2.0 (main) and runs every test.

## Tests

Choosing: `kernel.py --image <candidate> test` of all 17 units (-O2 and ASan/UBSan) per target on
its test machine (the branch on main dfc4aff), -j 2:
- 15.3.0: every target passes. `scalar`, `sse`, `avx2` (lc-amd) and `arm` (lc-arm) 104 of 104.
  `avx512` (lc-intel-test) and `zen5` (lc-zen5-test): 68 passed, then `taylor_shift` `test.cpp`
  ASan/UBSan was killed building ("Killed signal terminated program cc1plus": out of memory, the
  machines at load 10-16 with other agents' builds); rerun, the rest (`taylor_shift`, `tune`,
  `frontend`) pass, 38 of 38.
- 16.2.0: every target passes, 104 of 104: `scalar`, `sse`, `avx2`, `arm`, and (second run, after
  the same OOM in the first) `avx512` and `zen5`.
- Without the #183 workaround: 15.3 and 16.2 pass `roots` on `avx2`, 15.2 fails 109 of 1951
  (`backend/roots/notes/190-r1.md`).

Final tree (main 5ec304b, this change and the retuned tables, commit cae4c36): `kernel.py test` of
all 18 units (`factor` is new), -O2 and ASan/UBSan, 106 of 106 pass on every target, no skips:
`scalar`, `sse`, `avx2` (lc-amd, -j 2), `avx512` (lc-intel-test, -j 3), `zen5` (lc-zen5-test,
-j 3), `arm` (lc-arm-test, -j 3) with 16.2.0, and `avx2` on lc-amd with 15.2.0 (`--image`, the
`COMPAT` image), 106 of 106. Logs: `final/` in the folder above.

## Decision

16.2.0. It is faster or equal on every target (geomean 0.9943-1.0005, large cases 0.9953-0.9959),
passes every test, and fixes #183. 15.3.0 is no faster anywhere and above the 1.005 limit on
`zen5` (1.0060). The cost of 16.2: the short Bostan-Mori cases (#198) and `arm` square products of
64-512 words (#197, which 15.3 shares).

## After the switch

- `tools/tune.py` for every tuned kernel (`ntt`, `small`, `division`, `factorials`, in that order:
  `small` times the NTT with its compiled defaults) on main 5ec304b plus this change, each table
  its own commit (a first retune on dfc4aff was redone after #193, #202 and #206 merged; `factor`
  has no tuned table). Changes against main's 15.2 tables (variants: entries changed; costs: new /
  old, median over a field's entries):
  - `ntt`: `avx512` 1-2 of 20 entries of `forward`, `inverse`, `multiply`, `product`; `zen5` 1 of
    20 of `inverse` and `multiply`; `scalar`, `sse`, `avx2`, `arm` unchanged.
  - `small`: schoolbook variants 1-3 of 44 entries on `scalar`, `sse`, `avx2`, 11 on `avx512`, 15
    on `zen5`, 22 on `arm`; model fits 0.71-1.33 (`square` 0.71 and `call` 1.33 on `avx512`,
    `square` 1.24 on `arm`, `word` 0.78-0.97 on x86), NTT times 0.92-1.02.
  - `division`: cost fits 0.81-0.84 on `arm` (`forward`, `cyclic`, `product`, `inverse`; its NTT
    went to asm since the last tune), 0.87-0.89 on `zen5` (`forward`, `cyclic`, `inverse`),
    0.92-0.95 on `scalar`, 1.00-1.04 on `sse`, `avx2`, `avx512`.
  - `factorials`: the variant of 51 of 88 size classes on `sse` (Shoup to Montgomery products:
    `sse.1.montgomery` 1.2x faster than `sse.1.shoup` at n = 18-26 with 16.2), 1-7 on `avx2`,
    `avx512`, `zen5`; `scalar` and `arm` unchanged.
- Users on 15.2 stay supported: `tools/kernel.py` `COMPAT` lists (target, machine, image) pairs
  the gate tests (never times) beside the pin: `avx2` on `lc-amd` with 15.2.0, where #183 lives.
  The gate runs it as one more test job on `lc-amd` (its cores split 4 ways instead of 3); its
  skips work as for the pin, so a pull request that changes one kernel rebuilds that kernel's tests
  only. The #183 workaround stays (it costs nothing: `backend/roots/notes/190-r1.md`).
- The gate of this pull request runs `main`'s `gate.py`, which does not know `COMPAT` yet: the
  15.2 run starts with the next gate.
