## Steps

1. The port: lc-opt's steps on `ntt::Transform` (forward from a span, `inverse_product` of one
   term into the coefficients, `forward_upper` from a span), the pair step and the coset folds as
   templates on a simd struct of 8-word rows (`product_tree::Lanes` and `Sums`: x86 mul_even, Neon
   umull/umlal, Scalar u64), the shift by `ntt::middle_product` with factorials, zero masks per
   ISA, powers of 3 from three tables of 1024. Same run against lc-opt on Zen 3 (`lc-bench`, 3
   rounds, lg 7-12): random f 0.91-0.97, 2^lg distinct roots 0.93-1.08, repeated 0.95-0.99; lc-opt's
   N = 4000 tests: all_distinct 1.19, all_same 0.98, max_random 1.01. all_distinct: std::sort of the
   4000 roots (the API's order; lc-opt writes them in any order) cost about 1/6.
2. A radix sort (3 passes of 10 bits): all_distinct 1.19 -> 1.00.
3. Degrees just past a power of two, where lc-opt's lengths double (bit_ceil(d + 1)); the bench's
   2^lg sizes are that case. Graeffe steps at bit_floor(d) with the wrapped coefficients computed
   directly (`wrapped`, up to kWrap = 16), the shift's product at 2 bit_floor(d + 1) with up to 8
   top coefficients added directly, and l from d <= 8 2^l (a 9th term per row, not twice the
   points). Mac (M4, `phases.cpp`), d = 4096: random f 1127 -> 599 us, 4096 roots 1261 -> 812.
4. On H, A alone, x A' and B for groups with zeros (all three while the last group had zeros):
   random f of degree 4096 599 -> 409 us on the Mac; Zen 3 0.959 -> 0.366 of lc-opt's time.
5. GCC 15.2 at -O2 with AVX2 miscompiled the folds' loop over the terms for the Scalar struct (6 of
   8 lanes wrong; -O3, SSE and clang right; nothing under ASan/UBSan): `#pragma GCC novector` on
   that loop. Issue #183 has a reproducer.
6. The pair step spilled: 678 stack accesses per 8 pairs in GCC's asm (Avx512Vl; 679 Avx2) for 432
   vpmuludq, with even_parts' EvenOdd operands (two registers each) all live. Columns through a

## Not done, for later rounds

- Many repeated roots or collisions: a fold of f per 8 (16) tracked points per climb stage,
  quadratic (21 ms at d = 24576 with 4096 roots of multiplicity about 4, Zen 3): issue #185.
- The Graeffe step: 4 transforms of n (ntt's) and the pair pass (about 40% of a step on Zen 3 and
  Neoverse V2). A values-form step (3 products per pair of points, not 13 per word) loses to the
  split and join passes it needs (estimate from backend/gcd's 0.42 ns per word each on Zen 3:
  about 8.4 against 8.8 us per step at n = 4096); Karatsuba for E F - y O G would cut 28 of 104
  products per pair of leaves (guess: 3-5% of a run).
- H with many roots evaluates x A' and B everywhere to use them at about 1/15 of the points.
- Emerald Rapids: random f at 2^14 is 4% slower with the zmm folds.

Tests (`test.cpp`): planted roots (distinct, repeated, 0, all equal, times irreducible quadratics)
for n = 1 .. 70, 2^j - 1 .. 2^j + 1 to 2^13 + 1, degrees 2^j - 1 .. 2^j + 17 for j = 6, 7, 9, 11
(the wrapped steps and shift), lc-opt's N = 4000 shapes; random f of degree 2 .. 60 and 63 .. 145
against deg gcd(f, x^P - x) by naive powers, and of degree 200 .. 3000 times planted roots against
it by the backend's products, division and half-gcd; roots colliding under r -> r^(2^c) (c = 1 ..
23, at the kernel's own shift) with repeats; roots at the first shifts (the deflation loop); x^1024
- 1, x^119 - 1, x^1000 - 1, x^4096 - 3, x^2 + 1, x^2 - 3; three other seeds; every struct of 8-word
rows the target runs (and Avx512 folds); guard words after out; argument checks. Mutations caught:
no climbs (507 failures), a wrong step factor in the wrapped coefficients (344). -O2 and
ASan/UBSan on `lc-amd` (scalar, sse, avx2), `lc-intel-test`, `lc-zen5-test`, `lc-arm` and the Mac.
