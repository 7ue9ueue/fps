## Where the time went (base)

The blocked division (log, pow, inverse_blocks) sums B(B - 1)/2 leaf products of kept transforms in
its residuals (lc-opt's Toeplitz sharing saves one at 4 blocks). On Zen 5 a leaf product costs about
a transform (issue #251; `vprims.cpp` at 2^19: forward 0.46 ns per word, each further term of an
inverse_product 0.45-0.5), so 5 blocks of L/4 lost to the ragged chain there and inverse kept the
chain to r = L/4 (`chain_up_to` 8 on Zen 5).

## What went in

1. ntt: `forward(in, F, Form::values)`, `to_values(F, V)` and `inverse_product(..., Form::values)`
   (the products summed and joined in each run of leaves of a hooked inverse; ntt notes). In the
   values form a residual of j terms is one inverse with j Montgomery products per word (0.05-0.15
   ns each on Zen 5, 0.25-0.35 elsewhere, against 0.45-1.2 for leaf products), for a split in each
   forward of W and Q (+0.19 ns per word on Zen 5, +0.34-0.41 on Zen 3 and Emerald Rapids, +0.64 on
   Neoverse V2) and a join in each residual's inverse.
2. `divide()`: W_t and Q_i in `shape.values`' form (`DivisionShape::values`, from
   `values_division(t, k)`, t the blocks by transforms: a direct last block has none); H stays a
   leaf form (the products by h stay fused cyclic products: a values-form one is a forward with a
   split, a product pass and an inverse with a join, 1.37 against 1.21 ns per word on Zen 5 at 2^19);
   no Toeplitz product in the values form; inverse_blocks' Q_0 from H by `to_values` (its own
   array: one array of 2k more); log's q_j in R (a values-form forward does not run in place); pow
   keeps the leaf form where exp's last step takes its kept transforms (3 and 4 blocks: those save
   two forwards of 2k, more than the values form gains there).
3. The choice, per target: t = 5 everywhere; t = 4 on Zen 5 for k = 2^11 .. 2^18; t = 3 nowhere.
4. `chain_up_to`: with 5 values-form blocks the chain stops at 4L/32 on x86, 5L/32 on Neoverse V2
   (was 5L/32, and 8L/32 on Zen 5 and below L = 2^14); 6 at L = 128, 8 below; on Zen 5 from L = 2^21
   8 (its L3).

## Not done; next rounds

- 3 blocks (inverse and log at (1.25, 1.5] 2^k): 3 products in the residuals against 6 conversions;
  1.00-1.05 in the values form everywhere.
- Zen 5 at 4 blocks of k = 2^19 (log at (1.5, 2] 2^20) and the blocks at L = 2^21: the arrays pass
  its L3; fewer arrays (lc-opt shares one buffer for the residuals, q_j and T(q_j), notes/87-r2.md)
  would help both forms.
- Newton's products by kept transforms (inverse, exp, sqrt: 2-7 a step) stay fused leaf products:
  a values-form cyclic product as two hooked passes costs more (above); one depth-first pass with
  the split and join in its tiles (an ntt fused op) might win on Zen 5, by the costs above at most
  0.1-0.15 ns per word and product.
