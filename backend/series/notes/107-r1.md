# #107 r1: pow and sqrt from lc-opt

2026-10-13, claude (issue #107, round 1). `fps::series::pow(f, e, c, out)` (c (f / f[0])^e) and
`fps::series::sqrt(f, c, out)` (g^2 = f, g[0] = c), from lc-opt's `lib/poly/{pow,sqrt}.hpp`
(13e78b2; its notes: Power, Sqrt), on `ntt::Transform`'s fused ops, every target. Base: main
c47d47d. Harnesses in the exploration folder `~/Documents/hpc_library-explore/series/issue107/`
(`compare_pow.cpp`: fps, lc-opt and the frontend's `raw::pow`, `raw::sqrt` in one binary,
outputs checked equal, alternating, 5-7 rounds of 5 samples of >= 2 ms, medians; `phases.cpp`:
the two bodies with clocks; `probe_ip.cpp`, `probe_fwd.cpp`: placement probes).

## What went in

- log's blocked division as `divide(f, count, shape, arrays, numerator, place, done)`, which log
  and pow share (log's code path otherwise as before; `derivative_block` became `index_block`
  with a factor).
- exp's steps as `exp_steps(q, g0, g, arena, given)` from any q and g[0], templated on the
  transforms a caller hands the last step (`QTransforms` for pow, `NoQTransforms` for exp, so
  exp compiles as before; a first version with the transforms as a runtime argument moved
  `exp/11` by +5% and -0.6% on Zen 5 between two builds, code layout, #93).
- pow: the division of N = e x f' by f over n coefficients (lc-opt: f' over n - 1, then a pass
  that scales by e and shifts into q), so the blocks are q's own and T(q_0) + s T(q_1), T(q_2)
  are exp's last-step transforms for every n > 64 (lc-opt: `times_x` on T(f'/f), and not when
  n - 1 is a power of two). See notes.md, pow.
- sqrt: lc-opt's steps (h = -1/g at half precision, 11 T + 5 LP of m a step, Karp and Markstein's
  last step).
- Tests (`test_pow_sqrt.cpp`): against O(n^2) recurrences (pow from f g' = e f' g, sqrt from 2 c
  g_i = f_i - sum g_j g_(i-j)) on 1 .. 64, 2^k +- 1 to 4097, 3 2^k +- 1, 12 random sizes; f[0] =
  1, a square, random; random / mostly zero / all P - 1; f of n, n + 3, n / 2 words; e = 0, 1, 2,
  P - 1, (P + 1) / 2, random; identities at 4097 .. 300001 and 2^20, 1.5 2^20, 2^22 + 1 (not
  under ASan): f^2 = f f, f^3, f^-1 = inverse, sqrt(f)^2 = f, pow(f, 1/2) = sqrt(f), f^a f^b =
  f^(a+b), pow = exp(e log(f / f[0])), sqrt(p^2) = p for a polynomial p; (1 - x)^-1 and sqrt(1 -
  4x) (Catalan numbers) at 10^6; scratch at 6 offsets, too small a scratch; out over f in place
  and shifted both ways; 4 threads; n = 2^25 + 1 throws. 16 mutations of series.hpp, each caught
  (TQ's sign, block2 from Q_1, the numerator's shift, the given low ignored, c not applied, h's
  sign, d1 without g d0, g d0's lower half, half_residual without f[m + i] or with a wrong half,
  pow_direct's e + 1, sqrt_direct's 2c, the short last step's H, sqrt's copy of an overlapping f,
  the division's last block). Pass at -O2 and ASan/UBSan on `lc-amd` (avx2, sse, scalar),
  `lc-intel-test`, `lc-zen5-test`, `lc-arm` and the Mac.
- Bench: `bench_pow_sqrt.cpp` (a binary of its own, so `bench.cpp`'s code does not change):
  `pow/<lg>`, `sqrt/<lg>`, `_ragged`.

## Attempts and findings (Zen 3, `lc-bench`, GCC 15.2, -O2 -march=x86-64-v3)

1. First port, sqrt arrays G, H, W, R, h: sqrt 0.99 of lc-opt at 2^20 and 2^21 but 1.03 at
   327680, 458752, 500000. Phases at 500000: the last step's inverse_product of G G into R
   (2^18, 237856 words out) 675-685 us against lc-opt's 485 (in place). The same call into G or
   H (behind W) took 471 / 488 us, into R + 1 KiB 512, R again right after 589: the output 320
   bytes past the work array (mod 4 KiB) costs, out behind it does not. In `probe_ip.cpp`
   (repeated calls, warm) every placement took 465-520 us, so the effect needs the step's
   cache state; a guess: the 8 output streams of the radix-8 top level meet the input streams'
   prefetches in the same L2 sets. Arrays reordered G, H, R, h, W (W last): sqrt 0.99-1.00.
2. exp's arrays with W last too: 0.950 / 1.000 / 1.003 / 1.012 of lc-opt at 2^14 / 18 / 20 / 21
   against 0.946 / 0.996 / 1.005 / 1.014: no change, not kept.
3. `half_residual` with load_part and a Montgomery product by 1/2: 53 us against lc-opt's 28 at
   2^17. With plain loads when f covers the block and x / 2 as (x >> 1) + (x & 1) (P + 1) / 2
   (shift, sub, mul_lo, add): 27 us; the last step's two passes 30 + 30 us against 62 + 18.
4. log's `index_block` with the factor as a second Montgomery factor per lane: log/11 1.0175 on
   Zen 5, 1.0085 on Zen 3 (one more scalar mulmod per lane per block); with a 2^32 folded in
   once: 1.002.

pow phases at 500000 (us, fps / lc-opt): division 8983 / 8721, exp 10747 / 10754. In the
division: inverse 1555 / 1598, T(h) and T(W_t) (4 forwards of 2^18) 1244 / 1137, residuals of
blocks 2 and 3 1676 / 1441 (lc-opt's Toeplitz sharing: one leaf product of 2^18 fewer), the
forwards T(q_j) of 2^18 297-305 each / 251 (lc-opt in place with its fused radix-8 top for a
half-zero source; `probe_fwd.cpp`: fps's out-of-place forward of a half-zero source at 2^18 is
286-305 us at every placement mod 64 KiB), the blocks' cyclic products 2957 / 2906; lc-opt's
sink pass 190 and times_x ~175, which fps does not have.

## Not done, for the next rounds

- The division's Toeplitz sharing (blocks 2 and 3 of 4: 3 leaf products instead of 4, the sum m1
  + m3 stored by block 2's bottom): about 230 us of pow and log at 500000 on Zen 3. It needs an
  inverse_product bottom with a second, stored sum (ntt). With the existing ops (m1 by a
  multiply pass, terms alone) it saves one fused leaf product but adds 3 passes and moves the
  sums off the asm bottoms: about even, not tried.
- A forward of a half-zero source in place with lc-opt's sparse radix-8 top (ntt; 251 against
  ~290 us at 2^18): the division's T(h) and T(q_j), sqrt's T_2m(h), inverse's G.
- pow: the exp step before the last at m = k could take T_k(q mod x^k) from T(q_0)'s lower half
  (a multiply instead of a forward_product of length k, ~0.3% guessed); not tried.
