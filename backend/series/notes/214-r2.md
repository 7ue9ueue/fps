## What went in

1. `inverse_blocks`: the inverse as the blocked division with N = 1 (`divide<true>`, the same code
   as log's and pow's division with a flag): h = 1 / f mod x^k by Newton into g[0, k), H = T(h) is
   also Q_0, block j's residual is -(f Q)[jk, jk + c) (no numerator pass: the product by h is
   negated), and a last block that is not full keeps its residual in R (H is one of its terms). The
   division's shapes: 5 blocks of L/4 for n in (L, 1.25 L], 3 of L/2 to 1.5 L (4 of L/2 beyond).
   3 blocks of L/2 cost 10 transforms and 5 leaf products of L after h, 2 transforms fewer than the
   steps to L and the half step (the step to L keeps T(f mod x^L) and T(h) for the last block); 5
   blocks of L/4 have, at r = L/4, 2 transforms (of L) fewer and 2 leaf products more than the
   chain's steps.
2. The choice (`inverse_method`): the chain to r = 5L/32 (8L/32 on Zen 5 and below L = 2^14),
   blocks to L/2, Newton past it and at powers of two. The half step is gone: blocks replace it at
   every r it ran.

## Not done; next rounds

- 1.75 2^k for every op, and inverse past L/2. With power-of-two transforms the steps that land
  there cost about those of 2L: 4 blocks of L/2 (above) and 7 of L/4 (in leaf products, B(B+1)/2)
  do not beat the step at 2L, and the 1.75 L pieces of the 2L transform (x^L + 1, x^(L/2) + 1 and
  x^(L/4) - 1, each a transform we have: forward_upper at 2L and L, forward at L/4) save 0.45 of a
  transform of L per factor but need folds of each long factor and a recombination over 1.75 L
  words, which cost as much (about 0.3 ms each at 2^20 on Zen 3: copy 0.1 ns per word, a fold
  reads 1.75 L words and writes L). The gain needs the truncation inside the transforms (the
  truncated FFT: pruned subtrees in the forward, the known zero top in the inverse), issue #155.
- Small r for log, exp, sqrt and pow (r1's sweep at 2^17, Zen 3: 1.06-1.16 at i = 1-4): a last
  step or block of c coefficients still pays a residual at the step's length (exp: a cyclic
  product of q and g of length m; the division: W_4, Q_3 and 4 leaf products) whatever c. Ideas:
