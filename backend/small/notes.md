# backend/small

Products of short polynomials modulo P, 2^12 < P < 2^30. API: `small.hpp` (`multiply`,
`beats_ntt`). Variant: `schoolbook.hpp` (`Schoolbook<S, R, P, U, Solo, T>`, templates on any
`backend/simd` struct). Model: `model.hpp` (O(n m), one reduction per term). `select.hpp`: each
target's candidates (struct, R, U, Solo, T), the variant per shape class and beats_ntt's costs, from
`tuned.hpp` (generated by `tools/tune.py small` from `tuned.json`; measured by `tune.cpp`).
`tune.hpp`: the run-time measurements of the tuned choices "small.variant" and "small.ntt"
(`backend/tune`). Tests: `test.cpp`, `test_no_tuner.cpp`, `test_ntt_cost.cpp`; bench: `bench.cpp`.

## Design

- Long factor a (n words), short factor b (m <= n). Outputs in blocks of R vectors (R W words, W
  the struct's lanes). For each j, every vector of the block adds a[k - j] b'[j] for its words k:
  one unaligned load of a at k0 + r W - j, and one broadcast of b'[j] shared by the R vectors.
- Sums in 64-bit lanes:
  - x86 (`mul_even`): two vectors per output vector, the even and odd words. The odd words of the
    window at k0 + r W - j are the even words of the window one word on, which the step before
    loaded, so a step is 1 load, 2 `mul_even` and 2 `add64` per output vector (`EvenOdd`).
  - Neon: words 0-1 and 2-3, `umlal` and `umlal2` (`Halves`).
  - Scalar: one `uint64` per word (`Single`).
- b' = b 2^32 mod P (Montgomery form, by Shoup's product), so one Montgomery step (s + m P) / 2^32
  at the end gives the sum of a b. Bounds (`Sums<P>`): terms below (P - 1)^2; a fold
  s = h 2^32 + l -> s - h kQ = h (2^32 mod P) + l, with kQ = 2^32 - (2^32 mod P) a multiple of P
  (x86: shift, `mul_even`, `sub64`; Neon: `uzp2`, `umlsl`, `umlsl2`), keeps s below
  2^32 (2^32 mod P + 1) and below 2^64 for `kFold` more terms; the last step needs s < 3P 2^32
  (then it is below 4P, and two min-reductions make it canonical). For 998244353: a fold every 17
  terms, at most 12 terms before the last step from zero and 11 after a fold.
- Per panel of b: vectors at s >= m - 1 (from `begin`, m - 1 rounded up to W) with s + W < n load
  inside a; they run in full blocks of R (`full`), every j < m. The others, at either end
  (`edge`): each vector has its own range of j ([s + 1 - n, s + W) within [0, m)); both ends grow
  with the vector, so the j loop runs in phases with a fixed set of active vectors [first, last),
  each phase an unrolled loop reached by one jump through a table (`switch` on first, last). Their
  loads read a zero-padded copy of a's start (up to a[begin]) or end (from the last full block
  on), or of all of a when no full block fits: one copy each per panel, the first made before b'
  (a load that spans several of the copy's stores waits until they reach the cache). With Solo
  the edge vectors run one at a time (`one`), without phases. The last vector's words past the
  product are not stored (`store_first`: a masked store on AVX-512, pieces elsewhere).
- The loop with all R vectors is unrolled U times. Neon: every term loop is a loop on pointers
  (`neon_terms`), and an empty `asm` makes each step's base pointer opaque, so GCC loads each
  window instead of keeping windows in registers across j. The loop with all R vectors starts at a
  multiple of 32 bytes (a `.p2align 5` in an `asm` whose operands are the loop's pointers):
  Neoverse V2 runs it 9% slower at any other address, and GCC's `-falign-loops=32:16` aligns it
  only when that skips at most 15 bytes, so its place was luck (#197, `notes/197-r1.md`). The
  arm candidates are all U = 1 since.
- b in panels of at most 1024 words, so the stack scratch is fixed (about 9 KiB: b' and the copy);
  panels after the first add into out (`panels`, out of line). Any n, m work.
- Tiny path (issue #37 round 3): a product of at most T vectors (n + m - 1 <= T W, T = 2 to 4,
  a candidate's parameter) skips `panel` for `tiny`, a function with a small frame (b' and a copy
  of at most (2 + 2T) W words). Every vector runs every step j < m (`group`): zero terms where its
  own range is shorter, none for one vector and at most one for two, so no blocks, phases or edge
  calls. The copy has 2W zeros, a, then T W zeros, stored without overlap. On AVX-512 structs a
  product of one or two vectors has no copy at all: its windows are masked loads of a
  (`group_masked`, `simd::load_mask`; the mask of the window at a + x is bits W + x .. 2W + x - 1
  of ((1 << n) - 1) << W). A copy made just before its loads stalls them there: a load that spans
  several of its stores waits until they reach the cache (Log).
- The variant is a tuned choice per shape class ("small.variant", issue #54 round 3): the octave
  of m ((2^(k-1), 2^k], k <= 9, and m > 512) times 4 ratios (n < 2m, < 4m, < 8m, the rest), 44
  classes; a byte per class indexes the target's candidates (`Selected`: 4-9 of struct, R, U,
  Solo, T). `multiply` reads the table once per call (one acquire load), computes the class (a
  leading-zero count and three compares) and runs the candidate through a chain of compares that
  GCC makes a jump table, inlined into the caller; each candidate's `run` is one call of `panel`
  (one panel) or of `panels`. `tune.hpp` measures the table (`measure_variants`): each class at a
  few shapes, every candidate timed in turn, the least geometric mean of time / fastest wins
  unless the current variant is within the margin. `tuned.hpp` records a hash of each target's
  list (`kSignature`), so a table measured for another list does not compile and wisdom for it is
  refused.
- `beats_ntt(n, m)`: small's time modelled as term n m + word (n + m) + call + square min(n, m)^2
  ns, fitted per target on shapes within 0.4x-2.5x of the NTT; against the NTT's time at the
  product's length N = n + m - 1 (`ntt::multiply` into the caller's memory, as f * g runs it, with
  its ragged and truncated plans, ntt #155): `ntt_ns(N)`, linear in N between five lengths per
  class (2^(lg-1), 2^lg]: 2^lg (`ntt_times`, the tuned choice "small.ntt") and 2^(lg-1) + 1,
  1.25, 1.5, 1.75 2^(lg-1) (`kRagged`, compiled ratios to 2^lg's time), issue #212. Each is
  measured where the two cross for that N and scaled by the model's over small's measured time
  there, so the switch falls where the measured times cross. The fit and the NTT times are
  `tools/tune.py`'s (`tune.cpp`); `tune::run()` measures "small.ntt" at lg 6-16 on this machine,
  scales the rest, and keeps a default within 5%; the points between powers of two follow it by
  their compiled ratios (a choice of their own cost 108-121 ms more at run time to 2^12, and a
  line in the wisdom; notes/212-r1.md). Before #212 the NTT cost the same over each class (the
  plain product at 2^lg, measured by a Convolution at 3 2^(lg-2)): since #155 a product just past
  2^k costs about half of that, so small took shapes the NTT ran up to 2.1x faster. Past 2^25 the
  NTT counts double per step, so huge balanced products still go to the NTT (which throws) and a
  short factor of any length goes to small.

## Sources

- Store-to-load forwarding (a load that spans more than one in-flight store waits for them to be
  written): Intel's and AMD's optimization manuals. The fold by a multiple of P below 2^32 and the
  rest of round 2: ours.

  2^32 mod P: lc-opt's `multiply_lanes` (`lib/poly/product_tree.hpp`, ours), rewritten for one
  product across the lanes instead of one product per lane.
- Montgomery: P. Montgomery, Math. Comp. 44 (1985). Shoup: V. Shoup, NTL; D. Harvey, J. Symb.
  Comp. 60 (2014). Karatsuba: A. Karatsuba, Yu. Ofman, Doklady Akad. Nauk SSSR 145 (1962).
