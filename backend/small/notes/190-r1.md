# 190 r1: retuned with GCC 16.2.0

2026-10-13, branch `agent/toolchain-all-r1` on main dfc4aff. The pinned compiler moved from GCC 15.2.0
to 16.2.0 (issue #190), and `tools/tune.py small` measured the table again on every target with it.
The decision, the measurements and what changed in each table: `backend/tune/notes/190-r1.md`.

Seen while choosing: on `arm`, `multiply/64x64` to `512x512` are 1.3-8.1% slower with 16.2.0 (and with
15.3.0) than with 15.2.0, two runs agree; unbalanced and large products do not move. Issue #197.
