## The problem

`beats_ntt(n, m)` compared small's model with one NTT time per class (2^(lg-1), 2^lg], measured
by an `ntt::Convolution` at 3 2^(lg-2): the staircase from before #155. Since #155 `ntt::multiply`
runs a product just past 2^k at about the cost of 2^k (split and truncated plans), so small took
shapes the NTT ran up to 2.1x faster. tune.cpp's own check on 1422 swept shapes (below), main's
table against the new sweep: 78-92 shapes the slower way per target, worst 2.09-2.18x (Neoverse
V2 16384x96: small 148 us, NTT 71 us); geomean loss over all shapes 1.4-1.7%.

## What changed

- `ntt_ns(N)` (small.hpp): the NTT's time at the product's length, linear in N between five points
  per class (2^(lg-1), 2^lg]: 2^(lg-1) + 1, 1.25, 1.5, 1.75 2^(lg-1) and 2^lg. `ntt_times` ("small.ntt",
  20 values) is the time at 2^lg; `kRagged` (select.hpp, 80 compiled values) the points between,
  as ratios to 2^lg's time. `beats_ntt` = model_ns < ntt_ns(n + m - 1); one acquire load, as before.
- Measured with `ntt::multiply` into the caller's memory (what f * g, raw::mul and series run), not
  the Convolution (construct, copy in): `time_shape`. measure_ntt probes at N = 2^lg (was 3
  2^(lg-2)); `measure_ragged` probes at each of the four points, the same calibration (model's ns =
  model x NTT / small at the crossing of that N). One routine, `detail::measure_points<K>`.
- tune.cpp: the sweep adds the points 1.25 and 1.75 2^(lg-1), 1.125 and 1.625 2^(lg-1) (not points:
  a check of the interpolation) and n = 2^(lg-1) x m (just past a power of two); 1422 shapes, 53-80 s
  per timed target (24-32 s before), sse 94 s, scalar 274 s. Field `ragged`: the 80 ratios.
- `bench.cpp`: `product/<n>x<m>` (gated): beats_ntt's choice, small::multiply or ntt::multiply, as
  `fps::detail::Products::multiply_into` runs it, at 58 shapes near the crossover; `multiply@small`
  and `multiply@ntt` at the same shapes; `multiply@ntt` is now ntt::multiply (was the Convolution).

### Tried and changed

- A fifth tuned choice "small.ragged" (the 80 points in ns, measured by tune::run() to 2^12): 108-121
  ms per run on the four machines on top of "small.ntt"'s 47-55 ms, and `frontend/test_tune.cpp`
  counts the wisdom's lines (9, not 8). The points now follow "small.ntt" by their compiled ratios:
  no run-time cost, no new wisdom line.
- `ntt_ns`'s first form (a division, size_t to double): f * g's decision cost 3-13 ns more on x86
  (`product/24x24` against `multiply@small/24x24`: base +2-4 ns, new +5-13; Zen 5 41 -> 51 ns).
  Now a shift for the segment, a power of two from its exponent bits and signed conversions (one
  instruction each on x86): Zen 5 24x24 1.007, Zen 3 1.031 (`final2`).
- tools/tune.py small reran every field. The variant rows flipped 1-12 classes per target (Emerald
  Rapids 12, Zen 3 11), and Emerald Rapids' `multiply/64x64` read 1.066-1.070 in two runs (class 24,
  4 -> 0): main's variant rows are kept (`keep_variants.py`); the model (term, word, call, square),
  `ntt` and `ragged` are the rerun's at 97cdf19 (the `ragged` field converted from that run's ns to
  ratios, `to_ratios.py`: the same decisions on every shape `cutoffs.py` checks).

## Found

- `include/fps/polynomial.hpp` (head comment) still gives main's cutoffs ("min(n, m) up to 38-161
  next to a long factor of any length, or n = m up to 27-94"): now 30-110 next to a long factor
  (66-110 at 2^20), n = m 45-101. Not edited here (frontend).
- `Products::middle` (polynomial.hpp) asks `beats_ntt(n, m)` for a middle product: small computes
  all n + m - 1 words, but `ntt::middle_product` runs at length n (its cyclic product of 2^lg >= m - 1
  + count). `model_ns(n, m) < ntt_ns(n)` would compare the right lengths; with the NTT's cost at N
  just past 2^k now about that of 2^k, the gap is up to 2x where n + m - 1 crosses a power of two and
  n does not (a guess from the products' table, not measured).
- `Products::square` asks `beats_ntt(a, a)` against `ntt::square`, one forward transform fewer
  (about 2/3 of a product): squares go to small somewhat past their crossover.
- backend/series' `low_product` takes `ragged::choose(N)` (never truncated) on its NTT path, while
  beats_ntt now models `ntt::multiply` (`choose_product`): at 1.5-1.875 2^k its products cost 2-10%
  more than the model says (155-r2's table).
