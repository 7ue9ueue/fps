## What changed

- `gen_avx512_kernels.py`: forms an output picks per kernel, used by Zen 5 only (the Emerald
  Rapids header `avx512_kernels.hpp` is byte-identical to main's):
  - `Bottom.leaf = 'shuf'`: in the leaf products the odd windows 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 are `vpshufd
    0xb1` of windows 0, 2, 4, 6, 8: vpmuludq reads the even words only, and an odd window's even
    words are the window before's odd words. 3 `vpermi2d` per leaf row, not 8, and 5 index loads
    fewer (20 instructions fewer per `_last` and `_both`).
  - `Bottom.halves = 'blend'` (`Graph.halves`): a vector broadcasting one word to its low 8 lanes
    and another to its high 8 (B's coefficients in the leaf products, the pair twiddles) as
    `vpbroadcastd` + `vpblendmd` from an embedded broadcast, not `vpbroadcastd` + a broadcast merged
    under a mask: the same instructions, the second uop on any pipe instead of the permutes'.
  - `odd = 'shuf'` (`Graph.odd_form`): a Shoup product's odd words by `vpshufd 0xf5` (any pipe), not
    `vpsrlq` (two pipes).
  - Knob `feasible` (the Liveness rule on another target's model); op `vpshufd`. (The forms that
    lost below were deleted from the generator: B as lane rows with `vshufi32x4`, joins by
    `vpshufd` + `vpblendmd`, `ordered_loads`; their code is in the exploration folder,
    `x152/gen_avx512_kernels_explore.py`.)
- `zen5_kernels.hpp`: the product bottoms (`multiply_*`, `square_*`, `stored_*`) 'shuf' and 'blend',
  `_both` on the Emerald Rapids model with window 32, `_first` and `_last` window 128 with the
  feasibility rule; the bottom level's statements of 2 pairs 'blend' (forward on the Emerald Rapids
  model, window 96; inverse on Zen 5's, as before) and the forward's of 4 pairs 'blend' with 'shuf'
  odd words (Emerald Rapids model, window 128, feasibility). Unchanged: the inverse of 4 pairs, the
  group loops, two-column groups, scaled groups, first levels from memory.
- `backend/simd/machines.py` (Zen 5): the measured classes of `vpshufd`, `vpunpcklqdq`, `vshufps`
  (any pipe), `vpalignr`, `valignq`, `vshufi64x2` (the permutes' pair); `vinserti64x4` from memory
  corrected to the permutes' pair (main had any pipe: its probe mixed it with `vpaddd`, where the
  two loads per cycle set 0.25 whatever the class).

and `test_join.cpp` (2^6-2^7 joins). Mutations (`mutate.sh`): an odd window from the wrong words
in `multiply_both` (40 checks failed), a half of B from the low lanes in `multiply_last` (48), an
odd-word shuffle off in `bottom_forward4` (62), a pair twiddle half one word off in
`bottom_forward2` (87), a window of `square_last` (48). `kernel.py check` passes (the avx512
header is unchanged).

## Open

- Zen 5's product bottom now runs at about 80% of its uop floor (141 cycles per pair, floor 114);
  the multiplies (144 per pair on two pipes, 72 cycles) are not the bound. Without a PMU the rest
  is a guess (dispatch, the schedulers' pipe choice).
- The asm 4-pair bottoms stream from memory 1.14-1.36x slower than WideBottom's intrinsics loop
  (64 MiB, `pfam.cpp` stream cases): the inverse's first touch of its leaves past 2^23.
- Code placement keeps moving single ops by 2-7% (forward/10, the values form at 2^13-2^20 in one
  binary, middle_product/7 across binaries), as in rounds 1 and 2.
