# #91 r2: Transform on another transform's tables (for backend/product_tree)

2026-10-18, claude (issue #91, round 2: backend/product_tree; its log
`backend/product_tree/notes/91-r2.md`). Base main e948ff1.

- `Transform(lg, tables)`: a transform of length 2^lg on the twiddle tables and value rows of
  `tables`, a Transform of length at least 2^lg that must outlive it (std::invalid_argument if
  shorter). Both are prefix compatible across lengths (`build_table` extends entry by entry,
  `build_value_rows` row by row: row h is A(h) whatever the length), so one longest Transform
  serves every shorter one. Transform now keeps the tables' first line and their length, and owns
  them only when built by `Transform(lg)`; the values form throws std::length_error on tables past
  2^kValuesMaxLog (they have no rows). Move assignment keeps the object's address, so
  backend/product_tree moves its per-thread transforms onto longer tables in place
  (`reserve_transforms`).
- Why: a thread's transform per length kept tables of about 1.25 2^lg bytes each, 2.5 times the
  longest's in all (product_tree at 2^25 coefficients: about 64 MB per thread). On one table:
  1.25 times the longest's. Peak RSS of one product of 2^24 linear factors (lc-amd, with the
  rest of #91 r2): 813 -> 779 MB.
- Speed: the ntt bench's Transform cases (forward, inverse, leaf products, the values form, the
  inverse into other memory, cyclic and inverse products), 5 rounds against main e948ff1, gated
  geomean 1.000 (Zen 3), 1.001 (Emerald Rapids), 0.999 (Zen 5), 1.000 (Neoverse V2). A first form
  kept three pointers (roots, inverse roots, rows) in place of the computed ones: GCC then
  inlined differently across ntt (objdump of bench_leaves.cpp for zen5: `Ntt<Avx512Vl>::forward`
  753 instructions against 673), and on Zen 5 `forward_values` ran 1.02-1.06x at 2^12-2^23 and
  `forward/24`, `/25` 1.02-1.03x (two runs, 7 rounds); main's code with a padded Transform (the
  object's size alone) ran as main (0.999). The kept form holds a line pointer and the tables'
  length (`lines_`, `tables_lg_`), so the accessors compute as main's: the same function sizes in
  bench.cpp, a few within 2 instructions in bench_leaves.cpp, and Zen 5 at 1.001.
- Tests: `test_shared_tables.cpp` (new): every op that reads the tables (forward, inverse,
  forward_upper, inverse_upper, the values form, leaf products, the inverse into other memory, a
  cyclic product) on a transform on shared tables against one with its own, at every length up
  to tables of 2^(kMinLog+2), 2^12, 2^16, 2^17 and 2^20 (2^18 under ASan); after the owner moves;
  moved onto other tables in place (the old ones freed); the errors. A mutation (the inverse
  roots 16 words off) failed 295 checks.
