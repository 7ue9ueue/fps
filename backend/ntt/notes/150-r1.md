## What was built

- `gen_avx512_kernels.py` (new): gen_avx2_kernels.py's method on zmm, with the machine model
  from `backend/simd/machines.py` (notes.md, avx512). Builders for every butterfly shape the zmm
  intrinsics run (forward and inverse groups, identity groups and their input bounds, the bottom
  forward and inverse, the product bottoms fused or in stages); `EMITTED_LOOPS` and
  `EMITTED_BOTTOMS` choose what `avx512_kernels.hpp` holds: the ones that won.
- `avx512.hpp` (new): `variant::avx512`, the zmm intrinsics on a struct of their own with
  `intrinsics.hpp`'s new hook `Generated<S>` (3 call sites: forward_group_part, bottom_multiply,
  bottom_multiply_stored). A candidate of the avx512 target only; `tools/tune.py ntt --target
  avx512` re-measured the table.

## Where the zmm intrinsics spend their time (before)

`perf record` of `multiply` (factors 2^lg / 2), share of cycles and (p0 + p5 uops) / 2 / cycles
(`uops_dispatched.port_5_11` counts the load port 11 too, so these are upper bounds):

| lg | WideBottom::multiply | forward_groups | forward_group | inverse_groups | inverse_group(_scaled) |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 49% (0.90) | 23% (0.90) | 7.5% (0.91) | 11% (0.85) | 5.7% (0.99) |
| 16 | 39% (0.90) | 16% (0.95) | 21% (0.87) | 10% (0.90) | 10% (0.95) |

On L1 data (`perf stat`, 1024 words): forward groups 466 cycles per 16 columns at 0.985 of the
port bound, inverse 0.99, bottom forward 0.87, bottom inverse 0.95, product bottom 0.92. So the
groups were already at the bound; uops, not scheduling, set their pace. Shoup's product on zmm is
9 uops (shift, 2 vpmuludq, vpermt2d, 2 vpmulld of 2 uops, sub); no form with fewer was found
(even/odd 64-bit forms, IFMA in 52-bit lanes, signed: 10-13).

## Memory forms on Emerald Rapids (`mprobe.cpp`)

Cycles one instruction adds to a loop that keeps p0 and p5 busy with vpaddd (0.5 = one p0/p5 uop):
merge-masked load 0.64, zero-masked load 0.62, merge-masked broadcast 0.51, plain broadcast 0.09,
`vinserti64x4` from memory 0.52, `vpermd` from memory 0.62, `kmovw` from memory 0.50, masked
store 0.20, plain store 0.09. So masked loads cost an ALU uop; masked stores nearly nothing; a
mask loaded per statement costs a uop (masks are now "Yk" operands).

## Attempts (families of generated variants, `fam.cpp`, 1024 words on L1, median of 7, time / intrinsics)

1. Forward groups, 2 columns per iteration, odd words from loads 4 bytes on: 1.00 (p0 uops 452 ->
   424 per 16 columns, but p5 then bound it). 4 columns: 0.978 in one run, 0.997 in another
   (the intrinsics moved 112-115 ns between runs). Kept (4 columns, groups of at least 4).
2. Inverse groups: 0.991-1.005 (2, 4, 8 columns). Identity groups: within 1%. Not kept.
3. Joins by shift and blend instead of vpermt2d (to move work off p5): never in the top 5 of any
   family. Not kept.
4. Bottom forward: 0.997-1.01 best of 32; bottom inverse 1.06-1.10, every variant. Twiddle pairs
   by vpermd of the table block (as the intrinsics) instead of masked broadcasts: no change;
   outputs as 8 half stores under masks instead of 4 vpermi2d: bottom inverse 1.10 -> 1.06.
   Constants as GCC register operands: GCC spilled them (the asm's memory clobber) and reloaded
   them every pair, as many loads as loading them in the statement. Not kept: cause not found.
5. Product bottom in one statement per pair (windows by vpermi2d in registers, no rows stored in
   halves): 0.958-0.977. In two stages interleaved (last of pair j with first of pair j + 1, as
   lc-opt's): 0.93-0.94 (window 20, no shift, no jitter, best of 33; shifts 0.1-0.2 0.97-1.0).
   Kept, for products, squares and stored leaf forms (cyclic_product).
6. Source order (window 1-8): 3-10% slower than list-scheduled, every kernel.

## Tuned table (`tools/tune.py ntt --target avx512`)

The new candidate (index 3) where it beat intrinsics<Avx512> by more than the 1% margin: forward
2^15-2^17, 2^19; inverse 2^9, 2^16; multiply 2^8, 2^11-2^19; product 2^10, 2^12, 2^13, 2^15-2^17,
2^19. tune.cpp's geomean of time / fastest over its 120 points: 1.0095 with the old table, 1.0028
with the new. Scores against intrinsics_avx512 (time / fastest): multiply 1.010-1.023 at 2^11-2^19,
product 1.010-1.028, forward 2^16 1.058.
