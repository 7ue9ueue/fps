# #104 r1: Transform::inverse_upper

2026-10-12, claude (issue #104, round 1, for backend/evaluation's descent).

- Added `Transform::inverse_upper(h)`: forward_upper undone. h (L / 2 words) holds leaves
  L / 16 .. L / 8 - 1 of p's leaf form; out: p mod (x^(L/2) + 1), canonical, with the inverse's
  scale. Every variant: scalar (`inverse_levels` on group 1, or on groups 2 and 3 and then the
  radix-2 level with r(1)^-1), intrinsics (`inverse_tree` of group 1 and a scale pass under a
  radix-2 top; `inverse_tree` of groups 2 and 3 and the radix-2 level with the scale under a
  radix-4 one), avx2 (`inverse_trees` likewise). select.hpp: `run_inverse_upper`, the inverse's
  candidate at L / 2, as forward_upper takes the forward's.
- Use: the descent of a transposed product tree halves a node's transform to its child's
  length: P_lo - T(X mod (x^(L/2) + 1)) = 2 T(X_hi), one inverse_upper and one forward of L / 2
  per child, against an inverse of L and a forward of L / 2 (backend/evaluation, notes.md).
- Tests: `test_upper.cpp` (new): each variant (scalar, intrinsics on every struct, avx2, tuned)
  against forward_upper and the scalar model at 2^7 .. 2^22 (2^18 under ASan), random words,
  P - 1 and zeros; canonical output; 2^kMinLog throws. Passed on every target's test machine.
- Not measured alone: the scale pass under a radix-2 top could fold into the subtree's top
  group (inverse_group_scaled with kScaledRoots[0][lg]) and a canonical pass.
