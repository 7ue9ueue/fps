## What was built

- Single-group bottoms (2^6, 2^7): a subtree of 4 leaves is its bottom stage alone, so the
  intrinsics ran one group per call there and the pair kernels never ran. With
  `Generated<S>::kJoinBottoms` (neon only: `Ntt::kJoin`), the top levels call the bottom once for
  all their subtrees (2 groups from group 0 under the radix-2 level, 4 under group 0): products,
  squares, in-place multiply, transforms, inverses into other memory, `forward_from`,
  `forward_product`, `cyclic_product`, `inverse_product`, `forward_upper`, `inverse_upper`. Exact:
  those subtrees have no levels above the bottom and no folded scale.
- Generated loops (`TwLoop`: twiddles given as a block, values then quotients 8 words on, so a
  scaled block `scaled_block(s)` or a table entry is the block itself; the asm takes them in
  general registers): the forward top group on canonical inputs (kIn = 1; in place and from other
  memory), the scaled inverse top groups of the subtrees (k > 0: s, s x, s y, s z, x; k = 0: s,
  s z), the inverse top group (scaled or not; in place, into a product's output, and outputs 0-1
  or 2-3 only for cut products and upper halves), the inverse radix-2 level (the same forms: both
  halves, the sums, the differences). From other memory the hooks take any multiple of 8
  (radix-4) or 4 (radix-2) words from 16 on: the last 16 columns run again (they read only the
  source), so a product's boundary columns stay asm; the first levels of products from spans
  (`forward_from` hooks) too.
- `inverse_product` of one term a b (`Bottom::inverse_terms`; exp runs two a step): new kernels
  `bottom_fill_first` and `bottom_fill_both` (the rows from a's canonical leaves, no butterflies,
  interleaved with `bottom_stored_last` of the pair before), into the output. Other term lists
  stay intrinsics.
- `group_loop` drops unused ops (`prune`), for kernels that store fewer outputs.

## Steering probe (`steer.py`, `steer.cpp`)

An asm loop of one repeated pattern (>= 240 instructions per iteration), cycles by
`perf_event_open` (user cycles), median of 7. M = `mul .4s` (V0/V2), A = `add .4s` (any pipe),
U = `umlal .2d` by element, L = `ldr q`, S = `str q`; `@c` an accumulator chain in one register,
`^k` a source k ops back. Cycles per pattern instance (vector ops per cycle):

| pattern | cycles | pattern | cycles |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 0.25 (4.0) | M | 0.50 (2.0) |
| M A | 0.50 (4.0) | M A A | 1.00 (3.0) |
| M M A A | 1.00 (4.0) | M A A A | 1.00 (4.0) |
| M M A | 1.33 (2.25) | M M M A | 2.00 (2.0) |
| M A A A A A | 1.75 (3.4) | M M A A A A | 2.00 (3.0) |
| M x4 A x4 | 2.00 (4.0) | M x8 A x8 | 5.00 (3.2) |
| L A | 0.50 (A at 2/cycle) | L A A A | 1.50 (2.0) |
| L A x6 | 1.50 (4.0) | L L A x8 | 4.00 (2.0) |
| A A A A L | 2.00 (2.0) | A x8 L | 2.00 (4.0) |
| ldr x (scalar) A A A | 0.75 (4.0) | M L A | 1.00 (2.0) |
| L M A M A M A | 1.50 (4.0) | U A U A L U A U A | 2.00 (4.0) |
| U@0 (one chain) | 1.15 | U@0..7 (8 chains) | 4.00 for 8 (2.0) |
| U@0..3 A x4 | 3.00 (2.7) | U@0 A U@1 A .. U@7 A | 4.00 (4.0) |
| M then A, 12 chains of 6 cycles | 7.10 for 24 (3.4) | U then A, 12 chains | 10.9 for 24 (2.2) |

- Order matters even without dependences: period-2 M A runs at 4 a cycle, period 3 (M A A) at 3,
  runs of 3 or more multiplies lose (M M M A: 2 a cycle). No simple positional rule (op index mod
  2 or 4, with or without loads counted, round robin per class or shared) fits all rows.
- Vector loads (not scalar ones) cut runs of A ops to 2 a cycle when they come every 1-4 A ops,
  but not every 6-8, and not at all inside M A alternations (L M A M A M A, U A U A L ...: 4 a
  cycle). Guess: load data and A ops compete for the write ports of the pipes A ops use.
- umlal chains: an accumulator feeds the next umlal in about 1 cycle (U@0: 1.15), but chains
  mixed with adds run slower than the same count of independent multiplies; dependent U -> A -> U
  chains (an accumulator from an add) at 2.2 a cycle against 3.4 for mul -> add.
- On the product bottom (`multi.sh`, 32 groups on L1 data, cycles; main's kernel 5670-5693, the
  intrinsics 7652-7693): the repattern holding the wanted class on a miss 5682, window 128
  5637-5659, MMAA 5637, stores as S 5715; leaf sums in 2 chains per half (`add .2d` to join) 5747,
  4 chains 6101. All within 1% except the split chains (worse): the bottom's order is not what
  the probes can fix. Not kept (the knobs are in the exploration copy of the generator,
  `x151/gen_neon_kernels_explore.py`).
- The bottom's balance: per pair 496 multiplies (V0/V2 only) against 470 other vector ops and 64
  store data uops; the 4-pipe floor (257.5 cycles a pair) needs all but about 19 of the others on
  V1/V3. It runs at 354 a pair (73%); the same stream without dependences at 300 (round 1).
