## What went in

`backend/ntt/truncated.hpp`, a new plan kind (`ragged::kTruncated`) for multiply and square:
the product of N words, L < N < 2L, from the transform of 2L cut to the first rr >= N words of its
leaf form (van der Hoeven's truncated Fourier transform; the inverse is his mixed one).

- Nodes: node k of s words is x^s - r(k)^2, with children 2k, 2k + 1 (halves) or 4k + t
  (quarters); a node whose s / 8 is a power of 4 is the variants' group k. The shape of N (`shape`):
  the root x^(2L) - 1, split as the variants' tree is (radix 2 at the root if 2L / 8 is 2 4^j, then
  radix 4), into its full children (group products) and at most one partial child, recursively.
  rr = N rounded up so the deepest children have at least 128 words (`ragged::rounded`); at most 10
  levels and 3 full nodes a level.
- Group products (`Variant::group_product`, `group_square`; each variant): the variant's own
  subtree of group k (forward, leaf products, inverse; asm where the variant has it), in place,
  raw (times nv 2^-32). intrinsics (and neon, avx512 on it): `multiply_tree`; avx2: `Tree::visit`;
  scalar: its levels and leaves. `variant::tuned` runs the product row's candidate at the group's
  length.
- The levels above them, generic code on `simd::Native` (passes of 2 or 4 chunks, as the variants'
  levels): forward from f into each needed child (the root reads f; a radix-2 root with n <= L
  writes x^L - 1's half alone and the next level reads f too); inverse in two steps per level:
  top-down on the columns past the partial child's prefix, where the node's top words are known
  (the root's: zero, as c has no words past rr; x^L + 1's under a radix-2 root: x^L - 1's words,
  read in place), giving the unknown words and the partial child's known words; bottom-up on the
  rest after the partial child. Unnormalized scales as the variants' inverses (a node's words come
  out times its leaves 2^-32, the same as a group product of its size), so the constants carry
  the scale, a known word's factor mu (a power of 2) and the root's 1 / scale; the root writes
  out (cut to `count` words: cut products write out directly, no copy). Prefetch on Emerald
  Rapids as the variants' passes.
- The plan: kind 4 of "ntt.ragged" (`tune::Table`, measured by `measure_ragged` with the other
  kinds). Middle products and backend/series (`ragged_into` callers) have no truncated form, so an
  entry v >= 4 means "products truncated, the others kind v - 4" (`product_kind`,
  `other_kind`, `encode`); `choose()` stays the others' (never truncated) and `choose_product()`
  is multiply's and square's. `ragged_into` runs a truncated plan too (tables of 2L:
  `table_log`), and its top products use `choose_product`; `ragged::work_words(plan)` sizes any
  plan's work, for a caller outside ntt (series' `low_product` can switch to `choose_product`).
  The truncated choice is out of line (`truncated_choice`), and `split_plan` is round 1's plan.
- `tools/tune.py ntt` on every target; tuned.json keeps main's variant rows and records, only the
  `ragged` field is new (measured at ec29495, merged into main's file).

## Tried and dropped

- The CRT form first: A = c mod (x^L - 1) by the variant's cyclic product of length L, and x^L + 1
  truncated, combined by c = (A + B) / 2 + x^L (A - B) / 2. Mac M4, 2^20, 1.75 L: 0.948 of the plain
  product; its parts: cyclic product 4.82 ms, forwards 0.32, group products 3.37, inverse 0.88 (the
  levels kept every value in true scale, a product per word more, and repeated the top level the
  cyclic product had run). The whole tree with unnormalized scales: 0.907 there (forwards 0.48,
  groups 8.02, inverse 0.55 of 9.07 ms).
- The root's level and the next forward in one pass (the root's partial child never written): 24
  streams, 2^17 words apart; Mac 2^20 1.75 L forwards 0.49 -> 2.26 ms. As avx2's radix-8 notes
  found for 12 streams.
- `cyclic_into` always inline and `truncated_into` out of line (to keep round 1's split path):
  Zen 3's short products 0.9948 -> 0.9923 geomean, Zen 5's 0.9765 -> 0.9962 (2^6-2^9 up to 1.12):
  code layout; not kept.

## Not done; next rounds

- Middle products at (1.5, 2) 2^k still run the plain plan (1.14-1.23 per word at 2^20): the
  transpose of the truncated product (Tellegen) would serve them, and series' Newton steps.
- Series: `low_product` can take `choose_product` with `ragged::work_words`; its Newton steps want
  truncated leaf forms kept across products (a forward to the nodes, leaf products, an inverse),
  the group products split into the variants' forward and inverse trees.
- The top levels: ours cost about what the plain product's do for 2L (Mac 2^20: 1.03-1.20 ms
  against 0.56-0.94); a fused pass with many streams lost (above). Per call: shape() and the
  constants (a division each, about 30 per call) matter below 2^12, where the table keeps round 1.
