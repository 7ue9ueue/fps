# #157 r2: forward_upper from a span in the values form, an inverse through the caller's work

2026-10-18, claude (issue #157, round 2: backend/gcd; this is its ntt part). Base: main ff90ae6,
GCC 16.2.

Two `Transform` overloads on the variants' existing entry points, for backend/gcd, whose stack holds
this thread's region (so `inverse(F, out)` there would map a fresh one per call):

- `forward_upper(std::span<const u32> in, u32* H, Form form)`: `forward_upper(in, H)` (the variants'
  `forward_upper_from`: the first level reads in, zero past it), then for `Form::values` the split
  of `forward_upper(h, Form::values)`. gcd's doubling had copied each entry into H first.
- `inverse(const u32* F, u32* work, std::span<u32> out, Form form)`: the variants' `inverse(from, f,
  out, count)` with the caller's work as f (F may be work): the subtrees copy or join their leaves
  from F into work, the top level writes out. gcd had run the inverse in place and copied out.

On `lc-intel` (avx512 target, inverse_mod at 2^20) memcpy went from 7.0% of the time to 2.1% with
these and gcd's other changes (`backend/gcd/notes/157-r2.md`). Test: `test_values_into.cpp`, both
forms, every variant this machine runs and the tuned one, 2^6 to 2^16: the span form against a
zero-padded copy (sources of 0, 1, L / 4, L / 2 - 1 and L / 2 words, not written), the inverse
against `inverse(F, out, form)` (out of 0, 1, 7, L / 2, L - 1 and L words, F kept, F = work),
past the length it throws.
