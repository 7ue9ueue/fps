## 1. The forward's two lowest levels as loops (avx2)

The cause of #112 r1's "forward from a span 4-9% slower than lc-opt's": below the top level,
`Transform::forward` ran one asm call per group at h = 4 and `forward_leaves` (two groups per
statement) at h = 1, where lc-opt's transform layer runs `forward_h4` (h = 4 as one loop over a
tile's groups, twiddle addresses from the group index) and `forward_bottom` (h = 1, eight groups per
iteration). `gen_avx2_kernels.py` now generates both (`forward_h4`, `forward_leaves_loop`; lc-opt's
`lib/poly/gen_kernels.py` bodies and knobs, output identical to its `kernels.hpp`); avx2's
`forward_levels` (the tile's levels, used by `forward_trees`, `forward_tiles` and `cycle_trees`)
and `forward_bottom` (runs of 8k groups) call them. `probe_levels.cpp`, forward in place, us
(median of 11; two runs agreed within 1%):

The inverse's counterparts (lc-opt's `inverse_h4`, `inverse_bottom`) changed nothing (lg 17-21:
0.997-1.000 of ours, which beats lc-opt's in-place inverse by 3-5%); not kept.

## 2. Sources in the lower half at radix-2 lengths (avx2)

`forward(in, F)`, `forward_product` and `cyclic_product` with a source of at most L/2 words ran a
radix-2 top level, which for such a source copies it into both halves, then each half's first
radix-4 level: two passes over the transform. One pass, the radix-8 top from the source (12
streams 2^lg / 8 words apart), lost before (#112 r1, attempt 2; lc-opt: up to 10x slower at some
offsets). Now `forward_radix8_lower_from`: the radix-8 top in two passes, group 1's outputs (the
upper half) then group 0's, each reading the source's 4 blocks and writing 4 (8 streams), then
the 8 subtrees; `cyclic_product` then ends with the radix-8 inverse top (`inverse_radix8_out`,
as `inverse_product`). From 2^8 on. `probe_fwd.cpp`, `forward(span of L/2 words, F)`, us over 10
placements of F against the source (mod 64 KiB), before the loops of 1.:

| lg | radix-2 top | radix 8, one pass | radix 8, two passes | the same, depth first | lc-opt in place (top8) | lc-opt out of place |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 18 | 286-308 | 282-360 | 270-288 (330 at -4 KiB) | 271-322 | 268-282 | 279-301 |
| 20 | 1296-1313 | 1222-1291 | 1248-1264 | 1245-1254 | 1204-1209 | 1249-1260 |

(lc-opt in place includes a copy of the source, 13-28 / 54-59 us.) Radix-4 lengths (lg 17, 19,
21) read the source in their first radix-4 level already; fps 128 / 587 / 2625-2649 against lc-opt
out of place 125-129 / 565-568 / 2551-2576 there, before 1.

Tried, not kept: `forward_upper(in, H)` under a radix-2 level reading the source in group 1's
first level (the same two-pass part, writing H) instead of copying it in first (as lc-opt does):
exp / lc-opt at 2^17, 500000, 2^20, 2^21 0.957-0.961 / 0.976-0.978 / 0.983-0.991 / 0.982-0.984
against 0.961-0.963 / 0.979-0.981 / 0.984-0.986 / 0.976-0.977 with the copy (two runs each); pow
the same way. The copy and the asm group in place win at 2^20.

`forward(in, F)` in place (new): in at F, at most L/2 words, F's upper half not read (the
division's T(h) and T(q_j), backend/series). Every variant handles it (intrinsics' first levels
read before they write; scalar skips its copy); avx2 runs lc-opt's one-pass sparse radix 8
(`forward_radix8_sparse`, after zeroing F[n, L/2)) at radix-2 lengths from 2^8.

## 3. toeplitz_product (lc-opt's Toeplitz sharing)

`Transform::toeplitz_product(A1, A2, A3, B0, B1, work, out, o)`: out = coefficients of A2 B0 + A1
B1 (as `inverse_product`), and A3 = A3 B0 + A2 B1 (exact, a leaf form as any other), by three leaf
products: m1 = A2 (B0 + B1), m2 = (A1 - A2) B1, m3 = (A3 - A2) B0. log's division in 4 blocks
calls it for block 2 and then `inverse_product({A1, B2}, {A3, alone})` for block 3: 4 leaf
products for blocks 2 and 3, not 5.

- avx2: generated bottoms (`gen_avx2_product_kernels.py`): `bottom_toeplitz_*` (rows of S = B0 +
  B1, D1 = A1 - A2, D3 = A3 - A2 from the five leaf forms; m1 + m2 reduced and added; m1 + m3 from
  its 64-bit sums, < 16 P^2, as hi (2^32 mod P) + lo, canonical) and `bottom_side_*` (a product
  of leaves plus a leaf form alone, added to the 64-bit sums before the Montgomery reduction, so it
  takes the products' 2^-32 at no cost): `inverse_product` of one product and one term alone runs
  it (before: the intrinsics' bottom). In 'both' the current batches' pointers are the next ones'
  minus 256 bytes (13 asm operands; 17 do not fit). `vpsllq` added to the generator.
- intrinsics: `Bottom::inverse_toeplitz` (S, D1, D3 into a stack block, then fill_rows) and
  `WideBottom::inverse_toeplitz` (zmm); scalar: the four products (model).
- Scheduling: the toeplitz 'both' kernel deadlocks the scheduler from window 5 at every shift
  tried; with m3's sums first, then m1's, then m1 + m3, then m2 (at most 4 sums live) windows
  2-4 schedule at some shifts: (2, -0.1), (3, -0.2 / -0.05 / 0.05), (4, -0.2 / 0.05). All six
  within noise (2^20: 3985-4027 us); kept (4, 0.05).

`probe_toep.cpp`, us (division layout, upper half out), three runs:

| lg | toeplitz_product | product + alone | 1 product | 2 products | 3 products |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 16 | 228-236 | 111 | 107 | 156-159 | 220-232 |
| 18 | 1059-1148 | 553-579 | 537-552 | 744-798 | 1017-1090 |
| 20 | 3998-4088 | 2083-2132 | 2030-2050 | 2832-2899 | 3908-4153 |

So blocks 2 and 3 take 0.89-0.91 of the time of the 2- and 3-product sums they replace. Tried,
not kept:
- m1 + m3 in Montgomery form (lc-opt's: its side bottom adds it after the reduction), timed with
  wrong values: 0.95 of the exact form (2^20: 3776-3846 us). Exact keeps A3 a plain leaf form; a
  side in Montgomery form needs a term kind of its own in every variant's inverse_terms.
- 'first' and 'last' as two statements instead of 'both': no change (4004-4026 at 2^20).
- The intrinsics' `Bottom<Avx2>::inverse_toeplitz` on avx2: 4017-4023 at 2^20, as the asm.
- lc-opt's ToeplitzBottom is 3738 us at 2^20 in log (its phases), 0.93 of ours, and faster than
  our 3-product sum (3908-4153): our generated 3-product bottoms schedule in windows of at most 4
  (register budget). Not found.
