## What was built

- `inverse_product` of several terms (`Bottom::inverse_terms`; the intrinsics ran every list but one
  term a b): up to 4 product terms (a b) and one plain term (a times 2^-32), in passes over the
  subtree's groups, one per product term, each the rows of a's canonical leaves (`bottom_fill_first`
  / the first half of `_both`, as round 2's one-term kernels) and the leaf products by b's leaves:
  `bottom_terms` (first pass: into out), `_plain` (plus the plain term), `_add` (plus out),
  `_add_inverse` (plus out, then the inverse butterflies), `_plain_inverse` (one product and a plain
  term in one pass: series' inverse half step). A pass costs one load and one store of out per
  vector more than sums in registers would, but keeps 10 kernels instead of one per list (code size).
  Lists without a product term or with two plain terms stay intrinsics. The plain term times 2^-32
  is a Montgomery step on the word (`montgomery`: P - umulh(x P^-1, P), in (0, P]; with -P^-1
  umulh(x (-P^-1), P) + [x != 0]).
- The leaf products' reduction per kernel (`Kernel.reduce`): the sums s = H 2^32 + L < 8 P^2 as
  Montgomery's (s + m P) / 2^32, m = -L P^-1 (`umlal` of m P into both halves; 3 multiplies and 4
  other ops per 4 words), or as y = H - umulh(L P^-1, P) in (-P, 1.86P), then min(y + P, y - P) <
  2P (2 multiplies and 6 other ops; v31 holds P^-1 instead of -P^-1). The second for the product bottoms
  (`bottom_first/last/both`) and the passes above, the first kept for squares, products by stored
  leaves and the one-term `inverse_product` (measured both ways, below).

## Attempts

Kernels on L1 data unless said; cycles at the clock of a dependent add chain, medians of 9 rounds.

1. **umlal links back to back** (round 2's lead: a multiply between two links of an accumulator
   chain broke the fast accumulation in the steering probe). `Chains` in the scheduler and the
   repattern (`x151/gen_neon_kernels_chains.py`): once a leaf product's first link issues, the
   multiplies that follow are links of the open leaf products (at most `chains`) until their sums
   end, other ops between them freely; 220 of 224 sum links back to back with `chains` = 1 (93 of
   256 before). Product bottom, 32 groups: main 5696; chains 1 6055, 2 5797, 4 5796; chains 1
   without the pattern 6144, with window none 6308; intrinsics 7687. Worse: forcing the order costs
   the M/A alternation and the live set more than the fast accumulation gives back (in the bottom
   other work hides the chain latency). Dropped.
2. **The 'sub' reduction**, product bottom 32 groups: 5694 -> 5604 (0.984; 5604-5648 over window
   variants). Same build of every kernel with it (several builds in turn, `ab.sh`, below): products
   0.991-0.994 at 2^10-2^16, 0.999 at 2^20, f * g 0.986-0.995; but squares 1.014-1.027 and the
   stored bottoms (series' cyclic products) 1.01-1.02 slower (inv/14 1.022, sqrt/14 1.018). Kept per
   kernel: products and the sums of terms (2-3% better with it than without: ip2 0.912 against
   0.935 at 2^10), not squares nor stored leaves.
3. **Software prefetch in the group loops** (`prfm` one line per stream and iteration, 256 to 4096
   bytes ahead, load or store hints; one group on quarters of 2^12 to 2^18 words, in place):
   never faster. q = 2^18 (4 MiB): 0.2043 ns per word without, 0.2059 at 1024 bytes ahead (best);
   inverse 0.2126 against 0.2151. The hardware prefetcher follows the 4 streams; the groups past
   the subtrees are not memory-bound on V2 by this measure (their larger share in transforms at
   2^20 is not explained here).
4. **Groups of quarters of 32 words** (the level above the bottom, 2 loop iterations per call; a
   one-loop-per-level kernel as #152's `small_groups` was the idea): a level of 2048 words costs 1366
   cycles at q = 32, 1342 at 128, 1336 at 512 (forward; inverse 1422, 1406, 1408): 2% to win at
   most. Not built.
5. **Transform bottoms of 4 groups per statement** (Zen 5 gained from 1 to 4 pairs): 64 groups,
   forward 1851 (pairs, main) against 1897 (quads), inverse 1399 against 1422; intrinsics 2264 and
   1472. Dropped.
6. **Knobs of the stored bottom** (`multi2.py stored`: window 96/128/200/none, repattern window 16/64,
   age, latency scale, merge shift): 4764-5202 cycles against 4782 for main's, all within 0.4% or
   worse. 'sub' there: 5009 (1.047). Not changed.
7. Not tried: the sparse forward radix-4 top (0.9-2.4% of sqrt/exp at 2^11 in `perf record`, a
   radix-2-like pass the asm did not speed up in round 2), fused sums of two terms in registers
   (exp's last step: ip2 is 1.69 of ip1 with the passes; a fused kernel would save about 5% of it).

## Tests

pppx, and x, xx, pxx, which fall back) on 2 to 64 groups from first groups 0, count and random,

in `bottom_terms_add_both` 16 bytes off (108). All ntt tests at -O2 and ASan/UBSan, and series,
division, composition and frontend at -O2, pass on `lc-arm`; ntt on the Mac (arm without SVE2:
the intrinsics); `kernel.py check`. The other targets compile none of the changed code
(neon.hpp, neon_kernels.hpp, and test_neon.cpp's arm part).

## Open

- The product bottom runs at about 72% of its 4-pipe floor; neither the chain order (attempt 1) nor
  round 2's orders closed it. What V2's issue logic wants of a mixed M/A stream with dependences is
  still not modeled.
- The groups past the subtrees take 36-38% of transforms at 2^20 (in-subtree levels 19-20% for 3
  levels against 4), yet one such group alone runs at in-cache speed (attempt 3). Not found why.
- Sums of two product terms in one pass (registers), if exp's last step or division's windows need
  more.
