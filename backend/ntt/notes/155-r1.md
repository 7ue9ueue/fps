## Method (ragged.hpp)

A product c = f g of N = n + m - 1 words, L < N <= 2L, L = 2^lg, r = N - L. With A = c mod (x^L -
1), a cyclic product of length L, and q = c div x^L (the top r words): c = A + (x^L - 1) q, so
c[k] = A[k] - q[k] for k < r and c[L + k] = q[k]. q comes from:

- a twisted piece of s words (a power of two, max(64, L/32) <= s <= L/2): X = q mod (x^s - z^s)
  = (C - A mod (x^s - z^s)) / (z^L - 1), C = c mod (x^s - z^s). x = z y turns x^s - z^s into
  y^s - 1, so C is the existing cyclic product of length s on f(z y), g(z y) mod (y^s - 1).
  z = 3 generates the group, so z^L != 1 for every length (roots of unity would need order 2L,
  which P - 1 = 2^23 * 7 * 17 lacks past 2^22, and gain nothing here);
- the top product: T = the top t = r - s words of c depend only on the top t words of f and g, so
  T is the top of their product (at most 2t - 1 words), itself a ragged product, planned again;
- q = X + (x^s - z^s) T (t <= s); with s = 0, q = T; with t = 0, q = X.

Plans (kinds): plain (the old product at 2L), top (s = 0, t = r), floor (s = bit_floor(r), t =
r - s), ceil (s = bit_ceil(r), t = 0); only splits with s + 2t <= L (else their products are no
shorter than 2L), and a kind that is no split at some N falls back to the next (ceil, floor, top,
plain). Every variant's existing cyclic product of length L (`Variant::multiply(Product)` with n,
m <= L and n + m - 1 > L, as middle_product already used it) runs unchanged; the new code is the
passes on `simd::Native`: twist_fold (one pass per factor: chunks folded with constants w^c, one
Montgomery product by z^k per word), combine (A folded per chunk, untwist, q into place), fold
(a factor longer than L).

Middle products (`middle_product`, out[i] = (f g)[m - 1 + i] from n' = m - 1 + count words of f)
run the transpose (Tellegen's principle) of the split of u -> u G, G = g reversed: split_transposed
(of combine) gives Abar, Cbar, Tbar from f; then out[i] = (Abar g x^-(m-1) mod (x^L - 1))[i mod L]
(the old middle product's staging), plus z^i Z[(i + m - 1) mod s] with Z = Cbar (z^(m-1) g(y/z) mod
(y^s - 1)), plus the middle product of [0, .., 0, Tbar] and g[0, mt) into the top words.

Sources, read for the ideas (no code): J. van der Hoeven, "The truncated Fourier transform and
applications", ISSAC 2004 (transforms at N points of a 2L transform); D. Harvey, "A cache-friendly
truncated FFT", Theor. Comp. Sci. 410 (2009); D. J. Bernstein, "Multidigit multiplication for
mathematicians" (2001), and "The tangent FFT" (2007) for twisting x^s - w into y^s - 1; the CRT /
Newton-form recombination is the textbook one (von zur Gathen, Gerhard, "Modern Computer Algebra",
ch. 5). Middle products by transposition: G. Hanrot, M. Quercia, P. Zimmermann, "The middle product
algorithm I" (AAECC 2004); A. Bostan, G. Lecerf, E. Schost, "Tellegen's principle into practice"
(ISSAC 2003). lc-opt has no non-power-of-two path (every product length is `bit_width(n + m - 2)`;
lib/ntt, lib/poly), so there is no lc-opt bar here.

## The plan: a tuned choice ("ntt.ragged")

`kinds`, a `tune::Table` of one kind per main length 2^lg (6 <= lg <= 24) and bucket of r / L
((b - 1)/16, b/16]; `choose(N)` is one table read. `measure_ragged` (tune.hpp) times every distinct
plan at each bucket's middle, factors of N/2 + 1 and N - N/2 words through `detail::product`, rows
short to long (each row is set before the next, so top products run measured rows). tune.cpp:
every row on the timed targets, to 2^14 on scalar and sse; 5 samples of >= 1 ms, margin 1%
(Zen 3 76 s, Zen 5 74 s, Neoverse V2 108 s in all, with the variant rows). Run time
(`kRaggedRunTime`): rows to 2^10, 3 samples of 30 us. The compiled tables (2026-10-15, commit
5c89400), rows 2^16-2^24 on every timed machine, buckets 1-16: top top floor|top ceil floor floor
ceil ceil floor floor floor|plain plain ... (bucket 11 floor on most rows; 12-16 plain). Short rows
differ per machine: Zen 5 plain to 2^7 and from bucket 3 at 2^8; Zen 3 and Emerald Rapids the top
product alone through buckets 4-8 at 2^7-2^10. tuned.json keeps main's variant rows and records:
the tuning runs also moved up to 15 variant entries on Emerald Rapids and Zen 5 (within the 1%
margin's noise; the full records are `tuned-full-*.json` in the exploration folder); only the new
field `ragged` is from 5c89400. After #203 (GCC 16.2) a rerun of `tools/tune.py ntt` measures it
again with the rest.

The first table (8fea1b4) kept the compiled default's kind where its plan was within the margin,
and stored that kind; at Zen 3's 2^8 bucket 4 the default "floor" fell back to the top product at
the bucket's middle (r = 56: a piece of 32 words is below 2^kMinLog) but ran a twisted piece of 64
words at r = 64 (`product/8.25`, N = 320): 1.57x slower than the old product on Zen 3, 1.83x on
Emerald Rapids. measure_ragged now stores the least kind with the winning plan (top there): 0.84x
and 0.83x.

Before the table, a cost model (product time ~ L (lg + 4), 0.3 ns per pass word) picked plans up to
1.32x slower than the best (`options.cpp` on lc-bench: top alone at r = 3L/8, where floor is 0.81
of plain against 1.07): replaced by the measured table.
