## What changed

`intrinsics::Values` and `WideValues` (intrinsics.hpp), the lane table (common.hpp), the inverse
scale after a join (intrinsics.hpp, avx2.hpp), the scalar model (scalar.hpp). The words of the
values form are main's (canonical, so the same residues give the same words).

- A(g / 8) = 1 for blocks g < 8 (common.hpp: A(h) = r(256 h)): those blocks skip the 7 broadcast
  products of the twist each way (`each_block`: kA false below block 8). All of L <= 2^9, and the
  first 8 blocks of any transform (gcd and bostan_mori run many short transforms).
- The 8-point transform with fewer reductions: 84 adds, subtracts and mins per set, not 100 (the
  inputs taken < 2P, each level's outputs reduced to what the next needs; 5 of main's `low` were on
  values already below 2P).
- The lane table's rows are quotients then values, so on x86 the odd lanes' quotients of a per-lane
  Shoup product are a load 4 bytes on (inside the row's line) instead of a shift of q: 7 shifts
  fewer per set and direction.
- The join leaves the 8-point transform's gain 8 in; the inverse after it scales by
  `values_scale(lg)` = 2^-lg instead of `leaf_scale(lg)` (the folded scale tables gain
  `kScaledRoots[2]`): its k = 0 product is gone, 19 products per set, not 20. The join's leaves are
  < 2P: every bottom level of the intrinsics (and their generated asm) takes < 2P; the avx2
  variant asks for canonical leaves (`join<true>`: its asm `inverse_leaves` takes canonical ones).
- Instruction-level parallelism: the work of several sets goes step by step through the same
  source loops (`twist`, `forward8`, `inverse8` on N sets): two pairs of blocks per step on 16
  lanes (`WideValues`), two blocks on 8 lanes with 32 registers (`Values<Avx512Vl>`), and on Neon
  (4 lanes, 2 sets per block) both sets of a block; Avx2 keeps one block per step (attempt 6).
  GCC on x86 schedules in source order, and one block's chain of 2 products per twist and the 3
  levels did not fill the machines.
- Leaf products (item 1) on Zen 4 and 5: `WideBottom::products`, two groups per zmm (below).
- `WideValues` reads its lane factors from a pair table (`kPairs`, 12 KiB: per pair of blocks and
  k, 16 quotients, the odd lanes' quotients, 16 values), one zmm load each, not `load_halves` of
  the narrow rows (a vinserti64x4 each).

The zmm form: WideBottom's pair layout (lane 8h + i: word i of quarter t of group k + h), the
rows filled from the leaves by load_halves, `leaf_products_at` (Montgomery, as the fused bottoms),
then 2^32 by one Shoup product; two pairs of groups in lockstep. Kept on Zen 4 and 5 only
(`kWideProducts`): `WideBottom::products`, the words of `Bottom::products`. Interleaving the leaves
of `Bottom` itself lost everywhere (GCC kept the chains apart already; more live registers).

Past the caches it lost: Zen 5 `leaf_product/22` 1.44x and `/23` 2.53x main's in the bench
(`Bottom` stays at 0.61 ns per word there: it is not memory bound). Filling the next step's rows
before this step's products (as `Bottom` does, against loads that wait behind stores of out) did
not change it (lbench at 2^22: 1.17 ns per word against 0.61; 2^20 0.84x, 2^10-2^14 0.65x). Not
understood; the wide form runs to 2^20 (`kWideProductsMaxLog`).

## Leads

- The bottom level before the split could leave its outputs < 4P (the split's products take any
  input; only x[0] needs one `low`): 30 of about 290 ops per block fewer, but the bottom levels are
  generated asm on every timed target (avx2 `forward_leaves`, the zmm and Neon bottoms), so a lazy
  copy of each, and twice the bottom code where both forms run (Zen 5's op cache).
- `forward_upper(h, Form::values)` runs the split as a pass of its own (#103): fused in the tiles,
  as `forward_values`, it would save a pass over the half where it is beyond L2.
- Two pairs per step cost Emerald Rapids at 2^7-2^8 (insitu.cpp: split 23-26 ns per block against
  20-22 with one pair, the same body at 2^7): the step's code, not its work. Fewer instantiated
  bodies (no two-pair step below block 8, where A = 1) may get both.
- The zmm leaf products past the caches (above).
