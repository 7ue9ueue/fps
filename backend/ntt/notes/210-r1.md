The levels below the top are as fast as lc-opt's or faster (the tiles' sizes do not matter); the
whole gap is the top level from another array, which moves twice the lines: P's half that is not
zero read, all of FP fetched for writing and written back, against a read and a write of one
array in place (at 2^20: 186 against 149 us, 1.25x, the ratio of the lines moved). With FP 2 KiB
or 2.5 KiB past p instead of 320 bytes the top level took 25.5-26.9 us at 2^17 (27.8-36.4) and
187.7-201.8 at 2^20: a little 4K aliasing at 2^17, bandwidth at 2^20. In place the whole forward
beats lc-opt's at every level at 2^20 and matches it at 2^17.

So compose now transforms P^ in place in p (backend/composition, `compose_levels`): the
product hook of the inverse at 4m reads p, and every variant's inverse_hooked writes out (p) in
its top level, after the last hook call; `Transform::forward_hooked` and `inverse_hooked` now
document both (ntt.hpp). intrinsics' `forward_hooked` read a source of at most half the length
in both subtrees' first groups after the lower subtree had overwritten it in place: in place it
now takes the radix-2 level (`in != f`). In compose's call sequence (`phases.cpp`, m = 2^20, 9
runs interleaved; lc-opt's `phases_lcopt.cpp` in the same session), the forwards of P^ summed
over the levels, us:

## The loops (the issue's task)

#87 r2 had put lc-opt's `forward_h4` and `forward_leaves_loop` in the forward tiles, and #149 r3
`forward_h4` in the pruned ones; lc-opt's `inverse_h4` and `inverse_bottom` had changed nothing in
the plain inverse (0.997-1.000). A new reason to look again: on a 256-vector tile in L1 the
inverse loops do win, and the products' tiles (`Tree::tile`) still called a kernel per group at
h = 4 (`forward_pair` for a and b, `inverse`). `levels.cpp`, rdtsc ticks per vector, median of 31
rounds (min-max within 2-5%):

| level | a call per group | loop |
|---|---:|---:|
| forward h = 4 (done, #87) | 3.97 | 3.43 (`forward_h4`) |
| forward h = 4, a and b | 7.04 (`forward_pair`) | 6.59 (`forward_h4_pair`), 6.82 (`forward_h4` twice) |
| inverse h = 4 | 3.54-3.74 (two runs) | 3.44 (`inverse_h4`, lc-opt's) |
| forward h = 1 (done, #87) | 5.18 (`forward_leaves`) | 4.54 |
| inverse h = 1 | 3.55 (`inverse_leaves`) | 3.13 (`inverse_leaves_loop`) |

- `inverse_h4`: lc-opt's (the generator's output equals its `kernels.hpp`, instruction for
  instruction). `inverse_leaves_loop`: 8 groups per iteration with `inverse_leaves`' butterfly on
  canonical leaves (no reductions of the sums); lc-opt's body (inputs < 2P) 3.36. Its knobs:
  windows 16, 24, 32, 36 (margin 1): 3.27, 3.21, 3.13, 3.14, margin 0 at 32: 3.17; 40 and up
  deadlock the scheduler. Kept 32, margin 1.
- `forward_h4_pair`: `forward_h4`'s body on a and b, a group of each per iteration, the
  butterflies of a and b interleaved (both arrays' in sequence 6.74; window 12 6.66, 24
  deadlocks). Kept window 16 (`H4_KNOBS`).
- Used in: `inverse_bottom` (runs of 8k groups), `inverse_levels` (`inverse_trees`,
  `inverse_tiles`, `cycle_trees`, `inverse_trees_with`, `Tree::tile`), `inverse_pruned_with` (h =
  4 is below `kPruneInverse`: whole groups), `Tree::tile`'s forward (`forward_h4_pair`, or
  `forward_h4` for a square).

## Found

- Short ops move with code layout: product/6 1.09 and inverse/7 1.14 of main's time with the same
  instructions on their paths (`Tree<PairBottom>::tile<4>` is the same 9 instructions, a jump to
  `PairBottom::run`; inverse/7 runs no loop: 4 groups at h = 1), while `product<PairBottom>`
  grew from 651 to 786 instructions (GCC inlined `visit` into it once more) and the functions
  moved. As #93 and #152 found.
- Power projection's forward of P at 4m reads p (2m words) into FP (`projection_levels`): the
  same doubled traffic in its top level, but in place needs p to have 4m words (lc-opt's p is a
  4m span). Compose's forward of Q at 4m reads q (3m) into levels[s] likewise.
