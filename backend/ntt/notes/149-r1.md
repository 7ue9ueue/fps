## The ops (ntt.hpp)

- `forward_hooked(in, F, hook, zero)`: `forward(in, F)`, and `hook(leaves, first, count)` on each
  run of leaves after its bottom level, in cache; the hook may read and change them.
- `inverse_hooked(hook, work, out, o, unused)`: the hook writes each run of leaves (a leaf form
  times 2^-32, as Montgomery sums leave it) before its bottom level; then the inverse levels, the
  top level writing out as `inverse_product` does (`Output`: lower or upper half, negated). With
  a no-op hook and work = a stored leaf form, an inverse in place of it into out.
- Runs: avx2 the tile runs (up to 256 leaves, at least 8); intrinsics the subtrees (up to 256
  leaves; below 2^8, where subtrees have 4 leaves, one run of all the leaves: the hook before or
  after a whole transform); scalar one run of all.
- Pruning: `zero` (forward) is a column bit of the index, zero in the input; `unused` (inverse)
  one whose outputs the caller ignores. The groups whose butterflies are above the bit run only on
  the columns j with (j & bit) == 0; the level holding the bit and those below are whole. Groups
  with quarters below 32 vectors (forward) or 8 (inverse) run whole (lc-opt's limits). The top
  level is pruned too (common.hpp `PrunedLevel`, a wrapper of the `first_level`/`last_level` runs):
  the forward's writes zeros to the skipped columns (the level holding the bit reads them), the
  inverse's leaves them unwritten. Not pruned: intrinsics' subtrees whose first group reads the
  caller's source (radix-2 top, source of at most half the length), scaled top groups (pruned
  inverses scale in the top level).
- avx2: `kernels::forward_columns`, `inverse_columns` (gen_avx2_kernels.py): the forward() and
  inverse() loops' scheduled bodies on chunks of `width` columns, each followed by `width` skipped
  (as lc-opt's gen_kernels.py `columns_function`); k = 0 runs the general loop (x = y = 1).
  intrinsics: `Butterflies::forward_group_columns`, `inverse_group_columns` (each2 per chunk; chunks
  of at least 2 S::lanes words).
- Dispatch: the forward's tuned choice (`run_forward_hooked`), the inverse's (`run_inverse_hooked`).

## Tests (`test_hooked.cpp`)

Every variant and the tuned one, lg 6 .. 20 (17 under ASan; scalar to 13): forward_hooked against
the scalar forward of a zero-padded copy with every column bit 0, 1, 2 .. L/4 zero in the source
(sources of L, L/2 + 3, L/2 and random words); the hook sees each leaf once, in runs of a power of
two of at least 4 leaves starting at a multiple of the run, at F + 8 first, with the finished
leaves, and its changes stay. inverse_hooked from random leaves against the scalar inverse times
2^32, every unused bit, both halves, negated, counts L - offset, L/2 and 5, words past out
untouched; the argument checks. Breaking the source's zeros (or reading the unused outputs) fails
the pruned cases (checked once by hand).

## Measurements (`lc-bench`, Zen 3, GCC 15.2, -O2 -march=x86-64-v3)

Cost of the levels pruning saves, at the composition's lengths (m = 2^17; `issue149/top.cpp`), us:

| transform | whole | its top level |
|---|---:|---:|
| forward 2^19 from 2^18 + 64 words | 598 | 54 (radix 4) |
| forward 2^18 from 2^17 + 64 words | 295 | 31 (radix 2) |
| inverse 2^19, upper half out | 564 | 66 |
| inverse 2^18, all out | 305 | 91 (radix 8: two levels) |

perf on `lc-intel` (compose at n = 2^17, avx2 build): the top levels (`forward_radix4_part`,
`inverse_top4_upper_to`, `inverse_top_to<8>`, ...) took 16% of compose before the top level was
pruned; pruning it took compose 33.2 -> 32.1 ms on `lc-bench` (same run as lc-opt's 29.6).

Per step at m = 2^17, level s = 8 (L = 512; `issue149/parts.cpp`), us: forward of 2^19 601 ->
515 pruned (levels above the bit; before the top level was pruned), inverse of 2^18 306 -> 256,
forward of 2^18 303 -> 249, inverse of 2^19 with the composition's product hook 897 -> 807. At
s = 2 (bit 2^15) nothing but the top level is above the bit: no change before the top was pruned.

The hooks themselves cost what the passes they replace cost when run apart (compute-bound leaf
sums: Graeffe 265 us in a hook or alone, the compose product 333): the gain is the passes over
memory they remove and half the products of the old spread-then-multiply (backend/composition).

Small lengths on Zen 5 (`issue149/ops.cpp`, ns): at 2^7 the intrinsics' subtrees have 4 leaves, so
the composition's hooks ran one leaf pair at a time: `inverse_hooked` with its product hook 258
against `inverse_product`'s 153, `forward_hooked` with the Graeffe 279. One run of all the leaves
below 2^8: compose at n = 128 on Zen 5 7.2 -> 5.9 us (base 6.4).
