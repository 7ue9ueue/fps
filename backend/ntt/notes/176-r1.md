## What was built

- `Generated<Zmm>::kJoinBottoms` (avx512.hpp): at 2^6 and 2^7 every subtree under the top level is
  one group, and `intrinsics<S>` ran each bottom with `count = 1`, so `Leaf` (ymm, one group at a
  time) ran there and none of the pair kernels did. With the member, `Ntt::kJoin` (#151 r2's hook,
  unchanged) runs all subtrees' bottoms in one call: the generated product bottoms
  (`multiply_first/last/both`, `square_*`, `stored_*`), the transforms' bottom level
  (`bottom_forward2/4`, `bottom_inverse2/4` on Zen 5, `WideBottom` otherwise), WideBottom's
  `inverse_terms`.
- `Ntt::inverse_into` (intrinsics.hpp; the inverse into other memory and of the values form):
  its joined lengths (2^6, 2^7) dispatched at its top to `inverse_into_joined`, a function of its
  own, so its code for the other lengths is the code without joins (Layout, below). The only change
  to intrinsics.hpp; it reaches arm too (neon joins since #151 r2).
- Bottom level in statements of 2 and 4 pairs on Emerald Rapids too (round 150 tried one pair per
  statement only: 1.00-1.01 forward, 1.06-1.10 inverse): `bottom_forward2` (Emerald Rapids model,
  window 128), `bottom_forward4` (Zen 5 model, window 128: the Emerald Rapids scheduler, with no
  feasibility check, deadlocks on 4 pairs), `bottom_inverse4` (Zen 5 model, window 64);
  `Generated<Zmm>::bottom_forward` and `bottom_inverse` now on both targets (the inverse on Emerald
  Rapids: 4 pairs from a multiple of 8, any other run to `WideBottom` whole).
- Zen 5's 4-pair bottoms rescheduled with a window of 128 (round 152 tried none, 32, 64), and its
  2-pair forward on the Emerald Rapids model (`PAIR_KNOBS` in gen_avx512_kernels.py).
- `test_join.cpp` (new): every op that joins (transforms in place and in the values form,
  forward_upper and inverse_upper, inverses into other memory, Convolution products sparse and
  dense, products, squares and middle products from spans, forward from spans, forward_product,
  forward_upper from spans, cyclic products of both halves and negated, inverse_product of 1 to 3
  terms, forward_hooked and inverse_hooked) at 2^6, 2^7 and 2^8 against the scalar variant, word
  for word, on every variant and the tuned one, inputs random, all P - 1, zeros, mixed. Four
  mutations of the joins (forward's bottom from group 2, forward_upper's from group 0, the radix-4
  multiply's on 2 groups, inverse_hooked's stage on half the leaves) failed 6-15 checks each.
  `test_zen5.cpp`'s bottom tests (2 to 64 groups from first groups 0, 2, 6, 64, 1000, against
  `Ntt<Avx512>`) now run asm on the avx512 target too.
