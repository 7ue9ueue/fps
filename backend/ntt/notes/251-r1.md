## What went in

- `Transform::forward(in, F, Form form)`: Form::values from a span, by `forward_hooked` with a hook
  that splits each run of leaves after its bottom level (`ValuesFor<Native>::split`, the words of
  `forward(f, Form::values)`). F apart from in (the hooked forward has no in-place form).
- `Transform::to_values(F, V)`: the split as a pass, leaf form to values form (series' Q_0 from H).
- `Transform::inverse_product(terms, work, out, o, Form::values)`: terms of values forms (a b per
  word, or a alone); `inverse_hooked` with a hook that sums the terms' Montgomery products
  (`ValuesTerms<Native>`, lazy below 2P, one reduction to canonical) into the run and joins it
  (`join<true>`: canonical leaves, as every variant's hooked bottoms take). The leaves then carry
  2^-32 and the join's gain 8: a new output bit `kJoined` (common.hpp) makes each variant's hooked
  top level scale by `values_product_scale(lg)` = 2^(32 - lg) instead of `product_scale(lg)`
  (`hooked_scale`, used by avx2's `output_scale`, intrinsics' `product_top_out_pruned`, scalar's
  `inverse_hooked`; the same constants as before without the bit).

A values-form forward costs 0.19 (Zen 5), 0.34-0.41 (Zen 3, Emerald Rapids) and 0.64 (Neoverse
V2) more than a leaf-form one; a further term of an inverse_product 0.05-0.15 (Zen 5), 0.25-0.3

alone already costs less in the values form (the join and one product per word against an 8 x 8

division with 5 blocks (10 products in its residuals) on every target, 4 blocks on Zen 5 only.
