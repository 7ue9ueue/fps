## What changed

- avx2, the fused ops at even lg (`cyclic_product`, `inverse_staged`: `inverse_product` and
  `toeplitz_product`, `inverse_hooked`): `split_top(lg, words, out)` picks the top level per call.
  From 2^14, at 2^18 always and elsewhere when out lies less than 640 bytes past the work array
  or less than 448 before it (modulo 4 KiB), each half's last radix-4 group runs in place after
  its 4 subtrees and the radix-2 level writes out (`scale_radix2_out`, `scale_radix2_upper_out`;
  pruned for the hooked op); else the radix-8 top as before. The cyclic product keeps its radix-8
  forward top (`radix8_lower`).
- `detail::place` (products from spans, `middle_product`, the ragged plans, `inverse(F, out)`,
  series' short products): a at the middle of the widest gap between the page offsets of f, out
  and g - span, not the first offset 256 bytes from each; modulo 2 KiB at 2^11-2^12
  (`place_period`: the top level's blocks are 2 KiB apart there), else 4 KiB. Masks, not
  divisions: a first version with `%` by the period cost 2^6-2^7 products 5-19% (Zen 5, Emerald
  Rapids; 64-bit divisions).
- intrinsics' `inverse_radix2_part` (the radix-2 top of every intrinsics variant, the fused ops'
  at even lg): with the output 1-256 bytes past the input modulo 4 KiB (`backwards`, common.hpp)
  the columns run last to first, in a cold function of their own (`inverse_radix2_backwards`, no
  software prefetch). Neon's generated level is unchanged.
- Tests: `test_placement.cpp` (new).

## The slowdown (Zen 3, `lc-bench`, `top.cpp`)

A, B, a source and work laid out as series' Arena (320 bytes apart modulo 4 KiB), out in a
mapping of its own at work's offset plus d modulo 64 KiB. `inverse_product` of one product, all
words out, us, median of 3 (`top1_lc-bench.txt`), at 2^20:

| 64 | 2254 | 2122 | 2055 | 2055 |

| 768-3072 | 1996-2000 | 2118-2124 | 2030-2040 | 2054-2063 |

| 4160, 8256, 16448, 32832 (64 mod 4 KiB) | 2205-2216 | 2121-2127 | 2040-2048 | 2054-2056 |

The same shape at 2^14, 2^16 and 2^22: the radix-8 top within 64 bytes past work modulo 4 KiB
1.07-1.15x its best, 128-512 bytes past or 64-320 before 1.03-1.05x, 640 or more past and 512 or
more before its best (`top9` placed the edges: 576 past 1.00-1.02x, 512 and 576 before at its
best); the radix-2 top flat
within 2-3% at every d, 1.0-1.03x slower than the radix-8 top's best (2^14 1.03, 2^16 1.02, 2^20
1.02, 2^22 1.02) and level with lc-opt (in place in its output). The upper half out, and
`cyclic_product`, the same with smaller ratios (cyclic at 2^20-2^22: the radix-2 top 1.016 of the
radix-8's best). Mod 4 KiB, not 64 KiB: d = 4 KiB + 64 behaves as 64.

2^18 is different: with out and work in one 2 MiB page (out right after work, as #143's
`terms.cpp` laid them out) the radix-8 top took 1155 / 587 / 523 us at d = 64 / 320 / 2048 against
484 / 481 / 470 for the radix-2 top (`top3b`); in separate mappings its time moved between runs

in three runs, 571 in a fourth (526-630 at its other offsets), while the radix-2 top stayed at
470-484. Hence radix 2 at 2^18 whatever d. (#236's 1152 us was this same-page layout.)

Other targets run the intrinsics' radix-2 top at even lg already (`top1_<vm>.txt`): Zen 5 flat;
Neoverse V2 flat at even lg (odd lg 2^19, all words out: 1.03-1.07x at d = 128-512, left for a
later round); Emerald Rapids 1.04-1.16x at d = 64-128 at 2^18-2^20, with `ld_blocks.address_alias`
247k per run at d = 64 and 128 against 108k at 2048 and 8k at -64 (`perf1_lc-intel.txt`): 4K
aliasing of the loads of the next columns with the stores just made, gone with the columns in
reverse (`top7_lc-intel.txt`: at d = 64, 2^18 all words 503 against 557 us, 2^20 2164 against
2359).

## Attempts

1. Radix-8 top in place in work, then a copy of the output: flat but 1.06x the radix-8 top's best
   at 2^20 (2118-2132 us). Dropped.
2. Radix-2 top always from 2^14: flat; 1-3% slower than the radix-8 top where its placement is
   good (above). Kept where the placement is not.
3. The run-time choice (`split_top`): first symmetric, the radix-8 top 768 (then 640) bytes or
   more from work either way and lg != 18 (`top5a`: the op ran at the better of the two at every
   d and lg, 2^14-2^22; at 2^16 one run had both at 150 us for d = 64, the physical pages again).
   That sent composition's lg-22 inverses (out 640 bytes before work: `compose/21` 1.015) and
   bench_fused's `inverse_product/20` (out 608 before: 1.015) to the radix-2 top where the radix-8
   top was at its best; `top9` then showed the band before work is narrower than the one past
   it. Final: 640 past, 448 before (composition and bench_fused level again).
4. `inverse(F, out)` (work in this thread's region, placed by `place`) with the radix-2 top:
   1.02-1.05x slower than the radix-8 top at 2^14-2^22 once place() keeps 1-2 KiB (`inv1`). Not
   changed.
5. avx2's radix-2 level (`scale_radix2_part`) in reverse too, for the radix-2 top with out 64-128
   bytes past work: 0.965 of the in-order time at 2^16 and 2^20 (`top8`), but the code it adds
   moved Zen 3's middle_product/6 (`mp6.cpp`, one binary each: 132 ns without the check, 146 with
   the loop inline, 136-141 with it in a cold function; the bench saw 1.29x) by layout alone.
   Dropped for avx2; kept for the intrinsics (Emerald Rapids' 1.04-1.16x).
6. place(): `prod.cpp` sweeps a's page offset x with f, g, out at 16 (glibc's large vectors), Zen
   3, us: x = 320 (the old rule's choice) against 1024-3072: 2^14 48.6 / 46.1, 2^16 222 / 214,
   2^18 1016 / 978, 2^20 4462 / 4300, 2^22 19594 / 18998 (`prod1`, `prod2`); within 64 bytes
   1.2-1.35x. At 2^12 x = 0 and x = 2048 both 1.05x (blocks 2 KiB apart): hence the 2 KiB period
   at 2^11-2^12. Flat at 2^6-2^10. Emerald Rapids and Zen 5 flat within noise; Neoverse V2 x = 320
   1.01-1.017x at 2^17-2^20. b farther from a than span bytes (b 2 KiB more): no gain (`prod2`).
