## What changed

- `kMaxTerms` 4 -> 8 (`common.hpp`; `ntt::kMaxTerms` in ntt.hpp for callers).
- `intrinsics::for_products(terms, n, sum)`: lists of 2 to 8 products (every term with b) call
  `sum.template operator()<K>()`; other lists (terms alone among them) keep the run-time loop,
  now `inverse_mixed`.
- `Bottom<L>::inverse_sum<K, C, Product>` and `WideBottom<S>::inverse_sum<K, C>`: the K products
  with K a template argument, in passes of up to C terms over a run's groups (`sum_pass`). One
  pass keeps each output vector's sum in registers (lc-opt's structure: group g + 1's rows filled
  before group g's products, each product reduced, then added); more passes leave partial sums
  (< 2P) in the run's output, in L1, and read 2C leaf forms at a time instead of 2K.
- avx2: 1 and 2 products keep the generated `bottom_leaves_*`, `bottom_leaves2_*`; 3 to 8 run
  `Bottom<Avx2>::inverse_sum<K, 3>` with `AsmLeaf`, lc-opt's leaf product (one asm statement per
  leaf in a fixed order). `bottom_leaves3_*` (and `SumBottom<3>`) left the generator: 3500
  generated lines; every other kernel's text is unchanged (the diff of the file only deletes).
- zmm (`WideBottom`, avx512 and zen5): one pass while K 2^lg <= 7 2^15 words (Emerald Rapids) or
  3 2^15 (Zen 5), else passes of one term. `Bottom`'s C for the intrinsics variants: 3 on 8
  lanes, 2 on Sse and scalar, 1 on Neon.
- neon: its generated passes (`inverse_sums`, one product per pass, #151 r3) took any number of
  products already; only the bound moved.

## Attempts (`terms.cpp`, ns per word of the transform, medians of 5-9 rounds, same binary)

1. Run-time term loop (main's `Bottom::inverse_terms`) against K as a template argument with the
   intrinsics' leaf product, Zen 3: 0.93-0.97 at 2^12 (4-8 products), 0.87-0.97 at 2^16-2^20.
   Against lc-opt still 1.01-1.08 out of L2: faster per term in L2 (0.80 against 0.88 ns per word
   per term at 2^12), slower out of it (1.07 against 0.94 at 2^20).
2. GCC's code of that leaf product (`sum4.s`): sums spilled to the stack, -P^-1 and 2P reloaded
   from it, the add chains reassociated. lc-opt's fixed-order asm leaf product in the same bottom
   (`AsmLeaf`): 0.87-0.97 of the intrinsics' time at 2^12-2^20. For 3 products against the
   generated `bottom_leaves3_*` (two groups per statement): 0.997 at 2^12, 0.90-0.92 at
   2^14-2^20. For 2 products `bottom_leaves2_*` stays 0-2.5% faster in L2, equal out of it: kept.
3. Software prefetch of the next groups' leaf forms (4 and 12 groups ahead), Zen 3: 1.00-1.18x
   the time at 2^16 and 2^20. Dropped.
4. Passes. Zen 3, passes of 3 terms: 0.77-0.94 of one pass's time at 2^14-2^16 for 4 to 8
   products, 0.99-1.02 at 2^12, 2^13, 2^18, 2^20 (passes of 2 and of 4 between). Emerald Rapids
   and Zen 5 (zmm): one pass is best while the leaf forms fit L2 (passes cost 1.00-1.10x there),
   passes out of it (one pass took up to 1.2x on Emerald Rapids, 1.5x on Zen 5: Zen 5 at 2^14, 8
   products, 5.25 against 3.57 ns per word); passes of one term beat passes of 2 by 2-9% on Zen 5
   at 2^17-2^20 (`terms11_lc-zen5.txt`), equal on Emerald Rapids. Sse: one pass of 4 to 8 products
   spilled, 1.4-1.7x the time of passes of 2 (`terms12_sse.txt`, Zen 3). Neon: the generated
   passes beat every intrinsics form (one pass, passes of 1-3, the run-time loop) by 8-24%
   (`terms10_lc-arm.txt`); among those, passes of one term were best.
5. No pipeline (rows filled right before their products; zmm): 1.07-1.40x one pipelined pass.
