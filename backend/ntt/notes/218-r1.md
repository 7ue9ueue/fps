## What changed (avx2 only)

- `toeplitz_product`: `ToeplitzLeaf` (avx2.hpp) in place of the generated `bottom_toeplitz_*`:
  lc-opt's structure, intrinsics around `AsmLeaf` (its fixed-order asm leaf product). Group g + 1's
  rows of S = B0 + B1, D1 = A1 - A2, D3 = A3 - A2 are filled before group g's products; per leaf
  m1 = S A2, f = low(m1 + D1 B1) to the inverse butterflies, A3 = canonical((m1 + D3 B0) 2^32 mod P)
  by a Shoup product. D1, D3 as t = x - y, min(t, t + P) (3 ops, not 4); 2^32 mod P passes through
  an empty asm statement, else GCC multiplies by the constant with 3 `vpslld` and 2 adds.
- Rows not reduced: in a row [w A, A] of a canonical leaf A, w A is Shoup's product as it comes,
  below (1 + A / 2^32) P < 1.24 P (with q = floor(w 2^32 / P), A w - floor(A q / 2^32) P < P + A ε / 2^32,
  ε = w 2^32 mod P < P). A leaf product's 64-bit sums of 8 products are then below 8 * 1.24 P^2 + 2^32 P
  < 1.42 10^19 < 2^64 and its Montgomery reduction below 3.3 P < 4P, so AsmLeaf's single
  min(x, x - 2P) still leaves it below 2P. `AsmLeaf::kRawRows` makes `Bottom::sum_pass` fill its
  rows with `fill_rows<true>` (two ops less per row), and `ToeplitzLeaf` fills its own the same way.
  Other bottoms and targets are unchanged (`fill_rows<false>`, the default).
- Sums of 2 products: `inverse_sum<2, 3>` with `AsmLeaf`, as 3 to 8 already were; `SumBottom` and
  the generated `bottom_leaves2_*` are gone. One product keeps `bottom_leaves_*`. The generator lost
  6308 lines of output (the toeplitz and two-product kernels; no other kernel's text changed).
- `bench_fused.cpp`: `toeplitz_product/<lg>` (new, no base). `test_lazy_rows.cpp` (new).

## Found

- An A3 kept times 2^-32 (lc-opt's form) would take `toeplitz_product` to 0.97-0.98 of lc-opt on
  Zen 3 (6 above; 3% of the op, about 0.3% of log at 2^21). The cost is the API: A3 is no longer a
  leaf form, so log's block 3 needs a term alone that already carries 2^-32 (avx2: added after the
  reduction, as lc-opt's `SideProductBottom`, or into the high words of the 64-bit sums before it;
  intrinsics' `inverse_mixed`: no multiply by 2^-32; scalar: times 2^32; neon's generated plain
  passes: a fallback or a kernel). For a later round.
- Rows not reduced hold on every target whose leaf products sum 8 products in 64 bits with a
  Montgomery reduction: intrinsics' `fill_rows` and `forward_leaves`, `WideBottom::fill_rows`, neon's
  generated row fills, and the generated avx2 bottoms' first stages (`first()` in
  gen_avx2_product_kernels.py reduces w A and, for t = 1, 3, takes P - w A; with (P - y, ~q) as the
  factor, as the intrinsics do, both go). Two ops per row; avx2's sums gained 2-5% from it.
- The intrinsics' `inverse_toeplitz` (neon, sse, narrow avx512) fills each group's rows right before
  its products; lc-opt's pipelining (next group's rows first) won 3-5% on avx2.
- Log's block 3 (`inverse_product` of a product and A3 alone, generated `bottom_side_*`) runs at
  0.98-1.02 of lc-opt's `SideProductBottom` (`side*`, `lc-side*` in `final_lcopt_lc-bench.txt`).
