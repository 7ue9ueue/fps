# #157 r1: the values form's split and join on 16 lanes (AVX-512)

2026-10-16, claude (issue #157, round 1: backend/gcd; this is its ntt part). Base: main c32fd82,
GCC 15.2.

On AVX-512 the values form's split and join ran on `Values<Avx512Vl>` (ymm, 8 lanes: `Values` works
on 1, 4 or 8 lanes, one block of 8 leaves per step). Emerald Rapids (`bench_leaves.cpp`, avx512
target): forward 0.46 ns per word at 2^10, forward_values 1.12; at 2^14 0.72 against 1.31; the
inverse alike. In backend/gcd's inverse_mod they were a third of the time (perf, 2^14: split 20%,
join 13.5% + 2.7% from the avx2 variant's `Values<Avx2>`).

`intrinsics::WideValues<S>` (S of 16 lanes): two blocks per step, lane 8h + t of a vector = leaf t
of block g + h (g even), so each 256-bit half is `Values`' 8 lanes: the rows of 8 leaves come in by
`load_halves` (one leaf of each block per zmm), the 8 x 8 transposes run in both halves at once
(unpack 32 and 64 per 128-bit lane, then `vpermt2q` for the 128-bit lanes of each half), the lane
table rows of both blocks by `load_halves` (A(g / 8) is the same for g and g + 1), the twist and
the 8-point transform are `Values`' code on zmm, the values go out by `store_half`. A lone first
or last block (odd `first` or `count`, and 2^6: one block) runs on `Values<Narrow>`. Same words as
`Values`. `ValuesFor<S>` picks it for 16-lane structs (intrinsics<Avx512>, the avx512 variant's
Zmm, and through `ValuesFor<Native>` `forward_upper(h, Form::values)` and the avx2 variant on
AVX-512 targets); elsewhere it is `Values<Narrow<S>>` as before.
