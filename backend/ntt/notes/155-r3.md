## The idea

A middle product out[i] = (v g)[m - 1 + i], i < count, reads N = m - 1 + count words of v. Its plain
plan is a cyclic product of length 2L >= N: those words of v b mod (x^(2L) - 1), b = g turned by
x^-(m - 1), read only v[0, N) (no term wraps). Round 2's truncated product cannot run it as it is:
its inverse needs the product's top words to be known (zero), and here they are garbage.

Two ways round that:

- Tellegen (round 1's form for the split plans): the middle product is the transpose of u -> u G
  (G = g reversed), so the transpose of the truncated product of N words costs what it does. Per
  full node the transpose of a group product (multiplication by G mod x^s - w) is J M_G J (J: the
  node's words reversed; the bilinear form [x^(s-1)] a b is associative with Gram matrix J), so
  every node would need its words reversed on the way in and out (two passes; a guess from round
  2's pass costs, 0.1-0.3 ns per word each: 4-12% of the time at 2^20), or transposed group
  products in every variant; plus the transposed levels. Not tried.
- Free words (taken): for any p that agrees with v on [0, N), out[i] = (p g mod (x^(2L) - 1))[m -
  1 + i] (those words read only p[0, N)). Take p = v on [0, rr) (rr: N rounded up to round 2's
  nodes, zero from N on) and choose its words [rr, 2L) so that p = 0 mod every node past the cut
  (the children past the partial one at each level: 2L - rr words of conditions, as many as free
  words; p = v + x^rr h with h = -v x^-rr mod their product, so it exists and is unique). Then q =
  p g mod (x^(2L) - 1) is 0 mod those nodes too, so q follows from its residues at the cut's full
  nodes alone (CRT): no transposes, no reversal, and g in its natural order.

So a truncated middle product is `co_forward` (p's residues at the full nodes, from v), round 2's
`forward` of g, the variants' group products unchanged, and `pruned_inverse` (q's words from the
full nodes' residues, zero past them) writing words [m - 1, N) to out (truncated.hpp, Middle
products).

`co_forward`, per level (a partial node, chunks p_u of c words, its first n words known, t = n / c
full children, a partial child with rest = n mod c known words, children past it zero), column j,
P(X) = sum_u p_u[j] X^u with R_v = P(lambda_v): step 1 (top-down, j < rest) solves for the free p_u,
u > t, from P = 0 at lambda_v, v > t, and gives R_v for v <= t (R_t: the partial child's known word
j); step 2 (bottom-up, after the partial child, j >= rest) solves for p_u, u >= t, from those zeros
and R_t, the child's free word j, and gives R_v for v < t. With rest = 0 the level runs step 1 with
t - 1 on every column. Closed forms per t (radix 4, lambda = y, -y, z, -z, x = y^2 = -z^2): e.g. t =
0, step 1: P = p0 (X + y)(X^2 + x) / (x y), so R0 = 4 p0 and the free words are p0 / y, p0 / x, p0 /
(x y); t = 2, step 2: G = R2 / 2, H = G / z, then R0, R1 = 2 p0 - G +- y (2 p1 - H). Free words are
written in place of the zero children's and of the partial child's own free words, where the
parent's step 2 reads them, so the pass touches each word once, as the forward does. True scale.

`pruned_inverse`: bottom-up, each node's words by the variants' unnormalized inverse from its
children's (zero past the partial child: radix-4 butterflies with 1 to 4 inputs). A node's words
come out times its leaves 2^-32, the group products' scale for its size, so no level rescales (round
2's inverse needed a factor mu per level for its known words; here there are none). The root writes
words [m - 1, N) to out times 1 / its scale: runs of columns with the same chunks inside (16 masks
at a radix-4 root), the vectors across an edge word by word; out at any offset.

Plan: kind `kTruncated` for middle products too, chosen by a second table in "ntt.ragged"
(`ragged::Kinds` is now two tables: products and the others, as before, then middle products,
`choose_middle`); `measure_ragged` times every kind's middle product (f of N words, g of N / 2: the
bench's shape) at each bucket's middle and first length besides the products (below: Bucket 1). A
split plan's top middle product uses `choose_middle` too. `choose` (backend/series' `ragged_into`)
is unchanged.

Sources, for the ideas (no code): J. van der Hoeven, "The truncated Fourier transform and
applications", ISSAC 2004; D. Harvey, D. S. Roche, "An in-place truncated Fourier transform and
applications to polynomial multiplication", ISSAC 2010; G. Hanrot, M. Quercia, P. Zimmermann, "The
middle product algorithm I", AAECC 2004 (the middle product as a transpose); A. Bostan, G. Lecerf,
E. Schost, "Tellegen's principle into practice", ISSAC 2003. The free-word extension follows from
the CRT as above; it is not taken from these.
