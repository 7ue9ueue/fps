## The ops (ntt.hpp)

- Doubling for Kronecker layouts (lc-opt's `Doubling`, `lib/poly/projection.hpp`). A block of b
  words of a leaf form is group j's subtree (b / 8 a power of 4) or, under the second stage of group
  j / 2 (a radix-2 level with r(j)), the subtrees of groups 2j, 2j + 1: f mod (x^b - r(j)^2), the
  same in every length.
  - `truncate_blocks(F, block, copy, hook)`: per block the inverse levels within it, the lower half
    of the coefficients (times b / 8, canonical) to copy, the upper half zero (aligned) or a copy of
    the lower (a radix-2 level with a zero upper input), the forward levels; the hook on runs of a
    tile (blocks below a tile go in runs, the top group of an aligned block on half its outputs
    and inputs).
  - `join_blocks(copy, block, scale)`: the inverse levels above the blocks, pruned (unused bit
    block / 2), in place, times scale (the top level's scale with (b / L) folded in).
  - `forward_upper_hooked(H, hook, zero)`: `forward_upper` with pruned groups and a hook (leaf
    indices of the whole form). Under a radix-4 top its first level (radix 2 with r(1)) skips and
    zeroes the masked columns; under a radix-2 top (group 1's subtree from H in place) the masked
    columns are zeroed first below 512 words, where a whole group at the bit reads them. So H's
    masked words are not used from zero = 16 on.
- `forward_hooked`: the runs come in increasing order (documented; intrinsics' radix-2 top ran the
  upper subtree first): a hook may write F below its run (power projection's V and W in place).
  With 32 <= zero < length / 4 the source's masked words are not used (the pruned top level writes
  zeros there; intrinsics no longer take the unpruned from_top path then; scalar masks its copy):
  composition's zero fills of those columns went.
- Pruned groups: the group whose quarter index holds the column bit (h = width: quarters 1, 3; 2h =
  width: quarters 2, 3) reads half its inputs (forward) or writes half its outputs (inverse), lc-opt's
  `forward_half`, `inverse_half` (avx2.hpp intrinsics loops; intrinsics.hpp `forward_group_half`,
  `inverse_group_half`), from quarters of 32 vectors (forward) or 8 (inverse) as the column loops.
  Width 1 (bit 8 words, composition's level T - 4): avx2 `kernels::forward_even_columns`,
  `inverse_even_columns` (gen_avx2_kernels.py: forward()'s and inverse()'s scheduled body with the
  second butterfly 64 bytes on; only additions to avx2_kernels.hpp), intrinsics chunks of one vector
  (`each`, one column at a time).

## Tests (`test_doubling.cpp`)

The scalar variant against the definitions (f mod (x^b - r(j)^2) from the coefficients, lg 8 ..
11, every block); every variant and the tuned one against scalar (lg 8 .. 20, 17 under ASan;
scalar to 14): truncate_blocks (leaves, copy, hook: each leaf once, in increasing runs, changes
kept), then join_blocks giving the rows cut to x < b / 2; join_blocks with a random scale against
the definition; forward_upper_hooked with random masked words (from 16) for every bit; the halves
of a transform of 2L of a layout from the three ops against forward() of 2L; forward_hooked with
random masked words (32 <= zero < length / 4) and its runs in order with and without a bit; the
argument checks. test_hooked.cpp (round 1) covers the half groups and width-1 columns (every bit).
composition's test_doubling.cpp checks the ops in use (a broken row-0 fix there fails 21 checks).

## Measurements (`lc-bench`, Zen 3, GCC 15.2, -O2 -march=x86-64-v3)

One generic level's transforms in isolation, m = 2^17, fps against lc-opt's `forward_pruned`,
`inverse_pruned` (same memory, `issue149r2/micro.cpp`), us, before the half groups / after:

| s (L) | fwd Q 4m | inv V 2m | fwd P 2m |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 (16384) | 534 / 503 lc-opt | 234 / 233 | 267 / 245 |
| 8 (512) | 478 / 443 | 198 / 214 | 252 / 224 |
| 13 (16, width 1) | 489 / 487 | 268 -> 203 / 209 | 331 -> 275 / 263 |

In compose at 2^17 (`issue149r2/phases.cpp`, the real call sequence): the half groups took the
generic levels' forwards and inverses 31.69 -> 30.98 ms of compose (power projection 30.40 ->
29.64), the width-1 columns the level T - 4's inverse of V 268 -> 203 us and forward of P 330 ->
275 us.

Doubling at m = 2^17 (per array, summed over levels 3 .. 13, us): truncate_blocks 3193 (P, no
hook), join_blocks 933, forward_upper_hooked 2672: 6797 against lc-opt's `Doubling` 6620; with
the projection hook (Q) 6493 + 916 + 6046 = 13455 against 12867. compose's Q (Graeffe hook):
4854 + 921 + 4195 = 9970 against the plain path's 11.3 ms (forward of 4m with the hook and the
inverse of 2m).
