# #103 r1: forward_upper in the values form (for backend/gcd)

2026-10-12, claude (issue #103, round 1, target all). One addition for `backend/gcd`, which keeps
its matrices' transforms in the values form and doubles them as lc-opt doubles leaf forms.

- `Transform::forward_upper(h, Form form)`: `Form::leaves` is `forward_upper(h)`; `Form::values`
  runs `forward_upper(h)` (the variant the forward's table picks at L / 2), then the split of
  those leaves (blocks L / 128 .. L / 64 - 1 of 64 words) by `intrinsics::Values<N>::split` of
  `simd::Native`, whose words are every variant's (the scalar model's). Throws
  `std::length_error` past 2^kValuesMaxLog, as `forward(f, Form::values)`.
- Split as a pass after the transform, not fused into its subtrees as `forward_values` does: at
  gcd's lengths (up to 2^19 for n = 2^20) the half is in L2 when the split runs. Not measured
  apart; gcd's numbers are in `backend/gcd/notes/103-r1.md`.
- Test: `test_upper_values.cpp`, every variant this machine runs and the tuned one, lengths
  2^7 .. 2^20 (2^16 under ASan), random words, all P - 1 and zeros, against the upper half of the
  scalar `forward(f, Form::values)`; `Form::leaves` against `forward_upper(h)`; the throw past
  2^23.
