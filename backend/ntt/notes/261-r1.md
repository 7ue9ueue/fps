## What went in

- `intrinsics.hpp`: `Source` with a tail (`first_group_tail`): a factor of half + e words, e <= half /
  16, half >= 512, is shared under a radix-2 top too. Each subtree's first group (x^half -+ 1) stages
  its first E = e rounded up to a step of columns (quarter 0 as f + tail or f - tail + P, < 2P;
  quarters 1-3 copied) and runs them in place, then runs the other columns from f. `multiply` and
  `square` from spans (`detail::Product`), so every product with such a factor, not only the ragged
  plans. At 2^8 (first groups of 32 columns) the staging lost: Neoverse V2 1.10 against 1.04 of the
  reference, Zen 5 1.11 against 1.07 (`tails-1`); from 2^10 it won (Neoverse V2 1.02 against 1.045,
  Zen 5 0.99 against 1.04): hence half >= 512. At e = half / 16 it is about even with the radix-2
  pass (Emerald Rapids 2^20 + L/16 1.023 against 1.028).
- `ragged.hpp`, `ntt.hpp`: the top plan's middle product (s = 0) reads v itself (`middle_into`: no
  Abar; `middle_words` without it), and `stage_turned` writes each word once for m <= L.
- `ntt.hpp`: `placed()` took place()'s offset (from a base aligned to its period) and added it to an
  unaligned work pointer, so a fell anywhere modulo 4 KiB. The top plan's middle product at 2^14 +
  2^9 put a 64 bytes past f and out (128 before this round's layout): Zen 3 1.05x slower (`recheck1`,
  `align1`); now at the target (14+L/32: 0.873 of the base, 1.019 of 2^14's per word). Every ragged
  plan uses it (split, truncated, middle); their other cases moved within noise (below). The main
  product of a split middle product places b far from g (the staging copies g), as the plain one.
- The middle table: `tools/tune.py ntt` on every target (at 1d7f799, `tune1/`); only its moves to
  the top plan are taken (70 entries: scalar 13, sse 11, avx2 12, avx512 12, zen5 17, arm 5; mostly
  truncated -> top in buckets 1-3, and plain -> top at 2^7 on Zen 3 and Zen 5). Its other moves (2-12
  per target) are near-ties and stay main's, as does the products' table: the products' bucket 1 was
  the top plan on every row from 2^12 already, and the tails change no other bucket's plan (the
  bucket's middle, 3L/32, has e = 3L/64 > half / 16). That run moved 1-27 product entries per target
  (Emerald Rapids 27: 2^16 bucket 1 to the truncated plan, 3% faster in one sample there, 15% slower
  in `parts-2`): noise.
- Tests: `test_bucket1.cpp` (new): cyclic products and squares of length 2^8-2^16 whose factors have
  half + e words for e from 1 past the steps to half / 16 + 1, half / 8 + 5, half / 4 + 1 and half / 2,
  against the exact product folded, every variant; products from spans with such a factor; the top
  plan's products, squares and middle products at L + r for r = 1, 2, 3, 15-17, L/128, L/32 + 1, L/16 -
  1, L/16 at 2^8-2^16 (g of 1, 2, r, r + 1, half, L - 1, L, L + 1, N - 1 words) against schoolbook or
  the plain plan; `stage_turned` against its definition. `mutate.py` (ASan/UBSan, the Mac): 16 of 16
  mutations caught (tail sign, rounding, staged quarters, the last columns, the threshold past a
  quarter, the upper sources, stage_turned's three cases, Tbar, v's offset).

## Tried and dropped, and why the issue's truncated ideas were not built

- The truncated plan in bucket 1 (the issue's three ideas): after this round the top plan runs bucket
  1 at 0.98-1.03 per word of 2^k's for r <= L/128, where the truncated one ran 1.04-1.31. Fusing the
  chain of partial levels saves under 1% (its levels below the root cost that little, above); taking
  the partial node whole one level earlier adds group products for less chain; fusing the root's
  level into the first group products is what the tail sources do inside the variants' own product
  (children x^(L/2) -+ 1 of a radix-4 root of 2L are f +- its tail), after which the truncated plan
  is the top plan plus its x^L + 1 chain: at best a tie at 2^20 + 1 and 2^22 + 1, so below the
  issue's 2% bar. At L/16 a fully fused truncated product would cost about P(L) (1 + 0.06 + the
  folds' 0.03-0.05) against the top plan's P(L) (1 + 0.1): 1-3%, for group products from caller
  memory in every variant; not attempted.
- Placement of the middle top plan's main product relative to (v, out, out) or (v, g, out): within
  1.5% of each other (`parts-2`); (v, g, out) kept, the plain middle product's rule.
- Middle products' harness numbers against the plain plan at L (`parts-2`: Zen 5 0.91-0.94 at 2^20-
  2^21) came from the harness's own work memory (a std::vector, 4 KiB pages); the bench's region
  gives 1.00-1.01.

## Found

- `placed()` (fixed here) had put every ragged plan's arrays at an offset place() did not choose
  since #155 r1; split and truncated plans moved within noise with the fix (other ragged cases 0.999-
  1.008 geomean), but the bench's spans all sit 64 bytes past a page: other layouts may have paid.
- The products' table's tuning on Emerald Rapids moved 27 of 304 entries by noise in one run (#225's
  near-ties).
- Products at r in (L/32, L/16] still pay the top plan's product of 2r words (1.01-1.08); a twisted
  piece with a root of unity of order 2L / s instead of z = 3 would skip the twist and untwist passes
  (one product per word each) but keep the folds of f, g and A (a product per word read), about what
  the top product costs there.
