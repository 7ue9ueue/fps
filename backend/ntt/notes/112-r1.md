## The ops (ntt.hpp)

- `forward(in, F)`: from a span (any alignment, zero past in.size() <= L); `forward_product(in, B,
  F)`: F = T(in) B; `forward_upper(in, H)`: the upper half of the leaf form of length L of in
  (in.size() <= L/2). `cyclic_product(in, B, work, out, {offset, negate})`: out = coefficients
  [offset, offset + out.size()) of in b mod (x^L - 1), offset 0 or L/2, negated or not.
  `inverse_product(terms, work, out, o)`: an inverse of a sum of 1-4 leaf products (or a term
  alone). Existing ops unchanged.
- Shifted sources (#112 item 4) are not needed: x^(L/2) times a leaf form negates its upper half,
  so (x^(L/2) a) b mod (x^L - 1) is a b rotated by L/2, and a Newton step's second product (e at
  x^k) is e g with the lower half out. The variants take output flags (common.hpp: kUpperHalf,
  kNegated): the upper half by the top level computing the differences only (generated
  `inverse_top_upper_to<T>`, `inverse_top4_upper_to<T>`; intrinsics `kUpper` parts), the sign
  through the top level's scale (intrinsics: no folded scale then).
- Variants: scalar (step by step, the model); intrinsics<S> (`forward_tree_from`, `cycle_tree`,
  `inverse_tree_with`: subtrees whose bottom stage is a callable; `Bottom::multiply_stored` and
  `inverse_terms`, `WideBottom::` the same on zmm); avx2 (`cycle_trees`, `inverse_trees_with` on
  the asm levels, and generated bottoms `bottom_stored_*` (b as leaves: forward butterflies of a,
  products, inverse butterflies), `bottom_leaves_*`, `bottom_leaves2_*`, `bottom_leaves3_*`
  (windows from a's leaves; sums of 2, 3 products, each reduced then added); terms alone and 4
  terms run the intrinsics' bottom on Avx2). Leaf products in the bottoms carry 2^-32, undone by
  product_scale at the top. Dispatch: forward's tuned choice for the forwards, the product row's
  for cyclic_product, the inverse's for inverse_product.
- Generator: every existing kernel's text unchanged (checked by diffing the generated file with
  the new sections cut out). The 'both' kernels of sums deadlock the scheduler from window 8 (2
  terms) and 6 (3 terms); windows 6 and 4 with shift -0.05 do not.

## Attempts (Zen 3, `lc-bench`, GCC 15.2, -O2 -march=x86-64-v3; `compare_ops.cpp`: fps / lc-opt, same run)

1. Leaf products as a tile hook between the asm bottom levels (forward bottom, exact products in
   L1, inverse bottom): cyclic 1.07-1.10 of lc-opt's, inverse_product 1.11-1.15. The products in
   the hook cost 0.97 ns per word, lc-opt's fused bottom 0.72.
2. Forward from a span with product()'s radix-8 top (`forward_radix8_from`): 1.48 of in place at
   2^18 (16 streams 2^15 words apart; a guess: L2 sets, Zen 3's L2 is 8-way). A radix-2 top level
   (lc-opt's choice): 1.04, within 1% elsewhere. Kept (avx2 `from_top`).
3. The intrinsics' bottoms on Avx2 inside the asm levels (`Bottom::multiply_stored`): cyclic 2%
   faster than 1., inverse_product unchanged.
4. Generated asm bottoms with b as leaves, negation and half outputs at the top level instead of
   per leaf: cyclic 0.95-0.98 of lc-opt's, inverse_product 0.95-1.00 (lg 12, 15, 18, 21). Kept.
5. Sums of 2 and 3 products: the intrinsics' bottom 1.10-1.11 of lc-opt's inverse_product_sum;
   generated sums 0.98-1.01 (2) and 1.04 (3) at 2^15, 2^20. Kept.
6. zmm: the fused bottoms on ymm (`Bottom<Avx512Vl>`) under zmm levels made Zen 5's series 3%
   slower than main (geomean); `WideBottom::multiply_stored`, `inverse_terms`: 0.88. Kept.

Remaining against lc-opt (Zen 3): forward from a span 1.04-1.09 (the top level 0.03-0.04 ns per
word more in `probe_top.cpp`, where fps's array is not in huge pages: not conclusive; the code of
`forward_radix2_part<1>` and `forward_radix4_part<2>` is minimal), forward_product 1.01-1.02.
