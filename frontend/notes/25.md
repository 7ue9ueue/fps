# #25: algebra: gcd, inverse_mod, roots, factor

2026-10-12. `include/fps/algebra.hpp`: `gcd(f, g)`, `inverse_mod(f, m)`, `roots(f)`, `factor(f)`,
all plain, as the coordinator asked ("keep frontend parts small"). The prototype's `algebra.hpp`
called its private copy of lc-opt's half-gcd (`lcopt/gcd.hpp`); that stays out, and the algorithms
here run on `f % g`, `divmod` (#21) and `f * g`.

## What it does

- `gcd`: Euclid on `f % g`, inputs trimmed, the last nonzero remainder made monic. gcd(0, 0) is
  the empty polynomial. Random inputs of degree n take n steps, each a quotient of 2 coefficients
  (division's recurrence path) and a product of 2 by n (`small::multiply`): O(n^2).
- `inverse_mod`: extended Euclid on `divmod`, carrying only f's cofactor s (r = s f mod m). deg m
  coefficients; `std::nullopt` when the last nonzero remainder is not a constant. m = 0 throws; a
  constant m returns the zero polynomial (every residue is 0 in the zero ring).
- `roots`: f monic, f = x^z u gives the root 0; r = gcd(u, x^(P-1) - 1) is the product of x - a
  over the nonzero roots; r splits by Cantor-Zassenhaus with d = 1. Sorted. f = 0 throws.
- `factor`: Yun's square-free decomposition, then per part s the distinct-degree split
  gcd(s, x^(P^d) - x) for d up to deg s / 2, then Cantor-Zassenhaus per degree:
  b^((P^d - 1)/2) = (b b^P ... b^(P^(d-1)))^((P-1)/2) mod f. Yun needs deg f < P (else f' = 0
  for f = g(x^P)); f = 0 and deg f >= P throw. Sorted by degree, then coefficients from the
  constant term; a constant f gives the empty list.
- Powers modulo m: square and multiply on `square(a) % m` and `(r * a) % m`. Each `%` runs a fresh
  Newton inverse of the reversed modulus; reusing it is the obvious first speed-up (not done:
  "no extra fast paths"; the backend issues below replace all of this).
- Random b: a xorshift with a fixed seed per call, so calls are deterministic and thread-safe.

## Tests (`frontend/test_algebra.cpp`)

- gcd: every pair of sizes {0, 1, 2^k - 1, 2^k, 2^k + 1, k <= 7} with the four kinds of
  test_division (random, mostly zero, all P - 1, at most 20 terms), a third with a common factor,
  some with trailing zeros, both argument orders, against Euclid on long division; long pairs
  (a c, b c) with deg c = 300 and quotients of degree 1 and of 5000 by 40 (division's Newton path);
  a Fibonacci-like chain (300 quotients x); special values.
- inverse_mod: every size d above with f of 0, 1, d - 1, d, d + 1, 2d + 3 coefficients, kinds,
  trailing zeros on m; existence against naive gcd = 1, then f h = 1 mod m and size deg m; shared
  factors; f = 3 m; a long pair (5000 mod 3000); constants.
- roots: random f of every size and kind (some times x (x - a)(x - b)) against
  gcd(f, x^P - x) by naive powers; built from known roots with multiplicities 1-3, repeats and 0,
  times irreducible quadratics (x - a)^2 - z for a non-square z; x^4 - 1 (the four 4th roots of
  unity); 2000 random roots with repeats; constants.
- factor: 24 products of known irreducibles (linear, quadratic, random of degree 3-8 by rejection
  with Rabin's test, five quartics at once for the equal-degree split, x itself), multiplicities
  1-4, a random lead, against the exact sorted list; random f of degree 1-33 (some times f^2 (1 + x),
  trailing zeros): monic, trimmed, irreducible (Rabin), product = monic f, sorted, distinct;
  x^4 + 1 into its four linear factors; a long case of degree 174 with multiplicities.
- 4 threads, 5 reps each, on all three; every error: m = 0 (empty and of size 3), f = 0 (empty and
  of size 4) for roots and factor, deg f = P for factor (a polynomial of P + 1 unwritten words, the
  top one set: degree() reads one word).
- 14 mutations: 13 fail the test (two by timeout: a cube in place of a square in pow_mod, Yun's
  c not divided); the 14th (stripping x^(z-1) in place of x^z in roots) is not a bug, since
  x^(P-1) - 1 does not vanish at 0.
- Pass at -O2 and ASan/UBSan: Mac (scalar, arm), `lc-amd` (scalar, sse, avx2), `lc-intel-test`
  (avx512), `lc-zen5-test` (zen5), `lc-arm` (arm).

## Bench (`frontend/bench_algebra.cpp`)

No `@kernel` pairs: no backend call is wrapped. Cases: `gcd/<lg>` and `inverse_mod/<lg>` on two
random polynomials of degree 2^lg (coprime: 2^lg Euclid steps); `roots/<lg>` of a random
polynomial of degree 2^lg (about one root: x^(P-1) mod f and one gcd) and `roots_split/<lg>` of
2^lg distinct linear factors; `factor/<lg>` of a random polynomial of degree 2^lg. Median of 7
rounds, GCC 15.2, origin/main 6856d40 plus this change; ms per call:

| case | `lc-bench` (avx2) | `lc-intel` (avx512) | `lc-zen5` (zen5) | `lc-arm` (arm) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| gcd/6, /8, /10, /12 | 0.021, 0.230, 3.24, 49.6 | 0.017, 0.199, 2.70, 41.5 | 0.015, 0.173, 2.45, 37.6 | 0.021, 0.240, 3.45, 53.2 |
| inverse_mod/6, /8, /10, /12 | 0.027, 0.267, 3.66, 53.9 | 0.020, 0.220, 3.19, 46.8 | 0.018, 0.191, 2.66, 39.5 | 0.026, 0.283, 3.98, 59.9 |
| roots/6, /8, /10 | 0.136, 0.477, 4.13 | 0.106, 0.384, 3.45 | 0.112, 0.366, 2.98 | 0.136, 0.596, 5.00 |
| roots_split/6, /8, /10 | 1.03, 5.28, 27.3 | 0.775, 4.11, 21.7 | 0.745, 3.97, 20.0 | 1.04, 5.68, 31.4 |
| factor/4, /6, /8 | 0.111, 1.46, 43.9 | 0.087, 1.31, 35.9 | 0.078, 1.13, 33.6 | 0.119, 1.44, 56.4 |

- gcd is O(n^2): 2.96 ns per n^2 at 2^12 on Zen 3 (2.2-3.2 across the four), about 12 us per
  Euclid step at 2^12, the step a quotient of 2, a product of 2 by n and the allocations of q and r.
  lc-opt's half-gcd (#103): 115 ns per degree at 1023 on `lc-amd`, so 0.12 ms against 3.24 here
  (27x), and the gap grows linearly.
- roots_split is gcd-bound: the splitting tree runs a gcd per node, sum of d^2 over nodes. lc-opt's
  tangent Graeffe finds 4000 roots in 0-2 ms on `lc-bench`; here 1024 take 27 ms.
- factor is the distinct-degree loop: deg / 2 P-th powers, each 30 squarings with a fresh
  inverse. lc-opt's Berlekamp factors degree 100 in about 1 ms per judged run (its I/O included);
  here degree 64 takes 1.46 ms and degree 256 44 ms on Zen 3.
