# #27: points: geometric points, extrapolate, dft, idft

2026-10-12, claude. `fps::geometric(a, r, m)` (a `Geometric`), `fps::evaluate(f, geometric(...))`,
`fps::interpolate<P>(geometric(...), values)`, `fps::extrapolate<P>(values, c, m)`,
`fps::dft(f, L)` and `fps::idft<P>(values)` in a new header, `include/fps/geometric.hpp`
(included from `fps/fps.hpp`; `points.hpp` untouched). Each is one call of the backend's chirp
kernels (#106 r1, PR #133): `chirp::evaluate_geometric`, `chirp::interpolate_geometric`,
`chirp::extrapolate`; idft is `evaluate_geometric` at w^-1, then `chirp::multiply_chirp` by
1 / L. The prototype's `points.hpp` had the same shapes (Geometric, dft by evaluate at a root,
idft at the inverse root and a scale); its kernels stay there.

- dft order: natural, `dft(f, L)[i] = f(w^i)`, w = 3^((P - 1) / L) (3 generates the units mod
  P), any `f.size()` (so the DFT of f mod (x^L - 1)), L = 0 or any divisor of P - 1 = 2^23 7 17
  (not only powers of two). The prototype threw on L = 0; here it gives no values, as `evaluate`
  at no points does.
- w and w^-1 come from a compile-time table over the 96 divisors of P - 1; 1 / L is
  P - (P - 1) / L (L (P - 1) / L = -1). With `mod_pow` at run time (three of them at first) dft
  and idft at L = 2^10 ran 2-5% over their kernel cases.
- Errors: a, r, c, values >= P throw `std::domain_error`; the backend's `std::invalid_argument`
  for points that repeat becomes `std::domain_error` (DESIGN.md's convention), caught around the
  call (the sizes are right, so the only invalid_argument left is the repeat); interpolate's m >
  2^25 throws `std::length_error` before any work (the backend would first fill two scratch
  arrays); extrapolate's n > P throws `std::domain_error` before the values are read. Evaluate and
  extrapolate leave length checks to the backend (`ntt::middle_product`), since their small paths
  have no limit.
- The canonical check (`detail::geometric::check_canonical`): a scalar `any_of`, then a scalar
  max, ran 2 cycles per word on Neoverse V2 (cmp + csel chain): 3.3-6.3% of extrapolate and idft
  on `lc-arm` in the first run. Now 4 chains of `simd::Native` min of v - P (v - P wraps to at
  least 2^32 - P exactly for v < P): about 1% on every machine. `points.hpp`'s
  `check_canonical` is the scalar form; small next to its tree's 200+ ns per point.
- `Geometric` has an explicit constructor so that `interpolate<P>({1, 2, 3}, values)` cannot
  silently mean the points 1 2^i (an aggregate would take the braced list).
- DESIGN.md: a paragraph on geometric points and extrapolate (sizes, preconditions, errors), and
  dft / idft's order, root, lengths and cost under Values.
- Backend issue #138 (opened): a natural-order DFT of power-of-two length on the NTT. dft/18 is
  8.3x `to_values/18` on `lc-bench`, 9.5x `lc-intel`, 8.2x `lc-zen5`, 9.0x `lc-arm`.

Tests (`frontend/test_geometric.cpp`; 0.9 s at -O2 on the Mac, 1.4 s at -O2 and 3.7 s
under ASan/UBSan on `lc-amd` scalar): evaluate on all 900 pairs of edge
sizes (0, 1, 2^k +- 1, k <= 10) with a, r from random, 0, 1, P - 1 and a 7th root of unity,
against Horner at each point; long f by few points and the reverse; interpolate on every edge size
at random, 2048th-root and order-1904 r, checked by Horner at the points (the f of degree < m
through m points is unique), roots of unity of order k at m = k (pass) and k + 1, 2k + 5 (throw),
a = 0, r = 0, r = 1 errors, m = 1 with a = 0, m = 2 with r = 0 and r = P - 1; 2^14, 2^14 + 1 and
100003 both ways; extrapolate on edge n by m in {0, 1, 2, 17, 64, 257, 300} (n > 300: no 64, 257) at 8 c (0, 1, n / 2,
n, random, P - 1, P - n / 2, P - m / 2: samples, wrap-around), against Horner on the polynomial
that made the samples; 2^14 and 20001 at random points; dft at 17 lengths (powers of two, 7, 14,
17, 34, 119, 238, 1904) for f of 0, 1, L - 1, L, L + 1, 2L + 3 coefficients against the sum,
idft back to f mod (x^L - 1), dft(idft(y)) = y; the root table at every divisor; L = 4 by hand;
2^17 and 119 2^10; 4 threads; every error, a word >= P (P, P + 1, 2^32 - 1) at every position of
1 to 100 words, n > P and 2^25 + 1 points on unwritten mmap memory, the middle product past 2^25.
Mutations (11, on the first version of the header): 10 fail it; `values.size() < m` for `!= m` passes, since the backend then throws
its size error, which also becomes `std::domain_error` (only the message differs). Pass at -O2
and ASan/UBSan on the Mac (scalar, arm), `lc-amd` (scalar, sse, avx2), `lc-intel-test`,
`lc-zen5-test` and `lc-arm`.
