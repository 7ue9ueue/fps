# #23: fraction: Fraction

2026-10-12: `Fraction` (#23): `fps::Fraction<P>` in `include/fps/fraction.hpp`, from the
prototype's `fraction.hpp`: the two constructors, `[k]` for any k < 2^64, `take(n)`,
`numerator()`, `denominator()`, `a + b`, `a - b`, `-a`, `c * a`, `a * c`, `a * b`, `f * a`,
`a * f`, `a / f`, `a / b` and `derivative(a)`; prefix sums and shifts are `a / (1 - x)` and
`a * Poly::x(k)`. All on `f * g` and the series functions; no kernel of its own.

- `[k]`: Bostan and Mori, per bit of k two `f * g` (p(x) q(-x), q(x) q(-x)) and every other
  coefficient copied out; p and q cut to k + 1 coefficients first and after each step.
  Backend issue #114 (searched first: no open issue, and lc-opt has no Bostan-Mori).
- `take(n)` = `mul(p, inverse(q, n), n)`: `inverse` already runs the recurrence for q with at
  most 16 terms past q[0], so no separate small-d path (the coordinator asked for the simple
  form). DESIGN.md's cost row said "O(n d) by recurrence for small d"; now it says what runs.
- `a / b` checks b's numerator [0] itself (`std::domain_error`, before the two products); the
  constructor's check would throw too.
- `a + b`, `a - b`, `derivative(a)`: the second product is a named local freed before the third
  is allocated. Written as one expression (`{a.p * b.q + b.p * a.q, a.q * b.q}`) the temporary
  lived until the end and `fraction_add/18` ran 1.07-1.12x its kernel case on x86 (4.47 against
  4.01 ms on Zen 3); as now, 1.000. Guess: the third result reuses the freed block's pages
  instead of faulting in new ones.
- DESIGN.md: `a * c` and `a * f` listed (the operators exist for symmetry with `Polynomial`);
  the errors of `a / f` and `a / b`; the product counts per operation; the measured `[k]` cost
  replaces the guess (about 100 ms at d = 10^5, k = 10^18: 44 to 167 ms by machine).
- Tests (`test_fraction.cpp`): `take(n)` and `[k]` against the recurrence q[0] s_i = p_i -
  sum q_j s_(i-j) for every pair of sizes of p and q in 0, 1, 2^j and neighbours up to 65 (every
  k for sizes <= 17) and q up to 1025, random, mostly zero, all P - 1 and few terms (both paths
  of `inverse`); huge k (2^20 - 1 to 2^64 - 1) against Kitamasa's x^k mod the reversed q
  (O(d^2 log k), q up to 65 coefficients); d = 1, 2, 100, 1000, 2048 at huge k against partial
  fractions (q with distinct roots); d = 2^15 at k = 200000 against `take`; closed forms
  (Fibonacci by fast doubling, 5^k, k + 1, period 3); every operation against its termwise
  definition on 11 shapes and 3 kinds, scalars 0, 1, P - 1, -1, -3P - 5, 2^40, 2^64 - 1, and
  the results' huge coefficients against Kitamasa; DESIGN.md's examples (also F * F at 10^18
  by (K L_K - F_K) / 5); 4 threads; every error. 13 mutations: 10 fail it; the other three
  (no own check in `a / b`, `inverse(q, n + 1)` in take, `q * q` for `square(q)`) are no bugs.
  Pass at -O2 and ASan/UBSan on the Mac (scalar, arm), `lc-amd` (scalar, sse, avx2),
  `lc-intel-test`, `lc-zen5-test` and `lc-arm`.
- Bench (`frontend/bench_fraction.cpp`, its own binary; sizes stop at 2^18 to keep the gate
  short): `fraction_mul/<lg>` and `fraction_add/<lg>` (numerators and denominators of
