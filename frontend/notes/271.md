## The problem

`Products` (include/fps/polynomial.hpp) chose `small::multiply` or the NTT by
`small::beats_ntt(n, m)`: small's cost model against `ntt::multiply`'s time at n + m - 1 words
(backend #212). Two callers run another NTT op (backend `small/notes/212-r1.md`, Found):
- `middle`: `ntt::middle_product` runs a cyclic product of length >= n, not n + m - 1, while
  small computes all n + m - 1 words of f g.
- `square`: `ntt::square` runs one forward transform fewer than a product.

## What changed

- `Products::small_middle(n, m)` = `model_ns(kModel, n, m) < ntt_ns(n)`; `small_square(n)` =
  `model_ns(kModel, n, n) < 0.85 ntt_ns(2 n - 1)` (`kSquareShare`). Both read only what
  backend/small exposes (`model_ns`, `kModel`, `ntt_ns`, which follows the tuned "small.ntt").
- f * f: a and b the same words (`f * f`, `mul(f, f, n)`, `raw::mul(a, a, out)`) run as a square:
  `ntt::square` on the NTT path. `Products::square(a, n)` checks the one factor for a single term
  (5cd4bc2; through `multiply(a, a, n)` it checked both, a few ns at 2^6-2^7 on Neoverse V2).
- Head comments of polynomial.hpp and products.hpp: #212's cutoffs (the old ones were main's
  before #212), and the square and middle choices. DESIGN.md: the two lines on square and
  middle_product.
- Tests: `frontend/test_products_choice.cpp` (new): both sides of every switch of `small_square`
  (n < 1024) and `small_middle` (in n for 12 values of m, in m for 4 values of n), found at run time;
  every size to 160 and past powers of two for squares, written as `square(f)`, `f * f`, `g *= g`,
  `mul(f, f, k)` and `raw::mul(a, a, out)` with k around the square's size; spans of the same
  words with another length or start stay products; the NTT's length errors. Two mutations caught
  (the square path running a product with one word fewer, the middle's words moved from m, not
  m - 1). `test_products.cpp`: its spare-word check of the middle product's small path asks
  `small_middle`, not `beats_ntt`.
- `frontend/bench_products.cpp` (new): `square/<n>x<n>` and `middle_product/<n>x<m>` (gated) at

## Found

- backend/small models neither `ntt::square` nor `ntt::middle_product`: the frontend scales
  `ntt_ns` by one constant for squares (0.85) and takes the product's time at n for middles. The
  measured ratios differ per target (squares about 0.80 on Emerald Rapids, 0.88-0.90 on Zen 5 at
  their crossovers; middles 0.80-1.26 of the product at n), so Zen 5 runs squares of n = 79-90 on
  the NTT 1.06-1.21x slower than small (bench above). Would try: square and middle-product rows in
  small's tuned costs (as "small.ntt", measured by tune.cpp per target), `beats_ntt_square(n)` and
  `beats_ntt_middle(n, m)` in small.hpp; the frontend then calls them.
- small has no middle-product kernel: `small::multiply` writes all n + m - 1 words, (count + m - 1)
  m terms where count m suffice (count = n - m + 1). At n = m one output costs m^2 terms; at n = 2 m,
  2x the terms. A transposed schoolbook (count x m, the same panels) would move the middle
  crossover up and win outright for count << m (guess: 40x40 at Zen 3, small 198 ns and the NTT
  173, would take about 10 ns).
- small has no square kernel: a^2 needs n (n + 1) / 2 terms by symmetry, not n^2; squares would
  stay on small to larger n (guess: n up to about 1.3x the product's crossover).
- Zen 3 `square@ntt/32x32` moved 1.10-1.11 and the API's square at 32x32 1.23-1.25 between two
  binaries that differ only in polynomial.hpp; with 64-byte function and loop alignment 0.89 and
  0.99. The 2^6 NTT's layout lottery (#93) reaches the frontend's small squares.
